# Faithful Kids — Competitive Strategy (researched Aug 16, 2026)

Source: deep research on Minno, Superbook (CBN), Bible App for Kids (YouVersion),
Yippee TV, Hallow, Glorify, Abide, TruPlay. This file steers weekly growth work.

## Positioning (the one-sentence war plan)
**"Duolingo for the Bible."** Every competitor is passive video or toddler content.
Nobody gamifies Bible *learning*, nobody serves ages 9-15, nobody gives parents
proof of learning. We do all three. Never compete on library size or price —
compete on learning outcomes. Secondary frame: "the next step after Bible App
for Kids" (141M installs of a toddler app with no graduation path).

## The gaps, ranked (what winners do that we don't)

1. **YouTube firehose** — Minno: ~17.5M views/mo; Superbook: ~14M views/mo. Both
   post full free episodes and funnel to their product. We own ~2,010 finished
   videos and post zero. Single biggest gap. (Needs founder: channel access.)
2. **Seasonal challenge engine** — Hallow's Advent (Pray25) / Lent (Pray40)
   challenges drive 300% subscription lifts annually; drip-unlocked daily
   episodes + email + campaign. NO kids' Advent challenge exists in the market.
   Advent 2026 starts Nov 29. "25 Days of Christmas Bible Adventure."
3. **Free tier** — every winner is freemium ("the most popular content is free"
   — Hallow). We are hard-paywalled against free giants. Candidate: first
   episode of every series free + free daily trivia; premium = quizzes, levels,
   dashboard, full library. (Founder decision.)
4. **Church/homeschool channel** — Superbook Academy (1,000+ churches), Minno
   (free memberships for ministry staff), Hallow parishes (assets provided,
   discounts). Solo version: free-for-ministry-leaders tier + printable Sunday
   school companion + outreach to children's ministers + homeschool co-ops.
   Note: doctrinal review/statement of faith is a prerequisite for this channel.
5. **Bedtime audio wedge** — Hallow's most viral content; Abide's whole business
   (podcast on Spotify as permanent free funnel). We can extract audio from
   existing videos at zero cost; free bedtime section/podcast, library paywalled.
6. **Pricing** — REPRICED Aug 18, 2026: Monthly $8.88 (no trial), Annual
   $77.77 ($6.48/mo, 3-day trial). Now cheaper than Minno monthly ($10.99)
   and near Hallow/Minno annual ($69.99). Comparison pages updated; watch
   conversion + trial-to-paid in PostHog. Family-plan tier still an option.
7. **Trust signals** — Statement of Faith page, "doctrinally reviewed by"
   credit, pastor endorsements. Cheap, and unblocks the church channel. The AI
   Jesus avatar needs this cover most of all.
8. **Micro-influencer co-creation** — Hallow's 0→100K engine: don't pay for
   shoutouts; invite 10K-100K Christian mom/homeschool influencers to co-create
   content in the product ("Sarah's 7-day family challenge") they promote as theirs.
9. **Distribution surface** — Android (Capacitor, cheap; Christian family demo
   skews Android/Fire), later CTV. Minno's Prime Video Channels cited as a
   growth driver.
10. **Anchor artifacts** — YouVersion devotional plan (borrowed 1B-install
    audience, one-time content lift), printable/KDP devotional workbook as
    gift + Amazon discovery, gift subscriptions (grandparents buy this category).

## What we already do that nobody else does (protect these)
Active learning loop (quiz/XP/streaks/series-unlock) · ages 7-15 · parent
dashboard with real progress · direct Scripture curriculum (not IP
entertainment) · conversion-optimized quiz funnel · AI content velocity
(new series in days, ~$0 marginal cost).

## SEO implications (Growth Engine: act on these)
- Comparison/alternative keywords are underowned: "bible app for kids for older
  kids", "superbook alternatives", "minno alternative", "best bible apps for
  tweens", "bible curriculum with quizzes", "what age is bible app for kids for".
- Seasonal calendar is law: Advent/Christmas content live by October, Lent/Easter
  by February, VBS by April, back-to-school by July.
- "Safe alternative to toxic screens" (fear hook) demonstrably converts in this
  category — test alongside our education-forward framing.
- Bedtime/sleep queries are an acquisition wedge, not just a topic.

## Proven failures to avoid
Church-event flyers (zero conversions — Hallow), paying influencers for
shoutouts (co-create instead), competing on price alone, library-size claims.
