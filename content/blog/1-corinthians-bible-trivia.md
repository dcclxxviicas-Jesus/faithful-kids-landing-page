These 37 1 Corinthians Bible trivia questions cover the love chapter, the Lord's Supper, the body with many parts, and the great resurrection chapter -- with answers and verse references after every question. They're sorted into easy, medium, and hard rounds so ages 5 through adult can play the same game.

1 Corinthians is secretly one of the most quotable books in the Bible -- "love is patient," "in the twinkling of an eye," "bad company corrupts good character" -- so even kids who've never opened it will recognize half the answers. The hard round holds a genuine surprise: this famous letter wasn't actually Paul's first one to Corinth (question 30).

## What Is the Book of 1 Corinthians About?

1 Corinthians is the apostle Paul's letter to a talented but messy young church in Corinth, a wealthy Greek port city, answering their problems one by one: divisions, lawsuits, food offered to idols, chaotic worship, and confusion about the resurrection. Paul had planted the church himself, staying a year and a half (Acts 18:11), and wrote this letter a few years later from Ephesus (1 Corinthians 16:8), around AD 55.

The letter's heartbeat is chapter 13 -- the "love chapter" read at countless weddings, though Paul actually wrote it to squabbling church members -- and its summit is chapter 15, the Bible's fullest teaching on the resurrection. The big idea: a church with every spiritual gift still amounts to nothing without love (1 Corinthians 13:1-3).

## Easy 1 Corinthians Trivia Questions (Ages 5-8)

1. Who wrote 1 Corinthians? **The apostle Paul** (1 Corinthians 1:1)
2. Corinth was a big port city in which land? **Greece** (Acts 18:1)
3. Finish the famous verse: "Love is patient, love is ___" **Kind** (1 Corinthians 13:4)
4. Three things remain -- faith, hope, and love. Which is the greatest? **Love** (1 Corinthians 13:13)
5. Your body is a temple of whom? **The Holy Spirit** (1 Corinthians 6:19)
6. What special meal did Jesus begin that Paul explains, saying "do this in remembrance of me"? **The Lord's Supper** (1 Corinthians 11:23-25)
7. What happened to Jesus "on the third day according to the Scriptures"? **He was raised from the dead** (1 Corinthians 15:4)
8. Whatever you do, do it all for what? **The glory of God** (1 Corinthians 10:31)
9. The church is like one body with many ___. **Parts** (1 Corinthians 12:12)

## Why Was the Church in Corinth Such a Mess?

Because Corinth itself was -- and knowing the city unlocks the whole letter. Corinth was a rich, rebuilt Roman port sitting on a narrow land bridge between two seas, famous for its markets, its temples, and its anything-goes reputation. New believers walked into church carrying all of it: status games (chapter 1), lawsuits (chapter 6), meat from idol temples (chapters 8-10), and rich members feasting at the Lord's Supper while poor members went hungry (chapter 11).

That context turns chapter 13 from a greeting card into a thunderclap. "Love is patient, love is kind... it is not proud... it is not self-seeking" (1 Corinthians 13:4-5) is a point-by-point reversal of how the Corinthians were treating each other. A great dinner-table exercise for older kids: read 1 Corinthians 13:4-7 and replace the word "love" with your own name -- then talk about which line stings the most. Paul knew Corinth's games from his own eighteen months there; the story of how the church began is told in [Paul in Corinth](/blog/paul-in-corinth-for-kids).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who wrote 1 Corinthians?

The apostle Paul wrote 1 Corinthians, with a believer named Sosthenes named alongside him in the opening line (1 Corinthians 1:1). Paul had founded the church in Corinth on his second missionary journey (Acts 18) and wrote this letter from Ephesus around AD 55.

### How many chapters does 1 Corinthians have?

1 Corinthians has 16 chapters. A quick map: chapters 1-6 tackle the church's divisions and moral problems, 7-14 answer their written questions about marriage, food, worship, and spiritual gifts, and 15-16 cover the resurrection and a collection for Jerusalem.

### What is the love chapter in the Bible?

1 Corinthians 13, which begins "If I speak in the tongues of men or of angels, but do not have love, I am only a resounding gong." Though it's now read at weddings, Paul wrote it to a divided church to show that every gift and achievement is worthless without love -- and that "the greatest of these is love" (13:13).

### What is the main message of 1 Corinthians?

That the cross reshapes everything about how believers live together -- their unity, their bodies, their worship, and their future. Paul answers a dozen practical problems with one repeated principle: you were bought at a price, so build each other up in love (1 Corinthians 6:20; 8:1).

### Why is 1 Corinthians 15 so important?

It's the Bible's longest chapter on the resurrection, listing eyewitnesses of the risen Jesus -- Peter, the Twelve, more than 500 at once, James, and Paul himself (15:5-8) -- and arguing that if Christ was not raised, faith is useless (15:17). It ends with the promise that believers will be raised too, "in a flash, in the twinkling of an eye" (15:52).

### What age can kids start learning 1 Corinthians?

Ages 5-8 can memorize the love chapter's opening lines and the body-parts picture of chapter 12, which reads almost like a fable. Ages 9-12 can handle the whole letter's practical questions, and the resurrection logic of chapter 15 rewards teens.
