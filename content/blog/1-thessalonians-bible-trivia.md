These 25 First Thessalonians Bible trivia questions cover the trumpet call of God, the dead in Christ rising first, the three shortest commands in the Bible, and the mob that accused Paul of turning the world upside down -- sorted into easy, medium, and hard rounds by age. Every answer is bolded right after its question with a verse reference, so you can check any answer straight from the text.

First Thessalonians is likely the earliest letter of Paul's that we have -- written around AD 50, possibly before any of the Gospels were put on paper. That alone is great trivia. Add the most detailed description of Christ's return in Paul's letters, and this short book earns a full game night of its own.

## What Is the Book of 1 Thessalonians About?

First Thessalonians is Paul's letter of encouragement to a brand-new church, and its signature theme is the return of Christ -- every one of its five chapters ends by pointing to Jesus' coming. It opens from "Paul, Silas and Timothy" (1 Thessalonians 1:1) and was written around AD 50, most likely from Corinth shortly after Timothy brought back good news about the young church (1 Thessalonians 3:6; Acts 18:1, 5). That early date makes it probably the first of Paul's letters -- among the oldest documents in the New Testament.

The backstory is dramatic: Paul preached in Thessalonica for only about three Sabbaths before a mob ran him out of town (Acts 17:1-10), so this letter is a spiritual care package for believers he had to leave as newborns in the faith. It answers their most urgent question -- what happens to Christians who die before Jesus returns? -- with the famous promise that "the dead in Christ will rise first" (1 Thessalonians 4:16). The book matters because it shows what hope looks like when it's brand new.

## Easy 1 Thessalonians Trivia Questions (Ages 5-8)

Start here -- the three commands in question 2 may be the easiest memory verses in the whole Bible.

1. Which three men's names open this letter? **Paul, Silas, and Timothy** (1 Thessalonians 1:1)
2. What three short commands come in a row in chapter 5? **Rejoice always, pray continually, give thanks in all circumstances** (1 Thessalonians 5:16-18)
3. What did the Thessalonians turn away from to serve the living and true God? **Idols** (1 Thessalonians 1:9)
4. Who will come down from heaven at the end? **The Lord Jesus himself** (1 Thessalonians 4:16)
5. What three sounds will announce his coming? **A loud command, the voice of the archangel, and the trumpet call of God** (1 Thessalonians 4:16)
6. Who will rise first when Jesus returns? **The dead in Christ** (1 Thessalonians 4:16)
7. What are believers told to do for one another? **Encourage one another and build each other up** (1 Thessalonians 5:11)
8. How gentle was Paul with this young church? **Like a nursing mother caring for her children** (1 Thessalonians 2:7)

## Medium 1 Thessalonians Trivia Questions (Ages 9-12)

This round adds the backstory from Acts 17 -- a church born in a riot and checked on by a worried missionary.

9. For how many Sabbath days did Paul reason in the synagogue at Thessalonica? **Three** (Acts 17:2)
10. Whose house did the angry mob attack while hunting for Paul and Silas? **Jason's** (Acts 17:5)
11. What did the mob famously accuse Paul's team of doing? **Causing trouble all over the world -- turning the world upside down** (Acts 17:6)
12. Whom did Paul send back to check on the Thessalonians? **Timothy** (1 Thessalonians 3:2)
13. From which city did Paul send him? **Athens** (1 Thessalonians 3:1-2)
14. What news did Timothy bring back? **Good news about their faith and love** (1 Thessalonians 3:6)
15. How will the day of the Lord come? **Like a thief in the night** (1 Thessalonians 5:2)
16. How should believers NOT grieve for those who have died? **Like the rest of mankind, who have no hope** (1 Thessalonians 4:13)
17. What three ambitions does Paul give in chapter 4? **Lead a quiet life, mind your own business, and work with your hands** (1 Thessalonians 4:11)
18. After the dead in Christ rise, what happens to believers who are still alive? **They will be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air** (1 Thessalonians 4:17)

## Hard 1 Thessalonians Trivia Questions (Teens and Adults)

The expert round -- watch for question 24, a pattern hiding in plain sight that most lifelong readers have never noticed.

19. Chapter 1 pairs faith, love, and hope with three actions -- what are they? **Work produced by faith, labor prompted by love, and endurance inspired by hope** (1 Thessalonians 1:3)
20. What two pieces of armor appear in chapter 5? **The breastplate of faith and love, and the hope of salvation as a helmet** (1 Thessalonians 5:8)
21. What are believers told not to quench? **The Spirit** (1 Thessalonians 5:19)
22. After "test them all," what two instructions follow? **Hold on to what is good, reject every kind of evil** (1 Thessalonians 5:21-22)
23. In which city had Silas and Timothy rejoined Paul when this letter was likely written? **Corinth** (Acts 18:1, 5)
24. What theme closes every single chapter of this book? **The coming (return) of the Lord Jesus** (1 Thessalonians 1:10; 2:19; 3:13; 4:15-17; 5:23)
25. What did Paul charge them to do with this letter? **Have it read to all the brothers and sisters** (1 Thessalonians 5:27)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who wrote 1 Thessalonians?

Paul wrote 1 Thessalonians, with Silas and Timothy named alongside him in the greeting (1 Thessalonians 1:1), and its authenticity is essentially undisputed. It was written around AD 50, most likely from Corinth, which makes it probably the earliest of Paul's letters that we have.

### How many chapters does 1 Thessalonians have?

First Thessalonians has 5 chapters. Chapters 1-3 look back at Paul's visit and Timothy's encouraging report; chapters 4-5 look forward -- how to live a holy life and what will happen when Jesus returns.

### What is the most famous passage in 1 Thessalonians?

The return of Christ in 1 Thessalonians 4:16-17 -- the loud command, the archangel's voice, the trumpet call of God, and the dead in Christ rising first. The rapid-fire commands of 5:16-18 ("Rejoice always, pray continually, give thanks in all circumstances") are a close second.

### What happened when Paul first visited Thessalonica?

Acts 17:1-10 tells it: Paul reasoned in the synagogue for three Sabbaths, some Jews and many Greeks believed, and then a jealous mob dragged his host Jason before the city officials, accusing the missionaries of defying Caesar. The believers smuggled Paul and Silas out of the city by night to Berea.

### Why did Paul write about Christians who had died?

Some Thessalonian believers had died soon after the church began, and the survivors worried those loved ones would miss out when Jesus returned. Paul assures them the dead in Christ will actually rise first, and tells the church to "encourage one another with these words" (1 Thessalonians 4:18).

### What age can kids start learning 1 Thessalonians?

Ages 5-8 can start with the three short commands of 5:16-18 and the trumpet-call picture of Jesus' return, which kids find genuinely exciting. The questions about grief, hope, and the day of the Lord fit ages 10 and up -- especially kids who have lost a grandparent or pet and are already asking.
