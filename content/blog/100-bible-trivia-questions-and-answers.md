## Easy Bible Trivia Questions (1-30)

Start here with young kids or mixed groups. These cover the most famous trivia about the Bible -- the stories nearly everyone has heard.

1. What is the first book of the Bible? **Genesis** (Genesis 1:1)
2. Who were the first man and woman? **Adam and Eve** (Genesis 2:7, 3:20)
3. What was the name of the garden where Adam and Eve lived? **The Garden of Eden** (Genesis 2:8)
4. Who built an ark to save his family and the animals from the flood? **Noah** (Genesis 6:13-14)
5. How many days and nights did it rain during the flood? **Forty days and forty nights** (Genesis 7:12)
6. What sign did God put in the sky as a promise never to flood the whole earth again? **A rainbow** (Genesis 9:13)
7. Which baby was hidden in a basket on the Nile River? **Moses** (Exodus 2:3)
8. What sea did God part so the Israelites could escape Egypt? **The Red Sea** (Exodus 14:21-22)
9. How many commandments did God give Moses? **Ten** (Exodus 34:28)
10. On what mountain did Moses receive the Ten Commandments? **Mount Sinai** (Exodus 19:20)
11. Which shepherd boy defeated the giant Goliath? **David** (1 Samuel 17:50)
12. What weapon did David use against Goliath? **A sling and a stone** (1 Samuel 17:50)
13. Which strong man's power was connected to his long hair? **Samson** (Judges 16:17)
14. Who spent a night in a den of lions and lived? **Daniel** (Daniel 6:16-23)
15. Who was swallowed by a huge fish? **Jonah** (Jonah 1:17)
16. In what town was Jesus born? **Bethlehem** (Luke 2:4-7)
17. Who was Jesus' mother? **Mary** (Luke 1:31)
18. Where was baby Jesus laid after he was born? **In a manger** (Luke 2:7)
19. What did the wise men follow to find Jesus? **A star** (Matthew 2:2)
20. In what town did Jesus grow up? **Nazareth** (Matthew 2:23)
21. How many disciples did Jesus choose? **Twelve** (Matthew 10:1-4)
22. Who baptized Jesus? **John the Baptist** (Matthew 3:13)
23. In what river was Jesus baptized? **The Jordan River** (Matthew 3:13)
24. What was Jesus' first miracle? **Turning water into wine at a wedding in Cana** (John 2:1-11)
25. What prayer did Jesus teach that begins "Our Father in heaven"? **The Lord's Prayer** (Matthew 6:9)
26. What did Jesus use to feed more than 5,000 people? **Five loaves of bread and two fish** (John 6:9-11)
27. What did Jesus walk on to reach his disciples' boat? **Water** (Matthew 14:25)
28. Which short tax collector climbed a sycamore-fig tree to see Jesus? **Zacchaeus** (Luke 19:2-4)
29. Which disciple betrayed Jesus? **Judas Iscariot** (Luke 22:47-48)
30. On what day did Jesus rise from the dead? **The third day** (Luke 24:46)

85. On the road to which city did Saul see the blinding light? **Damascus** (Acts 9:3)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are good Bible trivia questions for beginners?

Start with the stories everyone recognizes: Noah's ark, David and Goliath, Daniel in the lions' den, and the birth of Jesus. Questions 1-30 above are built for beginners -- the answers are famous enough that new players score points early, which keeps them in the game.

### What is the hardest Bible trivia question?

From this list, the clean animals question wins: Genesis 7:2 says Noah took seven pairs of every clean animal, not two of everything. Eutychus falling from the window (Acts 20:9) and Obadiah as the shortest Old Testament book stump nearly everyone else.

### How do I run a Bible trivia game for mixed ages?

Assign tiers by age: easy questions for players under 9, medium for ages 9-12, hard and expert for teens and adults. Everyone answers questions at their own level for the same point value, so a seven-year-old can beat grandpa fairly. Our [family Bible trivia night guide](/blog/family-bible-trivia-night) has full formats, scorekeeping, and prize ideas.

### Which Bible translation do these answers use?

The answers follow the wording of modern translations like the NIV, but every fact is translation-independent -- names, numbers, places, and events read the same in the KJV, ESV, NLT, and others. Each answer includes a verse reference so you can check it in your family's preferred Bible.

### How many questions should a Bible trivia game have?

Fifteen to twenty questions makes a game that lasts about half an hour, which is the sweet spot before younger kids fade. For a tournament or youth group night, run three rounds of ten with a break between rounds and save five expert questions as a tiebreaker.
