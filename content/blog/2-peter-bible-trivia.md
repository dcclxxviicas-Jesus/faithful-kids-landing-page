These 22 2 Peter Bible trivia questions cover the apostle's short, urgent final letter -- with answers and verse references included after every question. They're sorted into easy (ages 5-8), medium (ages 9-12), and hard (teens and adults) rounds, so the whole family can play together.

2 Peter is only three chapters long, but it's stuffed with trivia gold: a talking donkey, a ladder of virtues, a day that equals a thousand years, and the apostle Peter gently admitting that Paul's letters can be hard to understand. Most adults have never quizzed on this book, which makes it a great "stump the grown-ups" round.

## What Is the Book of 2 Peter About?

The book of 2 Peter is the apostle Peter's farewell letter, written to remind believers to keep growing in their faith, to watch out for false teachers, and to keep trusting that Jesus really is coming back. Peter says plainly that he knows his death is near -- "I will soon put it aside, as our Lord Jesus Christ has made clear to me" (2 Peter 1:14) -- so the whole letter reads like a leader's last words to people he loves.

The letter opens in the name of "Simon Peter, a servant and apostle of Jesus Christ" (2 Peter 1:1), the fisherman Jesus called by the Sea of Galilee. It's worth knowing that 2 Peter's authorship is the most debated of any New Testament book -- some scholars question whether Peter wrote it himself, while church tradition has long received it as his. Either way, the letter's message is clear and consistent: chapter 1 says grow, chapter 2 says beware of false teachers, and chapter 3 says don't give up waiting for the day of the Lord.

## What Did Peter Say About Jesus Coming Back?

Peter said that Jesus' return is certain but on God's timetable, not ours -- "with the Lord a day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years are like a day" (2 Peter 3:8). Scoffers in Peter's day were already sneering, "Where is this 'coming' he promised?" (2 Peter 3:4), and Peter's answer is one kids can actually grasp: God isn't slow, God is patient, "not wanting anyone to perish, but everyone to come to repentance" (2 Peter 3:9).

Peter also gives one of the Bible's clearest pictures of what we're waiting for: "a new heaven and a new earth, where righteousness dwells" (2 Peter 3:13). And because he was an eyewitness -- he saw Jesus shining in glory on the mountain with his own eyes (2 Peter 1:16-18) -- his confidence isn't secondhand. That eyewitness moment, the transfiguration, shows up in the medium round below, and it's a great story to read together afterward (Matthew 17:1-8).

## Easy 2 Peter Trivia Questions (Ages 5-8)

1. Who wrote 2 Peter, according to its very first verse? **Simon Peter, a servant and apostle of Jesus Christ** (2 Peter 1:1)

2. How many chapters does 2 Peter have? **Three** (2 Peter 1-3)

3. Peter says "this is now my second letter to you" -- what was his first? **1 Peter** (2 Peter 3:1)

4. What job did Peter have before Jesus called him? **He was a fisherman** (Matthew 4:18)

5. Which hero who built the ark does Peter call "a preacher of righteousness"? **Noah** (2 Peter 2:5)

6. What animal spoke "with a human voice" to stop the prophet Balaam? **A donkey** (2 Peter 2:16)

7. What does Peter tell believers to grow in, in the letter's last verse? **The grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ** (2 Peter 3:18)

## Medium 2 Peter Trivia Questions (Ages 9-12)

8. Peter builds a ladder of eight qualities -- what is the first rung everything else is added to? **Faith** (2 Peter 1:5)

9. What quality sits at the very top of that ladder? **Love** (2 Peter 1:5-7)

10. What amazing event did Peter see with his own eyes on "the sacred mountain"? **The transfiguration -- Jesus shining in majesty** (2 Peter 1:16-18)

11. What did the voice from heaven say on that mountain? **"This is my Son, whom I love; with him I am well pleased"** (2 Peter 1:17)

12. Who "carried along" the prophets as they spoke from God? **The Holy Spirit** (2 Peter 1:21)

13. With the Lord, one day is like how long? **A thousand years -- "and a thousand years are like a day"** (2 Peter 3:8)

14. Why hasn't the Lord returned yet, according to Peter? **He is patient, not wanting anyone to perish but everyone to come to repentance** (2 Peter 3:9)

15. How will the day of the Lord arrive? **Like a thief** (2 Peter 3:10)

## Hard 2 Peter Trivia Questions (Teens and Adults)

16. What does Peter compare his own body to when he says he will soon "put it aside"? **A tent** (2 Peter 1:13-14)

17. Which fellow apostle's letters does Peter say contain "some things that are hard to understand"? **Paul's** (2 Peter 3:15-16)

18. Which "righteous man" was rescued from Sodom, "distressed by the depraved conduct of the lawless"? **Lot** (2 Peter 2:7)

19. Peter quotes two animal proverbs about false teachers returning to their old ways -- name one. **"A dog returns to its vomit" or "a sow that is washed returns to her wallowing in the mud"** (2 Peter 2:22)

20. Through God's "very great promises," what does Peter say believers get to participate in? **The divine nature** (2 Peter 1:4)

21. What does Peter say will rise "in your hearts" when the day dawns? **The morning star** (2 Peter 1:19)

22. What are believers waiting for, "where righteousness dwells"? **A new heaven and a new earth** (2 Peter 3:13)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who wrote the book of 2 Peter?

The letter names its author as "Simon Peter, a servant and apostle of Jesus Christ" (2 Peter 1:1), the fisherman who became a leader of the early church. Its authorship is the most debated in the New Testament -- some scholars question whether Peter wrote it directly -- but church tradition has long received it as Peter's farewell letter, written shortly before his death in the 60s AD.

### How many chapters does 2 Peter have?

2 Peter has three chapters and 61 verses, making it one of the shortest books in the New Testament. Chapter 1 is about growing in faith, chapter 2 warns against false teachers, and chapter 3 is about the day of the Lord.

### What is the main message of 2 Peter?

The main message is: keep growing, stay alert, and don't stop waiting for Jesus. Peter urges believers to add goodness, knowledge, self-control, perseverance, godliness, mutual affection, and love to their faith (2 Peter 1:5-7), warns that false teachers will secretly bring in destructive ideas (2 Peter 2:1), and promises that the Lord's delay is patience, not forgetfulness (2 Peter 3:9).

### What is the most famous verse in 2 Peter?

Probably 2 Peter 3:8 -- "With the Lord a day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years are like a day." It's the Bible's classic answer to "why is God taking so long?", and 2 Peter 3:9 completes the thought: God is patient because he wants everyone to come to repentance.

### How is 2 Peter different from 1 Peter?

1 Peter comforts Christians suffering from the outside; 2 Peter warns about danger from the inside -- false teachers within the church. 1 Peter says "stand firm under pressure," while 2 Peter says "grow up in your faith and don't be fooled." Peter himself connects the two, calling this "my second letter to you" (2 Peter 3:1).

### What age can kids start learning 2 Peter?

Kids around 8 and up can follow 2 Peter well, especially the ladder of virtues in chapter 1 and the "day is like a thousand years" idea in chapter 3. Younger kids can enter through the stories Peter references -- Noah, Lot, and Balaam's talking donkey -- while the false-teacher warnings in chapter 2 land better with preteens and teens.
