These 35 2 Samuel Bible trivia questions cover King David's entire forty-year reign -- capturing Jerusalem, dancing before the ark, God's forever promise, the Bathsheba disaster, and Absalom's rebellion. Answers with verse references follow every question, sorted into easy, medium, and hard rounds by age.

2 Samuel is the rare Bible book that gives you its hero's whole story, failures included -- which makes for honest trivia and even better family conversations. The easy round celebrates David at his best; the hard round doesn't look away from his worst.

## What Is the Book of 2 Samuel About?

The book of 2 Samuel tells the story of David's reign as king of Israel -- roughly 1010 to 970 BC -- from his coronation after Saul's death, through his greatest triumphs, to the family tragedies that followed his sin with Bathsheba. Like 1 Samuel, the book is anonymous; 1 Chronicles 29:29 points to records kept by the prophets Nathan and Gad, and 1 and 2 Samuel were originally a single book in the Hebrew Bible.

The hinge of the whole book is God's covenant in chapter 7: David wants to build God a house, and God answers that he will build David a "house" instead -- a royal line and a kingdom that will endure forever (2 Samuel 7:16). That promise runs straight through the Old Testament to Jesus, "the son of David." It matters because it shows both what God can do through a person after his own heart, and how seriously sin still costs -- even for the man God loved.

## Easy 2 Samuel Trivia Questions (Ages 5-8)

1. Who became king after Saul died? **David** (2 Samuel 2:4; 5:3)
2. What city did David capture and make his capital, "the City of David"? **Jerusalem** (2 Samuel 5:6-9)
3. What special golden chest did David bring to Jerusalem with music and shouting? **The ark of God (the ark of the covenant)** (2 Samuel 6:12-15)
4. What did David do in the street as the ark came into the city? **He danced before the LORD with all his might** (2 Samuel 6:14)
5. Which prophet brought God's messages to King David? **Nathan** (2 Samuel 7:4; 12:1)
6. What did God promise David about his kingdom? **His house, kingdom, and throne would last forever** (2 Samuel 7:16)
7. To whose son did David show great kindness because of an old promise? **Mephibosheth, the son of his best friend Jonathan** (2 Samuel 9:6-7)
8. What was special about how Mephibosheth lived from then on? **He ate at the king's table like one of the king's own sons** (2 Samuel 9:11-13)
9. Which of David's sons was famous for his long, beautiful hair? **Absalom** (2 Samuel 14:25-26)
10. How many years did David reign as king in all? **Forty years** (2 Samuel 5:4)

## Medium 2 Samuel Trivia Questions (Ages 9-12)

11. Which son of Saul was set up as a rival king against David? **Ish-Bosheth** (2 Samuel 2:8-9)
12. How long did David rule from Hebron before moving to Jerusalem? **Seven and a half years** (2 Samuel 5:5)
13. What happened to Uzzah when he reached out and touched the ark? **He died -- God struck him down for his irreverent act** (2 Samuel 6:6-7)
14. Who watched David dancing from a window and despised him for it? **Michal, Saul's daughter** (2 Samuel 6:16)
15. How did David answer Michal's criticism? **"I will become even more undignified than this"** (2 Samuel 6:21-22)
16. Whose wife was Bathsheba when David first saw her? **Uriah the Hittite's** (2 Samuel 11:3)
17. How did David arrange Uriah's death? **He ordered Uriah placed at the fiercest front line, then had the troops pull back from him** (2 Samuel 11:15)
18. What story did Nathan tell to confront David? **A rich man with huge flocks who stole a poor man's one little ewe lamb** (2 Samuel 12:1-4)
19. What four words did Nathan say when David condemned the rich man? **"You are the man!"** (2 Samuel 12:7)
20. Which son born to David and Bathsheba later became king? **Solomon** (2 Samuel 12:24)
21. Which son rebelled against David and "stole the hearts" of Israel? **Absalom** (2 Samuel 15:1-6)
22. How did Absalom get stuck during the battle in the forest? **His head caught in the branches of a great oak while his mule kept going, leaving him hanging in midair** (2 Samuel 18:9)

## Hard 2 Samuel Trivia Questions (Teens and Adults)

23. Who killed Absalom as he hung in the tree, and with what? **Joab, who plunged three javelins into him** (2 Samuel 18:14)
24. What did David cry when he heard Absalom was dead? **"O my son Absalom! My son, my son Absalom! If only I had died instead of you"** (2 Samuel 18:33)
25. Who cursed David and pelted him with stones as he fled Jerusalem? **Shimei, from Saul's family** (2 Samuel 16:5-6)
26. Which of Absalom's advisers hanged himself when his advice was rejected? **Ahithophel** (2 Samuel 17:23)
27. How much did Absalom's hair weigh at his yearly haircut? **Two hundred shekels -- about five pounds** (2 Samuel 14:26)
28. What repeated line anchors David's lament for Saul and Jonathan? **"How the mighty have fallen"** (2 Samuel 1:19, 25, 27)
29. What did three of David's mighty men break through enemy lines to bring him -- and what did he do with it? **Water from the well near Bethlehem's gate; he refused to drink it and poured it out to the LORD** (2 Samuel 23:15-16)
30. What act of David's near the end of the book brought a plague on Israel? **He ordered a census -- counting his fighting men** (2 Samuel 24:1-15)
31. Whose threshing floor did David buy to build an altar and stop the plague? **Araunah the Jebusite's** (2 Samuel 24:18-25)
32. What did David say when Araunah offered him the threshing floor for free? **"I will not sacrifice to the LORD my God burnt offerings that cost me nothing"** (2 Samuel 24:24)
33. Who was the commander of David's army through most of the book? **Joab** (2 Samuel 8:16; 20:23)
34. Which brother did Absalom have killed, and why? **Amnon -- in revenge for what Amnon did to their sister Tamar** (2 Samuel 13:28-29, 32)
35. What happened to the man who claimed he had killed King Saul? **David had the Amalekite messenger executed for saying he destroyed the LORD's anointed** (2 Samuel 1:8-16)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who wrote the book of 2 Samuel?

The book of 2 Samuel is anonymous. 1 Chronicles 29:29 mentions the events of David's reign being written in the records of the prophets Nathan and Gad, which tradition connects to this book -- but Scripture never directly names its author.

### How many chapters does 2 Samuel have?

2 Samuel has 24 chapters. Chapters 1-10 cover David's rise and triumphs, chapters 11-20 cover his sin with Bathsheba and the troubles that followed, and chapters 21-24 collect final songs, lists of mighty men, and the census story.

### What is the most famous story in 2 Samuel?

David and Bathsheba (2 Samuel 11-12) is the book's most famous story, along with Nathan's unforgettable confrontation: "You are the man!" David's dance before the ark (chapter 6) and Absalom's rebellion (chapters 15-18) are close behind.

### What is the Davidic covenant in 2 Samuel?

The Davidic covenant is God's promise in 2 Samuel 7 that David's house, kingdom, and throne would be established forever (2 Samuel 7:16). The New Testament presents Jesus, "the son of David," as the ultimate fulfillment of that promise (Luke 1:32-33).

### What age can kids start learning 2 Samuel?

Ages 5-8 can enjoy David's coronation, the ark celebration, and the kindness shown to Mephibosheth -- that's what the easy round covers. The Bathsheba story, Amnon and Tamar, and Absalom's death involve adult themes, so most families save chapters 11-19 in full for ages 12 and up.

### Was David punished for his sin with Bathsheba?

Yes -- though God forgave David when he confessed ("The LORD has taken away your sin," 2 Samuel 12:13), the consequences still came: the baby died, and Nathan's prophecy that "the sword will never depart from your house" (2 Samuel 12:10) unfolded through Amnon, Absalom, and years of family grief. It's one of the Bible's clearest lessons that forgiveness is real and so are consequences.
