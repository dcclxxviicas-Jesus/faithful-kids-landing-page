Most "best Christian charities" lists rank by name recognition. This one checks every charity against **three independent sources**. **Charity Navigator** rates financial health and accountability from public tax filings. **ECFA** (the Evangelical Council for Financial Accountability) accredits ministries that meet its standards. **MinistryWatch** scores Christian ministries on donor confidence. Program-spending percentages come from each charity's IRS Form 990, or, where none is filed, from the audited financials on its ECFA profile. Everything was checked on November 16 and 17, 2026.

The headline finding: **the watchdogs sometimes disagree sharply**, and knowing why is the most useful thing on this page.

## Top Christian Charities: The Short Version

- **Top marks across the board:** World Relief (Charity Navigator 100%, MinistryWatch 100, ECFA member) and World Vision US (96%, MinistryWatch 89, ECFA member).
- **Clean water:** Living Water International (Charity Navigator 96%, ECFA member).
- **Fighting trafficking:** International Justice Mission (Charity Navigator 97%).
- **Volunteering with kids:** Feed My Starving Children, where families pack meals together (Charity Navigator 97%).
- **The big one with a split verdict:** Samaritan's Purse, rated 4 stars by Charity Navigator but "Withhold Giving" by MinistryWatch after it left ECFA in 2025. Details below.

## Child Sponsorship

**1. World Vision US.** Child sponsorship, community development and emergency relief. **Charity Navigator 96% (4 stars); MinistryWatch 89, transparency grade A; ECFA member since 1979.** It puts 85.8% of spending toward programs (Form 990, fiscal 2025). One thing worth knowing: World Vision lost about $170 million, roughly 10% of its budget, in the 2025 foreign-aid cuts, per its CEO in Christianity Today. [Sponsor a child](https://www.worldvision.org/sponsor-a-child)

**2. Compassion International.** Church-based child development through one-to-one sponsorship. **ECFA charter member since 1980**, putting 79.6% toward programs (ECFA profile, fiscal 2025). Charity Navigator shows it as Not Rated, because it reclassified as an association of churches and no longer files a public Form 990. MinistryWatch scores it 51 ("Exercise Caution"), largely for that reason. [Sponsor a child](https://www.compassion.com/sponsor-a-child)

## Relief and Development

**3. World Relief.** Refugee resettlement in the US plus overseas relief. **Charity Navigator 100% (4 stars), MinistryWatch 100**, one of only two ministries to score a perfect 100 in MinistryWatch's 2026 top 50, and an ECFA member since 1980. It puts 86.0% toward programs (fiscal 2025). [Donate](https://worldrelief.org/donate)

**4. Samaritan's Purse.** Disaster and medical relief, and the Operation Christmas Child shoeboxes, whose collection week is **November 16 to 23, 2026**. **Charity Navigator 94% (4 stars)**, putting 84.4% toward programs (fiscal 2024). But it **resigned from ECFA on October 1, 2025**: ECFA cited "a lack of agreement" with its new leader-care standard. MinistryWatch then moved it to **"Withhold Giving"** (score 36). Same charity, two very different verdicts: Charity Navigator measures the financial filings, while MinistryWatch weights accountability memberships and transparency. [samaritanspurse.org](https://www.samaritanspurse.org)

**5. Food for the Hungry.** Community development and child sponsorship. **Charity Navigator 88% (3 stars); MinistryWatch 80, grade A; ECFA member since 1980.** 75.2% toward programs. [fh.org](https://www.fh.org)

**6. Convoy of Hope.** Disaster response and food distribution. **ECFA member since 2001**, putting 91.4% toward programs (ECFA, fiscal 2024). Charity Navigator shows 97% but notes it can't score accountability on current filings, and MinistryWatch scores it 45, citing no public 990 since 2021. [convoyofhope.org](https://www.convoyofhope.org)

## How Christian Charity Ratings Work, and How to Vet One Yourself

1. **Check ECFA accreditation** at ecfa.org. Its standards require a board of at least five people, most of them independent, plus audited financial statements and transparency on request.
2. **Read the Charity Navigator rating, and its limits.** Charity Navigator gives 4 stars at 90 and above and 3 stars from 75 to 89, and it needs three years of public 990s to rate at all. Many ministries file as churches and can't be rated, so **"Not Rated" doesn't mean bad**.
3. **Ask whether it files a public Form 990.** MinistryWatch treats not filing as a transparency risk and scores accordingly.
4. **Compare program spending, but not alone.** Most charities here put 70 to 90% toward programs. A lower figure can reflect costly work like legal casework, not waste.
5. **Search for recent news** before a large gift.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best Christian charity to donate to?

Against all three watchdogs we checked in November 2026, World Relief (Charity Navigator 100%, MinistryWatch 100, ECFA member) and World Vision US (96%, MinistryWatch 89, ECFA member) score highest. For clean water, Living Water International; for justice, IJM; for hands-on family volunteering, Feed My Starving Children.

### Is Samaritan's Purse a good charity?

The watchdogs disagree. Charity Navigator rates it 94% (4 stars) from its financial filings, but Samaritan's Purse resigned from ECFA on October 1, 2025, and MinistryWatch then moved it to "Withhold Giving." Its financial ratios are solid. The disagreement is about accountability memberships, so read both and decide.

### Why is Compassion International "Not Rated" on Charity Navigator?

Compassion reclassified itself as an association of churches, which the IRS doesn't require to file a public Form 990, so Charity Navigator lacks the data to rate it. It remains an ECFA charter member, and its ECFA profile reports 79.6% of spending going to programs.

### What does ECFA accreditation mean?

ECFA, the Evangelical Council for Financial Accountability, accredits Christian ministries that meet its seven standards. These include an independent-majority board of at least five people, audited financial statements, and providing financials on request.

### Which Christian charities can kids help with?

Feed My Starving Children, where volunteers including children pack meals; Operation Christmas Child shoeboxes from Samaritan's Purse; and child sponsorship through World Vision or Compassion, where kids can write letters to a sponsored child.
