Most "best Christian colleges" rankings are a list of names with no numbers. This one is built from the numbers families actually need. Tuition comes from each school's own 2026-27 tuition page. Average net price, graduation rate and median earnings come from the US Department of Education's College Scorecard (accessed November 2026). And whether students must profess faith to be admitted is taken from each school's own admissions wording. We don't rank by prestige; we sort by type, so you can compare like with like.

Three terms, quickly. **Sticker tuition** is the published price. **Net price** is what the average aided student actually pays, which is usually far less. **CCCU** is the Council for Christian Colleges & Universities, the main association of evangelical schools.

## The Short Version

- **Highest graduation rates:** Notre Dame (95.3%), Georgetown (94.6%), Boston College (90.3%), Hillsdale (89.4%).
- **Lowest net price among the evangelical (CCCU) schools:** Calvin, about $21,100.
- **Lowest sticker tuition:** Grand Canyon ($16,500), then Grove City ($23,470) and Liberty ($25,390).
- **Strongest Christian community requirements:** Wheaton, Taylor, Biola and Cedarville all ask students to profess faith in Christ to be admitted.
- **No federal money at all:** Grove City, Hillsdale and Christendom.

## How to Choose

1. **Compare net price, never sticker.** Wheaton's sticker is about $49,000, but its average net price is about $26,000. Run each school's own net price calculator with your real numbers.
2. **Read the community covenant.** It tells you more about daily life than any brochure: chapel requirements, behaviour standards, and whether faith is required to enrol.
3. **Check graduation rate.** It's the best single signal of whether students thrive there.
4. **Ask about church-related scholarships.** Many denominations fund students at their own schools; see our [Christian scholarships](/blog/christian-scholarships) list.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best Christian colleges?

By graduation rate, Notre Dame (95.3%), Georgetown (94.6%) and Boston College (90.3%) lead the national universities. Among evangelical liberal arts colleges, Wheaton (83.4%) leads, with Calvin offering the lowest net price (about $21,100). Hillsdale (89.4%) and Grove City (82.1%) post strong graduation rates without federal aid. All figures are from College Scorecard, accessed November 2026.

### Which Christian colleges require students to be Christian?

From their own admissions wording: Wheaton, Taylor, Biola and Cedarville all ask applicants to profess faith in Christ. Baylor, Calvin, Azusa Pacific, Belmont, Grove City, Hillsdale and Messiah say students don't have to be Christians.

### What is the cheapest Christian college?

By sticker tuition for 2026-27: Grand Canyon ($16,500), Grove City ($23,470), Liberty ($25,390), Thomas Aquinas ($31,900), Christendom ($32,390) and Hillsdale ($33,710). By average net price among schools with federal data, Calvin ($21,122) and Grand Canyon ($21,509) are lowest.

### What Christian colleges have closed recently?

Since 2024: Eastern Nazarene, Clarks Summit, Notre Dame College (Ohio), Birmingham-Southern, Fontbonne, St. Andrews, Siena Heights, Trinity Christian, Lourdes and Providence Christian. Marian University in Wisconsin is being acquired by Alvernia University.

### Is Grand Canyon University a Christian school?

Yes, by its own description: "rooted in the Christian faith" and "Christ-centered." It's a private nonprofit and doesn't require students to be Christian, except in theology programs.
