## The Big Employers

**Hobby Lobby** (about 50,000 employees, 1,000+ stores): the first line of its statement of purpose is "Honoring the Lord in all we do by operating the company in a manner consistent with Biblical principles." The facts most lists get stale: full-time minimum hourly pay rose to **$21.00 effective October 5, 2026** (the fifteenth raise since 2009, per their own newsroom), and every store is closed Sunday. Jobs at [careers.hobbylobby.com](https://careers.hobbylobby.com), actively posting.

**Chick-fil-A**: corporate purpose, in its own words, "To glorify God by being a faithful steward of all that is entrusted to us," with the famous Sunday closure explained on their site as a day for employees to rest, be with family, "or worship if they choose." The distinction that matters for job seekers: restaurant team members are employed by independent franchise Operators, not by Chick-fil-A, Inc., so pay and culture vary by owner; the Atlanta corporate roles are the company itself. Start at [chick-fil-a.com/careers](https://www.chick-fil-a.com/careers).

## The Ministries (Where Faith Is the Job Requirement)

**Compassion International** had the best verified benefits in our whole pass: a **10% employer retirement contribution on top of salary**, a minimum of **25 paid days off plus 15 holidays**, a full-time chaplain, and a remote-first workforce (their FAQ: "most of our positions are 100% remote"). Their careers page frames the ask directly: candidates "qualified in their field of expertise" who "have an active spiritual life." [Careers here](https://www.compassion.com/about-us/careers/); 24 openings at our check.

**World Vision US**: "Driven by our faith in Jesus," and explicit that hiring requires agreement with its Statement of Faith or the Apostles' Creed. Benefits include retirement match, tuition reimbursement, student loan assistance, and stated hybrid or remote roles. [worldvision.org/careers](https://www.worldvision.org/careers).

**Focus on the Family** (Colorado Springs): "a vibrant community of believers," with three weeks of vacation accruing from day one, monthly staff chapel, weekly devotions, and two free counseling consultations, all from their own benefits page. No remote roles advertised. [Careers](https://www.focusonthefamily.com/careers/).

**CBN** (Virginia Beach): "The mission of CBN is to preach the Gospel to the nations." Distinctive verified perks: daily chapel as part of the paid workday, paid leave for short-term mission trips, adoption assistance, and stated remote working options; 14 featured openings at our check. [cbn.com/careers](https://cbn.com/careers).

**Samaritan's Purse**: the ministry's faith statements are clear on its own site, but its careers portal blocked our verification entirely, so we can't report openings or employment terms first-hand. [samaritanspurse.org](https://www.samaritanspurse.org/) links its careers site from the homepage; check it directly.

## The Businesses

**Interstate Batteries** (Dallas): purpose, "To glorify God and enrich lives as we deliver the most trustworthy source of power to the world," and the most useful nuance on this list: its own culture page says spiritual activities are optional and employees do not have to be Christian. It was also the biggest verified hirer here, with **102 open jobs** on its Workday board at our check. [Join their team](https://www.interstatebatteries.com/about/join-our-team).

**Ramsey Solutions** (Franklin, TN; 900+ employees by its own count): "We do our work as unto the Lord," with benefits including a 401(k) match, an extra PTO week for serving a ministry, and stated 40-hour workweeks. Two things to know going in: the company is deliberately 100% in-office ("under one roof"), and its conduct-policy terminations have drawn national coverage ([NBC News, 2021](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/dave-ramsey-s-company-fires-employees-over-premarital-sex-court-n1262498)); read their culture pages and decide if it's your fit, which is exactly what they'd tell you too.

**Medi-Share / Christian Care Ministry** (Melbourne, FL): "a Marketplace Ministry that glorifies God by running an exceptional business," with verified benefits most companies can't touch: 100% paid medical for employees (99% for family), six weeks paid parental leave for both parents, and weekly chapel. Hiring is central-Florida local by their own description. [Careers](https://mychristiancare.org/careers/).

**Life.Church / YouVersion** (Edmond, OK): the church that builds the Bible App, with 47 open roles at our check, and a correction worth printing: despite a remote-friendly reputation, every single posting on its board, including all YouVersion engineering roles, was marked on-site in Edmond when we checked. "We believe a job is something you do, but a calling is who you are." [Careers](https://www.life.church/careers/).

A few famous names are absent because their sites blocked verification entirely (In-N-Out foremost); we'd rather skip an entry than print second-hand facts about someone's employer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the top Christian companies to work for?

From our November 2026 verification, the strongest combinations of stated faith culture and verified benefits: Compassion International (remote-first, 10% retirement contribution, 25+ days off), Hobby Lobby ($21 full-time minimum wage, closed Sundays), Lifeway (work-from-anywhere culture), Medi-Share (100% paid employee medical), and Interstate Batteries (biggest verified job count, faith optional). The right pick depends on whether you want faith as the mission or faith as the culture around a regular job.

### Do you have to be a Christian to work at a Christian company?

It depends entirely on the employer, and the honest ones say so plainly. Ministries like World Vision require agreement with a statement of faith; Compassion asks for an active spiritual life; Interstate Batteries states outright that employees need not be Christian. Religious organizations have legal latitude to hire by faith; read each employer's own careers language rather than assuming either way.

### Which Christian companies hire remote workers?

Verified in November 2026: Compassion International (remote-first), Lifeway (most openings remote), Faithlife/Logos (all current openings remote), World Vision (stated hybrid/remote roles), and CBN (remote options). Two reputation corrections: Life.Church/YouVersion's board was 100% on-site at our check, and Ramsey Solutions is deliberately in-office. The full landscape, boards included, is in our [remote Christian jobs guide](/blog/remote-christian-jobs).

### How do I find Christian companies near me?

Beyond the national names above, the practical paths are your denomination's or church's business network, your city's Christian business directory or chamber-style association, and the ministry job boards (ChurchStaffing, ChristianJobs) filtered by state, since churches and Christian schools are the most widespread faith-based employers in every region.
