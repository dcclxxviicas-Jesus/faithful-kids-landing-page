Lists of Christian news sites rot in a specific way: outlets get absorbed, renamed, or paywalled, and the lists keep linking the old names. So we verified every entry below on its own site on November 16, 2026, publishing actively that week, with what each actually covers and what it costs. Two corrections you won't find on older lists: Catholic News Agency's domain now redirects to EWTN News, and ChristianHeadlines.com is now Crosswalk Headlines.

A note on how this list works, same as our other verified lists: we publish one of the entries, it's listed first because this is our site, and that's the whole editorial bias, disclosed. The rest are real newsrooms we'd point a Christian reader to anyway.

## 1. Raising Faithful Kids (that's us)

**What it is:** the editorial section of Faithful Kids, written for one specific reader the big outlets underserve: Christian parents raising young kids. Real essays by Christian parents and commissioned writers, interviews, and original data journalism from our research desk, including published studies like our [Bible literacy study](/research/bible-literacy) built from tens of thousands of real quiz answers. No paywall, no ads.

**Read it:** [faithfulkids.app/parenting](/parenting). And it's open: if you're a Christian parent or writer with an essay in you, we publish contributors with real bylines; pitch [team@faithfulkids.app](mailto:team@faithfulkids.app) or see [write for us](/parenting/write-for-us). A real person reads every pitch and replies either way.

## 2. The Christian Post

The general-interest evangelical daily: church news, religious liberty, politics, and commentary at wire pace. Free to read. [christianpost.com](https://www.christianpost.com)

## 3. Christianity Today

The flagship evangelical magazine, "advancing the stories and ideas of the kingdom of God": reported features, theology, and global Christianity, in magazine journalism rather than headlines. Metered paywall (subscriptions from about $6/month). [christianitytoday.com](https://www.christianitytoday.com)

## 4. WORLD (World News Group)

Christian worldview journalism as a print magazine plus daily news podcasts; The World and Everything in It publishes every weekday and the audio is free, while articles sit behind a subscription from about $3.99/month. [wng.org](https://wng.org)

## 5. Baptist Press

The Southern Baptist Convention's official news service: convention, missions, and state-convention coverage (it's annual-meeting season as we write). Free. [baptistpress.com](https://www.baptistpress.com)

## 6. Baptist News Global

Independent news and commentary from a moderate Baptist perspective, under the banner "Conversations that matter"; expect analysis and opinion alongside original reporting, with a left-of-center lean relative to most of this list. Free. [baptistnews.com](https://baptistnews.com)

## 7. CBN News

The Christian Broadcasting Network's TV newsroom: US, world, Israel, and health coverage, video-forward and free. [cbn.com/news](https://cbn.com/news)

## 8. EWTN News (formerly Catholic News Agency)

The big correction for Catholic readers: catholicnewsagency.com now redirects to EWTN News, which carries the same trusted global Catholic coverage of the Church, the Vatican, and Pope Leo XIV under the new name, free. [ewtnnews.com](https://www.ewtnnews.com)

## 9. National Catholic Register

The Catholic newspaper, "a service of EWTN News": reported news plus commentary from a faithful-to-the-magisterium stance. Free online, print by subscription. (Yes, same parent as the entry above; listing both because they're distinct reads.) [ncregister.com](https://www.ncregister.com)

## 10. Religion News Service

The religion-beat wire, "covering the world of religion," and an honest clarification: RNS is not a Christian outlet; it's a secular-style newsroom covering all faiths, with Christianity the largest beat. Indispensable anyway. Free. [religionnews.com](https://religionnews.com)

## 11. MinistryWatch

The donor-accountability niche: a free financial-transparency database on Christian ministries plus investigative reporting, "keeping our ministries transparent, accountable, and credible." Free, donor-funded, no ads. [ministrywatch.com](https://ministrywatch.com)

## 12. The Roys Report

Independent watchdog journalism on churches and ministries, "Reporting the Truth. Restoring the Church.": abuse cases, financial misconduct, leadership failures. Note the domain: julieroys.com now redirects to [roysreport.com](https://roysreport.com). Free.

## 13. Relevant

The faith-and-culture magazine for younger Christians: music, film, faith, and justice, publishing steadily (and still printing a magazine). Mostly free with a paid tier. [relevantmagazine.com](https://relevantmagazine.com)

## 14. ChurchLeaders

The trade read for pastors and ministry staff: church news plus leadership resources, "resourcing, informing, and connecting a community of church leaders." Free. [churchleaders.com](https://churchleaders.com)

## 15. Plough

The literary quarterly: long-form essays, poetry, and ecumenical Christian thought under the banner "Another Life Is Possible." Essays, not news, and the most beautiful writing on this list. Web articles free; the print quarterly is paid. [plough.com](https://www.plough.com)

## Worth Knowing, Beyond the Fifteen

- **The Gospel Coalition** ([thegospelcoalition.org](https://www.thegospelcoalition.org)) publishes essays, theology, and cultural commentary rather than news; where Reformed evangelicals go for analysis, free.
- **Religion Unplugged** ([religionunplugged.com](https://religionunplugged.com)) is nonprofit religion journalism, Christian-leaning, part of the Institute for Nonprofit News. Free.
- **The Pillar** ([pillarcatholic.com](https://www.pillarcatholic.com)) does serious independent Catholic investigative work, especially on canon law and Vatican finances; free to read, subscriber-supported.
- **Crosswalk Headlines** is the new home of ChristianHeadlines.com (the old domain redirects); celebrity-faith and church-news aggregation, free.
- **Premier** is the UK's largest Christian media group; its Premier Christianity magazine was verifiably publishing this month, while its news site blocked our verification, so check [premierchristianity.com](https://www.premierchristianity.com) first for UK coverage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best Christian news sites?

For daily evangelical news, The Christian Post and CBN News (both free); for depth, Christianity Today and WORLD (both paid); for Catholic news, EWTN News and the National Catholic Register; for accountability reporting, MinistryWatch and The Roys Report; and for Christian parents specifically, our own Raising Faithful Kids. All were verified actively publishing in November 2026.

### What Christian news sources are free?

Most of this list: The Christian Post, Baptist Press, Baptist News Global, CBN News, EWTN News, Religion News Service, MinistryWatch, The Roys Report, ChurchLeaders, Religion Unplugged, Raising Faithful Kids, and Plough's web essays. The two big paid ones are Christianity Today (about $6/month) and WORLD (from $3.99/month, though its daily podcasts are free).

### Is there a Christian news site for parents?

That's the gap we built for: Raising Faithful Kids ([faithfulkids.app/parenting](/parenting)) runs essays, interviews, and original research for Christian parents of young kids, free and without a paywall, alongside the Faithful Kids app itself. For teen-and-tech guidance the parachurch outlets (Axis, Plugged In) cover reviews; our [best Christian blogs](/blog/best-christian-blogs) roundup maps that wider landscape.

### What happened to Catholic News Agency and Christian Headlines?

Both still exist under new names, which older lists haven't caught: Catholic News Agency's domain now redirects to EWTN News (ewtnnews.com), and ChristianHeadlines.com now redirects to Crosswalk Headlines. Same journalism, new mastheads; update your bookmarks.
