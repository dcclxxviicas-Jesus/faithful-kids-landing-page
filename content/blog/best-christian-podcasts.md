Most lists of the best Christian podcasts include at least one show that stopped years ago. So we checked every podcast below against its own feed on November 16, 2026: each one published a new episode within the last few weeks, and the length and cadence listed are the medians of its last twenty episodes, not the show's own marketing. At the end you'll find six famous shows that have ended or gone quiet, because older lists still send people to them.

## The Short Version

- **Read the whole Bible this year:** The Bible Recap (7 to 11 minutes a day) or The Bible in a Year with Fr. Mike Schmitz (about 24 minutes, Catholic).
- **Go deep on Scripture:** BibleProject.
- **Hard questions and apologetics:** Unbelievable?, Reasonable Faith, Ask NT Wright Anything.
- **Faith and the news:** The Briefing (daily) or The Holy Post (weekly, with humour).
- **Pray, not just listen:** Pray As You Go or Pray the Word (5 minutes).

## Bible Teaching

**1. The Bible Recap** (Tara-Leigh Cobble). A short daily recap that walks you through a chronological reading plan, one year at a time; it's in its eighth year and published "Day 320, Acts 1-3" on the morning we checked. Seven to eleven minutes, daily. The easiest daily Bible habit on this list. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id1440833267)

**2. The Bible in a Year with Fr. Mike Schmitz** (Ascension). Daily readings plus a reflection, through the whole Bible; the Catholic counterpart to The Bible Recap. About 24 minutes, daily. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id1539568321)

**3. BibleProject** (Tim Mackie and Jon Collins). Long, unhurried conversations that trace a theme or a book through the whole Bible; currently a series through the Psalms. About 56 minutes, weekly. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id1050832450)

**4. Truth For Life** (Alistair Begg). Verse-by-verse teaching, a little over twenty minutes, every day. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id91473880)

**5. Timothy Keller Sermons** (Gospel in Life). Archive sermons from Redeemer Presbyterian in New York, released about three times a week since Keller's death in 2023. About 46 minutes. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id352660924)

## Christian Apologetics and Theology Podcasts

**6. Unbelievable?** (Premier). Christians and skeptics in real dialogue, plus history and science of faith; now hosted by Ruth Jackson and a team rather than its founding host. About 74 minutes, roughly weekly. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id267142101)

**7. Ask NT Wright Anything** (Premier). Listener questions answered by the New Testament scholar. About 40 minutes, weekly. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id1441656192)

**8. Reasonable Faith** (William Lane Craig). Philosophy and the defence of Christian belief, often responding to named critics. Usually under 20 minutes, about twice a week. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id252618197)

**9. Ask Pastor John** (John Piper, Desiring God). Short answers to real listener questions about Scripture and life. About 13 minutes, twice a week. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id618132843)

## Faith, Culture and the News

**10. The Briefing** (R. Albert Mohler Jr.). The day's news analysed from a Christian worldview, every weekday morning. About 26 minutes. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id390278738)

**11. The Holy Post** (Phil Vischer, Skye Jethani, Kaitlyn Schiess). Faith, politics and culture with humour, from the creator of VeggieTales; past episode 746. About 88 minutes, weekly. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id591157388)

**12. The Russell Moore Show** (Christianity Today). Faith and culture plus listener questions. About 45 minutes, once or twice a week. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id1074011166)

## Christian Podcasts for Women

**13. That Sounds Fun** (Annie F. Downs). Warm interviews about faith, friendship and life. About 69 minutes, weekly. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id944925529)

**14. With The Perrys** (Jackie Hill Perry and Preston Perry). Faith, marriage and culture from a married couple. About 64 minutes, weekly. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id1458672189)

**15. Therapy and Theology** (Lysa TerKeurst, Proverbs 31 Ministries). Counselling and theology for hard seasons, now in season 14. About 39 minutes, weekly in season. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id1641587501)

**16. The Daily Grace Podcast** (The Daily Grace Co.). Bible study and encouragement for women. About 31 minutes, weekly. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id1450011728)

**17. Jesus Calling: Stories of Faith.** Guest stories of faith tied to the bestselling devotional. About 24 minutes, roughly weekly. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id1081967443)

## Christian Podcasts for Men

**18. Wild at Heart** (John Eldredge). Masculine spirituality and restoring the heart, past episode 900. About 58 minutes, weekly. An honest note: this is the thinnest category in Christian podcasting, and Wild at Heart is the only big men's show we could confirm is still active.

## Daily Prayer and Devotional Podcasts

**19. Pray As You Go** (Jesuit). Guided daily prayer with music, Scripture and a reflection; episodes are released a day ahead. About 14 minutes. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id1447749598)

**20. Pray the Word** (David Platt). Praying through Scripture one verse at a time, about five minutes a day. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id1270453166)

**21. Abide Bible Sleep Meditation.** Bible-based meditations for falling asleep, about 17 minutes, every two weeks. [Listen](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id1488040861)

## Shows That Ended or Went Quiet

Older lists still recommend these. Here's where each one actually stands, from its own feed:

- **The Rise and Fall of Mars Hill** (Christianity Today) ended with its epilogue in November 2022; the feed was later reused for a different limited series.
- **The Happy Hour with Jamie Ivey** has had no new episodes since June 2024, when the host said she was stepping away from podcasting for a while.
- **Let's Talk** (The Gospel Coalition) aired its final episode in July 2022. Jackie Hill Perry now hosts With The Perrys, above.
- **The Liturgists** has had no regular episode since September 2023.
- **Questioning Christianity with Tim Keller** was a limited series that finished in June 2022.
- **BibleProject: Reflections** ended in August 2021 with an episode titled "Our Final Episode." The main BibleProject show continues.

One correction to a rumour: **Truth's Table has not ended.** It finished its tenth season on October 31, 2026 and is between seasons.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best Christian podcast to start with?

A daily Bible-reading show, because it builds a habit: The Bible Recap (7 to 11 minutes) if you want short, or The Bible in a Year with Fr. Mike Schmitz (about 24 minutes) if you want a Catholic reading and reflection. Both were publishing daily as of November 16, 2026.

### What is a good daily Christian podcast?

For Bible reading: The Bible Recap (7 to 11 minutes) or The Bible in a Year (about 24 minutes). For news: The Briefing (weekdays). For prayer: Pray As You Go (about 14 minutes) or Pray the Word (about 5 minutes). For teaching: Truth For Life (about 22 minutes).

### What are good Christian podcasts for women?

That Sounds Fun with Annie F. Downs, With The Perrys, Therapy and Theology with Lysa TerKeurst, The Daily Grace Podcast, and Jesus Calling: Stories of Faith, all verified publishing weekly in November 2026.

### Are there good Christian podcasts for men?

Fewer than you'd expect. Wild at Heart with John Eldredge is the established one and was publishing weekly in November 2026; many men's lists also include the theology and apologetics shows above, like Unbelievable? and Reasonable Faith.

### Is Hallow a podcast?

No. Hallow and Lectio 365 are prayer apps, not podcasts, which is why they're not on this list. For an audio prayer habit inside a podcast app, try Pray As You Go or Pray the Word.
