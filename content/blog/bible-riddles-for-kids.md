Want to make Bible learning feel like a game? Riddles are one of the best ways to get kids thinking about Bible stories without it feeling like homework. They challenge kids to recall characters, events, and details from Scripture, all while laughing and competing with siblings, friends, or parents.

These 45 Bible riddles are perfect for car rides, dinner table fun, Sunday school classes, family game nights, or any time you want to sneak some biblical literacy into a good time. Each riddle includes the answer and the Bible reference, so you can look up the story together afterward. For the youngest solvers who need a gentler warm-up first, start with our [easy Bible trivia questions](/blog/easy-bible-trivia-questions).

They are sorted into four sections: 20 classic riddles to start with, a batch of Old Testament riddles, a batch of New Testament riddles, and four tricky ones saved for the kid who already knows every Bible story cold.

Ready? See how many your family can solve.

## The Classic 20 Riddles

### Riddle #1
**I was swallowed by a great fish and lived inside it for three days and three nights. Who am I?**

Answer: **Jonah** (Jonah 1:17). God told Jonah to go to Nineveh, but Jonah ran the other way and ended up in the belly of a huge fish. After three days, the fish spit him out on dry land, and Jonah finally obeyed.

---

### Riddle #2
**I killed a giant with just a stone and a sling. I later became the most famous king in the Bible. Who am I?**

Answer: **David** (1 Samuel 17). As a young shepherd boy, David defeated the Philistine giant Goliath with a single stone. He went on to become Israel's greatest king and wrote many of the Psalms.

---

### Riddle #3
**I built a boat big enough for my family and two of every animal, even though it had never rained before. Who am I?**

Answer: **Noah** (Genesis 6-9). God warned Noah about a coming flood and told him to build an ark. Noah obeyed, and his family and the animals were saved when the waters covered the earth.

---

### Riddle #4
**I was sold by my brothers, thrown in a pit, sent to prison, and ended up ruling an entire country. Who am I?**

Answer: **Joseph** (Genesis 37-50). Joseph's jealous brothers sold him into slavery, but God was with him through every hardship. He eventually became the second most powerful man in Egypt.

---

### Riddle #5
**I am a wall that fell down when people marched around me and blew trumpets. What am I?**

Answer: **The walls of Jericho** (Joshua 6). God told Joshua to have the Israelites march around the city once a day for six days, then seven times on the seventh day. When they blew their trumpets and shouted, the walls collapsed.

---

### Riddle #6
**I was so strong I could tear a lion apart with my bare hands, but a haircut took away my power. Who am I?**

Answer: **Samson** (Judges 13-16). Samson's incredible strength came from God and was connected to his uncut hair. When Delilah tricked him into revealing his secret, his hair was cut and his strength left him.

---

### Riddle #7
**I am a bush that burned with fire but was never destroyed. What am I?**

Answer: **The burning bush** (Exodus 3). God spoke to Moses from a bush that was on fire but did not burn up. Through this miracle, God called Moses to lead the Israelites out of Egypt.

---

### Riddle #8
**I am a tiny boy who heard God call my name in the middle of the night. At first I thought it was my teacher, Eli. Who am I?**

Answer: **Samuel** (1 Samuel 3). Young Samuel was sleeping in the temple when God called his name. He ran to Eli three times before the old priest realized it was God speaking. Samuel became one of Israel's greatest prophets.

---

### Riddle #9
**I was a queen who risked her life to save her people from an evil plan. My story never mentions God by name, but He is all over it. Who am I?**

Answer: **Esther** (Esther 4-7). Queen Esther approached the king without being summoned, which could have meant death, to expose Haman's plot to destroy the Jewish people. Her courage saved an entire nation.

---

### Riddle #10
**I am a sea that split in half so people could walk through on dry ground. What am I?**

Answer: **The Red Sea** (Exodus 14). God parted the Red Sea through Moses so the Israelites could escape from the Egyptian army. When the Egyptians tried to follow, the waters crashed back together.

---

### Riddle #11
**I walked on water but started to sink when I got scared. Jesus had to catch me. Who am I?**

Answer: **Peter** (Matthew 14:28-31). When Peter saw Jesus walking on the water during a storm, he asked to come out and join Him. Peter walked on the water toward Jesus, but when he looked at the waves, fear took over and he began to sink. Jesus immediately reached out His hand.

---

### Riddle #12
**I was a tax collector sitting in a tree because I was too short to see over the crowd. Jesus called me by name and came to my house for dinner. Who am I?**

Answer: **Zacchaeus** (Luke 19:1-10). Zacchaeus climbed a sycamore tree to see Jesus passing through Jericho. Jesus looked up and said, "Zacchaeus, come down immediately. I must stay at your house today." Zacchaeus was so changed by the encounter that he gave half his possessions to the poor.

---

### Riddle #13
**I am food that fell from the sky every morning to feed hungry travelers in the desert. What am I?**

Answer: **Manna** (Exodus 16). When the Israelites were hungry in the wilderness, God sent manna (a flaky, bread-like substance) from heaven every morning. They could gather only enough for each day, trusting God to provide again tomorrow.

---

### Riddle #14
**We are a pair of brothers. One was hairy, and the other was smooth-skinned. Our father could not tell us apart when he was old and blind. Who are we?**

Answer: **Esau and Jacob** (Genesis 27). When Isaac was old and blind, Jacob put goatskins on his arms to feel hairy like Esau and tricked his father into giving him the firstborn blessing.

---

### Riddle #15
**I am a coat of many colors that made my brothers so jealous they could not stand me. What am I?**

Answer: **Joseph's coat** (Genesis 37:3). Joseph's father, Jacob, gave him a special colorful coat to show his favoritism. This made Joseph's brothers so jealous that they eventually sold him into slavery.

---

### Riddle #16
**I am a den full of hungry lions, but the man inside me was not harmed because God shut the lions' mouths. What am I?**

Answer: **The lion's den** (Daniel 6). Daniel was thrown into a den of lions for praying to God instead of the king. God sent an angel to shut the lions' mouths, and Daniel came out without a scratch.

---

### Riddle #17
**We were three friends who were thrown into a fiery furnace so hot it killed the guards who threw us in, but we walked out without even smelling like smoke. Who are we?**

Answer: **Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego** (Daniel 3). These three young men refused to bow down to King Nebuchadnezzar's golden statue. They were thrown into a blazing furnace, but God protected them. The king even saw a fourth figure walking with them in the fire.

---

### Riddle #18
**I am a donkey who talked to my owner because he could not see the angel of the Lord standing in the road. What Bible story am I from?**

Answer: **Balaam's donkey** (Numbers 22:21-34). The prophet Balaam was riding his donkey when the animal saw an angel with a drawn sword blocking the path. When Balaam beat the donkey for stopping, God opened the donkey's mouth and it spoke to Balaam.

---

### Riddle #19
**I am a boy who gave Jesus five loaves of bread and two fish. Jesus used my lunch to feed over 5,000 people. Who am I?**

Answer: **The boy with the loaves and fish** (John 6:1-14). His name is not recorded in the Bible, but his small offering became one of Jesus' greatest miracles. Jesus took the boy's lunch, gave thanks, and multiplied it to feed a massive crowd with twelve baskets of leftovers.

---

### Riddle #20
**I am a star that appeared in the sky to announce the birth of a king. Wise men followed me from the east all the way to Bethlehem. What am I?**

Answer: **The Star of Bethlehem** (Matthew 2:1-12). When Jesus was born, a special star appeared in the sky. Wise men (also called Magi) saw it and traveled a great distance to find the newborn king. They brought gifts of gold, frankincense, and myrrh.

## More Old Testament Riddles

### Riddle #21
**I am a garden where the first people ever lived. Everything in me was perfect until one bad choice changed the world. What am I?**

Answer: **The Garden of Eden** (Genesis 2-3). God placed Adam and Eve in a beautiful garden with only one rule. When they ate the fruit God had told them to leave alone, sin entered the world and they had to leave the garden.

---

### Riddle #22
**I was the very first man, made from the dust of the ground, and God let me give every animal its name. Who am I?**

Answer: **Adam** (Genesis 2:7, 19-20). God formed Adam from dust and breathed life into him. Then God brought every animal to Adam, and whatever Adam called it, that became its name.

---

### Riddle #23
**I am a colorful arch that appears in the clouds after the rain. God put me in the sky as a promise. What am I?**

Answer: **The rainbow** (Genesis 9:12-17). After the flood, God set a rainbow in the clouds as the sign of His promise never again to destroy the whole earth with a flood.

---

### Riddle #24
**I was an old man with no children when God took me outside, pointed at the night sky, and promised my family would be as many as the stars. Who am I?**

Answer: **Abraham** (Genesis 15:5). God promised Abraham descendants too many to count, and Abraham believed Him. Years later, when Abraham was 100, his son Isaac was born.

---

### Riddle #25
**I was a baby hidden in a basket among the reeds of a river, and a princess found me and raised me as her own. Who am I?**

Answer: **Moses** (Exodus 2:1-10). To save him from Pharaoh's order, Moses' mother placed him in a waterproof basket on the Nile. Pharaoh's daughter found him, and Moses grew up in the palace of the very king who wanted him gone.

---

### Riddle #26
**I am a tower built by proud people who wanted to reach all the way to heaven. God mixed up their words and they scattered across the earth. What am I?**

Answer: **The Tower of Babel** (Genesis 11:1-9). Everyone on earth spoke one language and decided to build a tower to make a name for themselves. God confused their language so they could not understand each other, and the building stopped.

---

### Riddle #27
**I am ten rules that God wrote with His own finger on two stone tablets and gave to a man on a smoking mountain. What am I?**

Answer: **The Ten Commandments** (Exodus 20; Exodus 31:18). God called Moses up Mount Sinai and gave him the commandments on two tablets of stone. They taught Israel how to love God and how to treat each other.

---

### Riddle #28
**I was not born an Israelite, but I refused to leave my mother-in-law. I told her, "Where you go I will go." Who am I?**

Answer: **Ruth** (Ruth 1:16). Ruth was from Moab, but after her husband died she stayed with Naomi and followed Naomi's God. She later married Boaz and became the great-grandmother of King David.

---

### Riddle #29
**God offered to give me anything I asked for. I asked for wisdom instead of money, so God gave me both. Who am I?**

Answer: **Solomon** (1 Kings 3:5-13). God appeared to young King Solomon in a dream and told him to ask for whatever he wanted. Solomon asked for a wise and understanding heart, and God gave him wisdom plus riches and honor he never requested.

---

### Riddle #30
**I was a prophet fed by ravens beside a brook, and at the end of my life I did not die. I went up to heaven in a whirlwind. Who am I?**

Answer: **Elijah** (1 Kings 17:4-6; 2 Kings 2:11). During a drought, God sent ravens to bring Elijah bread and meat morning and evening. Later, chariots of fire appeared and Elijah was taken up to heaven in a whirlwind while Elisha watched.

---

### Riddle #31
**I tested God twice with a piece of wool, then beat a huge army using only 300 men, some trumpets, and jars with torches hidden inside. Who am I?**

Answer: **Gideon** (Judges 6-7). Gideon asked God for a sign using a wool fleece, twice. Then God shrank his army from 22,000 men to 300 so everyone would know the victory came from God, not from soldiers.

---

### Riddle #32
**I lost my animals, my home, my children, and my health in a single terrible week, and I still would not turn away from God. Who am I?**

Answer: **Job** (Job 1-2; Job 42). Job suffered more than almost anyone in Scripture, yet he kept trusting God even when he did not understand. In the end, God restored him with more than he had before.

## New Testament Riddles

### Riddle #33
**An angel visited me and told me I would have a son named Jesus, even though I was not yet married. I answered, "I am the Lord's servant." Who am I?**

Answer: **Mary** (Luke 1:26-38). The angel Gabriel told Mary she would give birth to the Son of God. Mary was young and probably frightened, but she said yes to God's plan.

---

### Riddle #34
**We were working the night shift in a field when the sky suddenly filled with angels telling us a Savior had been born. Who are we?**

Answer: **The shepherds** (Luke 2:8-20). Shepherds watching their flocks near Bethlehem were the first people told about Jesus' birth. They hurried to see Him and then told everyone they met.

---

### Riddle #35
**I lived in the desert, wore camel hair clothes, ate locusts and wild honey, and baptized Jesus in the Jordan River. Who am I?**

Answer: **John the Baptist** (Matthew 3:1-17). John prepared the way for Jesus by calling people to turn back to God. When he baptized Jesus, the Spirit came down like a dove and God's voice said, "This is my Son, whom I love."

---

### Riddle #36
**I was sitting at my tax booth when Jesus walked by and said two words: "Follow me." I got up and left everything. Who am I?**

Answer: **Matthew** (Matthew 9:9). Tax collectors were hated in Israel, but Jesus chose Matthew as one of the twelve disciples. Matthew later wrote the Gospel that carries his name.

---

### Riddle #37
**I had been dead and buried for four days when Jesus stood outside my tomb and shouted my name. Then I walked out. Who am I?**

Answer: **Lazarus** (John 11:1-44). Jesus wept at the tomb of His friend, then called, "Lazarus, come out!" Lazarus walked out still wrapped in grave cloths, and many people believed in Jesus that day.

---

### Riddle #38
**I am a storm so violent that experienced fishermen thought they were going to drown, until Jesus stood up and told me to be quiet. What am I?**

Answer: **The storm on the Sea of Galilee** (Mark 4:35-41). Jesus was asleep in the back of the boat when the squall hit. He woke up, said, "Peace, be still," and the wind and waves went completely calm.

---

### Riddle #39
**I used to arrest Christians, until a blinding light knocked me to the ground on the road to Damascus. I spent the rest of my life writing letters to churches. Who am I?**

Answer: **Paul** (Acts 9:1-19). Saul was on his way to arrest believers when Jesus appeared to him in a bright light. He was blind for three days, then became Paul, the missionary who wrote thirteen books of the New Testament.

---

### Riddle #40
**I gave two tiny coins worth almost nothing, and Jesus told everyone I had given more than all the rich people put together. Who am I?**

Answer: **The widow with two coins** (Mark 12:41-44). Wealthy people dropped large amounts into the temple offering box, but the poor widow gave everything she had. Jesus measured her gift by her heart, not by the amount.

---

### Riddle #41
**I am a place with a stone rolled away and nothing inside but folded cloths. An angel told the women, "He is not here. He has risen." What am I?**

Answer: **The empty tomb** (Matthew 28:1-6; John 20:1-9). On Sunday morning, the women who came to Jesus' tomb found the stone moved and the tomb empty. The resurrection is the center of the whole Bible.

## Tricky Riddles for Bible Champions

### Riddle #42
**I hid two spies on my roof under stalks of flax and hung a scarlet cord in my window so my family would be safe. Who am I?**

Answer: **Rahab** (Joshua 2). Rahab lived in Jericho and helped the Israelite spies escape. When the city fell, she and her family were spared, and she later appears in the family line of Jesus (Matthew 1:5).

---

### Riddle #43
**I am mysterious writing that appeared on a palace wall during a king's feast. Only one man in the kingdom could read me. What am I?**

Answer: **The writing on the wall** (Daniel 5). During Belshazzar's feast, a hand appeared and wrote words no one could explain. Daniel read them and told the king his kingdom was about to end, and that very night it did.

---

### Riddle #44
**I was a broken-down wall around a ruined city, and a cupbearer turned builder put me back up in just 52 days while enemies mocked him. What am I?**

Answer: **The wall of Jerusalem** (Nehemiah 6:15). Nehemiah served wine to the king of Persia before God gave him the job of rebuilding Jerusalem's wall. Despite threats and insults, the whole project took less than two months.

---

### Riddle #45
**I am a day when the sun stopped in the middle of the sky so a battle could be finished. What am I?**

Answer: **The day the sun stood still** (Joshua 10:12-14). Joshua prayed for more daylight during a battle to defend Israel's allies, and God answered by holding the sun in place. Scripture says there has never been a day like it before or since.

## How to Use These Riddles

**Car rides.** Read one riddle at a time and let the family compete. Award points for correct answers and bonus points if they can name the Bible book where the story is found. When the riddles run out, keep the laughs going with these [Bible jokes for kids](/blog/bible-jokes-for-kids), or switch to our all-ages set of [funny Bible jokes](/blog/funny-bible-jokes) so the grown-ups in the front seat get something to groan at too.

**Sunday school.** Print the riddles on cards and use them as an icebreaker or review activity. Split kids into teams for extra competition.

**Dinner table.** Share one riddle per meal for 45 days. Over a month and a half, your family will have reviewed 45 major Bible stories without anyone feeling like they are studying.

**Game nights.** Combine these riddles with a board game, or mix them into one of these [Bible games for kids](/blog/bible-games-for-kids). Every time someone lands on a certain space, they have to answer a Bible riddle to advance.

**Bedtime.** Read one riddle as a bedtime brain teaser. If your child gets it right, they choose the bedtime story. If not, you look up the Bible passage together and read the real story.

## Why Riddles Help Kids Learn the Bible

Riddles work because they engage active recall, which is one of the most powerful learning techniques. Instead of passively hearing a story, kids have to search their memory for the answer. This strengthens the neural pathways that store Bible knowledge and makes the stories easier to remember next time. ([Bible trivia questions for kids](/blog/bible-trivia-for-kids) work the same recall muscle in a straight question-and-answer format, and our [Bible quiz questions and answers](/blog/bible-quiz-questions-and-answers) page turns that recall practice into a scored round.)

Riddles also create positive emotional associations with the Bible. When a child solves a Bible riddle and feels the rush of getting it right, their brain links Scripture with excitement and fun. That emotional connection is priceless for long-term spiritual formation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What age group are these Bible riddles best for?
These riddles work well for kids ages 6-14. Younger children (ages 6-8) may need hints or multiple-choice options. Older kids (ages 9-14) can try to answer from memory and name the Bible book where each story is found.

### Can I use these riddles for Sunday school or Vacation Bible School?
Absolutely. These riddles are free to use for church groups, Sunday school classes, VBS programs, homeschool co-ops, and family gatherings. Print them on cards, project them on a screen, or read them aloud.

### How can I make these riddles harder for kids who already know the Bible well?
Add extra challenges: require the child to name the Bible book and chapter, tell the story in their own words before revealing the answer, or explain why the story matters. You can also ask follow-up questions like, "What happened next in this story?"

### Where can I find more Bible riddles for kids?
You can create your own from any Bible story. Read a story with your child and then write a riddle together from a character's perspective. This exercise actually deepens comprehension more than answering riddles alone because it requires the child to think about the story from the inside out.
