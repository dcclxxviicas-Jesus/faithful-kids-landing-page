## Warm-Up Round: Deceptively Easy

Every question in this round sounds like Sunday school — and most tables miss at least three, because the answer people remember is not quite the answer in the text. Read the reference aloud after each reveal; the near-misses are where the conversation starts.

1. How many wise men does the Bible say visited Jesus? **It never gives a number — Matthew names three gifts, not three men** (Matthew 2:1, 11)
2. Where was Jesus when the wise men found him — still in the manger? **In a house; by the time they arrived, the family was no longer at the manger** (Matthew 2:11)
3. What kind of creature swallowed Jonah? **A great fish — the text never calls it a whale** (Jonah 1:17)
4. What did David actually kill Goliath with? **The stone knocked him down, but David killed him with Goliath's own sword** (1 Samuel 17:50-51)
5. Who cut off Samson's hair? **Neither Samson nor Delilah — she called in a man to shave his head while Samson slept** (Judges 16:19)
6. Where does the Bible say "God helps those who help themselves"? **Nowhere — the phrase never appears in Scripture** (cf. Proverbs 3:5)
7. How many people were saved on Noah's ark? **Eight — Noah, his wife, his three sons, and their wives** (Genesis 7:13; 1 Peter 3:20)
8. On which day of creation did God make the sun? **The fourth — light existed from day one, but the sun came later** (Genesis 1:14-19)
9. How tall was Goliath? **Six cubits and a span — over nine feet** (1 Samuel 17:4)
10. What did the prodigal son plan to ask his father when he came home? **To be taken back as a hired servant, not a son** (Luke 15:19)
11. Who asked God, "Am I my brother's keeper?" **Cain** (Genesis 4:9)
12. What trade did Jesus' first four disciples work? **They were fishermen** (Matthew 4:18-22)
13. What was Matthew's job before he followed Jesus? **Tax collector** (Matthew 9:9)
14. In Jesus' parable, the wise man built his house on what? **The rock** (Matthew 7:24)
15. Which disciple said he would not believe the resurrection until he touched Jesus' wounds? **Thomas** (John 20:25-27)

## Hard Round: The People Inside the Stories

This round assumes the famous stories are known and asks about the names, places, and details inside them. It separates the people who grew up hearing the stories from the people who have read them.

16. Which two people in the Bible are described as leaving this life without dying? **Enoch and Elijah** (Genesis 5:24; 2 Kings 2:11)
17. Who was the left-handed judge who killed King Eglon of Moab? **Ehud** (Judges 3:15-21)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are some fun Bible trivia questions for adults?

The most fun questions for adult groups are the ones where the popular version of a story differs from the text: how many wise men there were (the Bible never says), what David killed Goliath with (his own sword, after the stone), and who cut Samson's hair (a man Delilah called in). The warm-up round above is built entirely from questions like these, because a room full of adults confidently getting the same question wrong is the best icebreaker there is.

### How hard should Bible trivia be for a church small group?

Mixed difficulty beats uniform difficulty. A good session runs about one-third questions most people will get (to keep everyone playing), one-third detail questions that reward regular readers, and a few expert questions that nobody is embarrassed to miss. The three rounds on this page follow exactly that split — and letting teams confer means newer believers contribute without being put on the spot.

### What name is mentioned about 7,000 times in the Bible?

The personal name of God — Yahweh, rendered in most English Bibles as "the LORD" in small capitals — appears close to 7,000 times in the Old Testament, making it by far the most frequent name in Scripture. Among people, David is one of the most mentioned, with around a thousand appearances across both testaments.
