These 120 Bible trivia questions for adults are pitched at exactly the level a church game night needs: hard enough that nobody sweeps the board, but never so obscure that only the seminary graduate in the room has a chance. Every answer comes in bold right after the question, with a verse reference so you can settle disputes on the spot.

The questions are sorted into four topical rounds of 30 -- Old Testament, New Testament, Wisdom & Prophets, and the early church in Acts -- so you can run them as themed rounds, let teams pick categories Jeopardy-style, or mix and match. If you're hosting parents and kids together, keep this list for the grown-ups' turns and grab our [100 Bible trivia questions and answers](/blog/100-bible-trivia-questions-and-answers) for the all-ages rounds.

## How to Run Adult Bible Trivia Night

Split the room into teams of three to five and rotate through the four categories, ten questions per round -- that's a tidy 40-question game that runs about an hour. Award two points for a correct answer and one bonus point for naming the book it comes from. The verse references after each answer make you the instant referee: when someone insists the Bible says "apple" or that two of every animal boarded the ark, you can open the text and enjoy the moment.

A few hosting tips that make the difference between a quiz and a party: read questions aloud rather than passing papers around, let teams steal on a miss, and save the Acts round for last -- it's the category most adults know least, so it keeps the game alive until the end. ## Old Testament Trivia Questions for Adults

1. On which day of creation did God make the sun, moon, and stars? **The fourth day** (Genesis 1:16-19)
2. What was Abraham's name before God changed it? **Abram** (Genesis 17:5)
3. Which two cities did God destroy with burning sulfur? **Sodom and Gomorrah** (Genesis 19:24)
4. What new name did God give Jacob after he wrestled with God at Peniel? **Israel** (Genesis 32:28)
5. Where was Moses when he saw the burning bush? **Horeb, the mountain of God** (Exodus 3:1-2)
6. Who was Moses' father-in-law, the priest of Midian? **Jethro** (Exodus 3:1)
7. What was the first plague God sent on Egypt? **The Nile turned to blood** (Exodus 7:20)
8. What was the tenth and final plague? **The death of every firstborn in Egypt** (Exodus 12:29)
9. What does the name "manna" come from -- what did the Israelites say when they first saw it? **"What is it?"** (Exodus 16:15, 31)
10. Which leader actually fashioned the golden calf while Moses was on the mountain? **Aaron** (Exodus 32:2-4)
11. Which two of the twelve spies believed Israel could take the Promised Land? **Joshua and Caleb** (Numbers 14:6-9)
12. Why was Moses not allowed to enter the Promised Land? **He struck the rock at Meribah instead of speaking to it as God commanded** (Numbers 20:11-12)
13. Whose donkey spoke to him on the road? **Balaam's** (Numbers 22:28)
14. Who buried Moses, and in what land? **God himself buried him, in Moab** (Deuteronomy 34:5-6)
15. How many times did Israel march around Jericho on the seventh day? **Seven times** (Joshua 6:15)
16. For which leader did the sun stand still during a battle at Gibeon? **Joshua** (Joshua 10:13)
17. Which left-handed judge killed King Eglon of Moab with a hidden sword? **Ehud** (Judges 3:15-21)
18. Who was the only female judge of Israel? **Deborah** (Judges 4:4)
19. What did Gideon lay out -- twice -- to test God's promise of victory? **A wool fleece** (Judges 6:37-40)
20. How many men did God leave Gideon to defeat the Midianite army? **Three hundred** (Judges 7:7)
21. Who was Ruth's great-grandson? **King David** (Ruth 4:17)
22. Which prophet anointed both Saul and David as king? **Samuel** (1 Samuel 10:1; 16:13)
23. What was Goliath's hometown? **Gath** (1 Samuel 17:4)
24. Who was struck down for reaching out to steady the ark of the covenant? **Uzzah** (2 Samuel 6:6-7)
25. Which of David's sons declared himself king and drove his father out of Jerusalem? **Absalom** (2 Samuel 15:10)
26. When God offered Solomon anything he wanted, what did Solomon ask for? **A discerning heart to govern the people -- wisdom** (1 Kings 3:9)
27. Which foreign queen traveled to Jerusalem to test Solomon with hard questions? **The queen of Sheba** (1 Kings 10:1)
28. During which king's reign was the lost Book of the Law rediscovered in the temple? **Josiah's** (2 Kings 22:8)
29. Who told Esther she may have come to her royal position "for such a time as this"? **Mordecai** (Esther 4:14)
30. In how many days did Nehemiah's crews rebuild the wall of Jerusalem? **Fifty-two** (Nehemiah 6:15)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How hard should Bible trivia be for adults?

Aim for a 60-70 percent success rate -- hard enough that every team misses some, easy enough that nobody feels foolish. The questions above deliberately avoid seminary-level obscurity (no genealogies, no minor kings of the divided kingdom) and stick to named people, famous scenes, and quotable verses that regular churchgoers have actually encountered.

### How many trivia questions do I need for a church game night?

Forty questions fills about an hour with team discussion and scoring; sixty fills ninety minutes. Prepare more than you think you need -- rounds move faster with adults than with kids -- and keep ten tiebreaker questions in reserve. The four 30-question categories above give you three full game nights from one list.

### What's the best team format for adult Bible trivia?

Teams of three to five, mixed deliberately -- split up couples and seat newer attendees with longtime members, since the game is a disguised icebreaker. Rotate through topical rounds, allow one steal per question, and give a bonus point for naming the book. A "wager round" at the end, where teams bet their points on a final question, keeps last place interested.

### Which Bible category do adults get wrong most often?

The book of Acts, by a wide margin. Most adults know the Gospels and the Genesis-Exodus storyline from childhood, but the early church chapters -- Gamaliel, Rhoda, the Ethiopian official, the riot in Ephesus -- fade fast after Pentecost. That's exactly why the Acts round makes the best finale: everyone is guessing together.

### Can I use these questions for a mixed group with kids?

Yes, with adjustments -- pair each adult round with easier kid questions so every generation gets turns it can win. ### Are these questions suitable for any denomination?

Yes. Every question is drawn from the 66 books shared by all Protestant, Catholic, and Orthodox Bibles, and every answer cites chapter and verse rather than any tradition's interpretation. Wording follows the NIV, but the facts hold in any major translation.
