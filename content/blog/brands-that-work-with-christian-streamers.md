"I'm a Christian streamer. What brands should I actually work with?" Here are the top 10, verified: every program below was checked against the brand's own live page on November 16, 2026, each of the ten has a real, public way in, and the famous programs that died are named at the end so you don't waste a pitch on them. No "they probably do collabs" padding.

A note on how this list works, same as our [creators list](/blog/christian-brands-that-work-with-influencers): we are one of the brands on it (first, because it's our list; that's the only editorial bias, and we're telling you about it). The rest are real programs we'd point a Christian streamer to anyway.

## 1. Faithful Kids (that's us)

**What we make:** a Bible video app for kids ages 5-15, 300+ short video lessons covering the whole Bible, each about two minutes with a quiz and a reflection. No ads, no algorithm.

**The program:** free full access for your family, plus **paid collaborations** where the fit is genuine. Streamers are exactly who we're looking for: family-gaming channels, Christian variety streamers with parents in chat, moms and dads who stream. No follower minimums; we care whether your audience trusts you, not how big it is.

**How to apply:** email [team@faithfulkids.app](mailto:team@faithfulkids.app) with your channel links and one content idea (a "what our family's screen time looks like" segment, a stream-break plug, whatever fits your format). A real person reads every pitch and replies either way. Full details: [faithfulkids.app/creators](/creators).

## 2. Elevated Faith

**What they make:** Christian apparel and jewelry with a strong Gen-Z presence, which overlaps streaming's audience better than any other faith brand here.

**The program:** the [2026 brand ambassador program](https://elevatedfaith.com/pages/brand-ambassadors) is open: 10% commission on code and link orders, 15% off for your audience, payouts via PayPal or gift card from $25, and milestone rewards. Requires being 18+ and active on Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok, which a streamer with a clips presence satisfies.

## 3. Child of God Co.

**What they make:** faith-forward apparel.

**The program:** [brand ambassadors](https://childofgodco.net/brand-ambassadors) earn 10% commission while followers get 15% off, with free merch milestones and PayPal payouts mid-month. Open application, 18+, replies in about a week.

Streamers monetize with links too; see the verified [Christian affiliate programs](/blog/christian-affiliate-programs) directory, including the 50% program we run ourselves.

## 4. Logos Bible Software

**What they make:** the heavyweight Bible study platform, and the natural sponsor for anyone streaming Bible study, theology Q&A, or church services.

**The program:** a formal [partner program](https://www.logos.com/apply-to-partner) through Impact, with example thresholds of roughly 1,000 YouTube subscribers, 5,000 Instagram followers, or 10,000 monthly page views, alignment with their biblical-literacy mission, and promotion at least every 90 days.

## 5. Hosanna Revival

**What they make:** beautifully designed Bibles and Scripture tools.

**The program:** an [affiliate program](https://hosannarevival.com/pages/affiliate-home) aimed at women who point their audience to Scripture; the commission rate isn't published on the page. A fit for women streaming Bible study, art, or cozy variety content.

The next five aren't Christian brands, but all had open applications with no faith-based exclusions when we checked, and Christian streamers get sponsored through them every day.

## 6. Rogue Energy

**[Rogue Energy](https://rogueenergy.com/pages/sponsorship)** publishes its terms right on the page: up to 7% commission for affiliates, 10% for Partners, open to any 18+ creator with PayPal.

## 7. StreamElements

**[StreamElements](https://streamelements.com/)** runs vetted brand sponsorships you opt into from a free dashboard, with no follower minimum published; Razer-owned and alive, whatever the shutdown rumors said.

## 8. Razer

**[Razer](https://www.razer.com/affiliate)** pays up to 15% per sale, and its page tells streamers to apply through the #RazerStreamer program first.

## 9. G Fuel

**[G Fuel](https://gfuel.com/pages/affiliate-application)** keeps an open creator application with commission on code sales plus an audience discount code.

## 10. Humble Bundle

**[Humble Bundle](https://promo.humblebundle.com/affiliates)** welcomes Twitch, YouTube, Kick, TikTok, and Discord creators through Impact.

**Bonus, the platform itself:** since August 2026, Twitch Affiliates can complete a free certification and get matched to brand campaigns in the Sponsorship Dashboard. Full requirements, payout details, and the programs we checked that didn't make the cut are in our companion guide, [Twitch sponsorships for small streamers](/blog/twitch-sponsorships-for-small-streamers).

## The Dead List (November 2026)

Programs still recommended by other lists that no longer exist, verified the day this was published: **Kerusso**'s ambassador page is gone, **Walk In Faith Clothing**'s program redirects to its homepage, **We The Believers**' entire site is down, and **PowerSpike**, the sponsorship platform half the internet still cites, redirects to a holding company. Three of those were alive five days before our check, which is exactly why every entry on this page carries a date.

## How to Pitch Any Brand on This List

Three sentences: **who you are** (channel link and honest averages, not your best night), **why this brand** (one line proving you know what they sell), and **one concrete idea** ("a Saturday family stream where my kids and I unbox and react on camera"). Attach nothing. And guard your inbox: real offers come from the brand's own domain, never ask you to open a password-protected file, and never need your stream key; the [scam section of our sponsorships guide](/blog/twitch-sponsorships-for-small-streamers) covers the fakes targeting small streamers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best brands for Christian streamers to work with?

Verified open in November 2026: Faithful Kids (paid collaborations for Christian family creators, no follower minimum), Elevated Faith and Child of God Co. (open ambassador programs, 10% commission), Logos (for Bible-study content, with stated thresholds), and Hosanna Revival (women pointing audiences to Scripture), plus mainstream open-application programs like Rogue Energy, Razer, and StreamElements.

### Do Christian brands pay streamers or just give commission?

Mostly commission: the apparel ambassador programs pay a percentage of code sales. Faithful Kids offers scoped paid collaborations where the fit is genuine. Flat-rate paid deals in the faith niche are usually negotiated directly after you've shown a specific idea, not advertised on a program page.

### How many followers do I need before brands will work with me?

For most programs on this list, none: Elevated Faith, Child of God Co., and Faithful Kids state no follower minimum, and Twitch's sponsorship portal opens at Affiliate level (25 followers, 3 average viewers). Logos is the exception with published example thresholds around 1,000 YouTube subscribers.
