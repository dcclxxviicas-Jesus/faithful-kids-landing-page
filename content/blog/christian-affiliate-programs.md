"What Christian affiliate programs actually pay?" is a question mostly answered by lists that were last checked years ago, which matters because this niche churns: three faith-brand ambassador programs died in the five days before this page was published. So here is the verified answer, every program checked on its own page in November 2026, sorted by what you actually earn, with the dead programs named at the end so you stop pitching them.

The disclosure that makes this list honest: we run the highest-paying program on it, and it's listed first because this is our site. Everything else here is a real program we'd point a Christian affiliate to anyway.

## 1. Faithful Kids: 50% of every sale (that's us)

**What you'd promote:** a Bible video app for kids ages 5-15, where the whole Bible is short video lessons with quizzes, and Bible stories are interactive games kids play. No ads, no algorithm.

**The program:** affiliates earn **50% of every sale they refer**, which is several times this niche's going rate; as you'll see below, the standard faith-brand commission is 7-15%. There's no follower or traffic minimum, because the audience's trust in you matters more to us than its size.

**How to join:** email [team@faithfulkids.app](mailto:team@faithfulkids.app) with where you publish and how you plan to share it, and a real person replies with your link and the details. The full program is at [faithfulkids.app/affiliates](/affiliates).

## 2. The Faith-Apparel Ambassador Programs (10%)

The open-application standard of the niche, both verified live November 16, 2026:

- **[Elevated Faith](https://elevatedfaith.com/pages/brand-ambassadors)**: 10% commission on code and link orders, 15% off for your audience, payouts from $25 via PayPal or gift card, milestone rewards. 18+, active on Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok.
- **[Child of God Co.](https://childofgodco.net/brand-ambassadors)**: 10% commission, 15% off for followers, free merch milestones, PayPal payouts mid-month.

## 3. Logos Bible Software: the high-ticket pick

Bible software sells for real money, which makes [Logos's partner program](https://www.logos.com/apply-to-partner) (run through Impact) the high-ticket entry on this list even without a published rate. Their page gives example thresholds of roughly 1,000 YouTube subscribers, 5,000 Instagram followers, or 10,000 monthly page views, and asks for alignment with their biblical-literacy mission and promotion at least every 90 days.

## 4. Hosanna Revival

An [affiliate program](https://hosannarevival.com/pages/affiliate-home) for women who point their audience to Scripture; beautiful Bibles with devoted buyers. The commission rate isn't published on the page, so you learn it on acceptance.

## 5. The Network Programs (Rates on Acceptance)

Three established programs run through the big affiliate networks; none publishes a rate on its public page, so ignore the percentages in old roundups:

- **Christianbook.com** runs through CJ Affiliate, with commissions on qualified purchases. The biggest catalog in Christian retail, which makes it the broadest-match program here.
- **Bibles.com** (American Bible Society) pays commissions on store sales through Awin, and their page lists a direct contact for questions, which is rare and useful.
- **Angel Studios** runs show-specific partner programs (verified for The Tuttle Twins and Wingfeather Saga): an affiliate track with commissions on purchases and subscriptions, and an influencer track with rewards and early access. Built for anyone covering Christian kids' media.

## 6. The Ministry Partnerships (Not Quite Affiliate, Still Money)

- **World Vision** runs a partnerships program through Impact where you earn ad revenue when readers click through to their Gift Catalog, sponsorship, and campaign pages: sponsorship-adjacent income without a storefront.
- **Compassion International** has run blogger and influencer partnerships for years; their program pages block automated checking, so pitch directly at compassion.com.

Full context on both, plus display-ad networks and digital products, is in our guide to [how Christian bloggers make money](/blog/how-christian-bloggers-make-money), where every rate was verified the same way.

## The Dead List (Updated November 2026)

Programs that old roundups still recommend, verified gone:

- **DaySpring**: the famous affiliate program did not survive the brand's absorption into Hallmark; the old affiliate page redirects and nothing replaces it.
- **Crossway**: never was an affiliate program. It runs a review program (a free book monthly for an honest, disclosed review) that pays no commissions.
- **Kerusso, Walk In Faith Clothing, We The Believers**: all three ambassador programs died in mid-November 2026 (the last brand's whole site is down). We caught these because we re-verify; most lists won't for years.

## How to Actually Get Accepted

Programs in this niche approve people, not metrics. The application that works names where you publish, who reads or watches you (honestly sized), and the specific way you'll share the product: a curriculum roundup, a gift guide, a what-we-use post. Blast applications with no plan get ignored by the same programs that accept small, specific publishers the same week. And always disclose your links; every legitimate program above requires FTC-compliant disclosure, and your audience's trust is the entire asset you're monetizing.
