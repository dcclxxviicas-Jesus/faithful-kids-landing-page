Most "best Christian artists" lists are a decade behind: they lead with names that haven't released new music in years and miss the ones topping the charts right now. This one is built from what's verifiable this week: Billboard's Christian and gospel charts dated November 14, 2026, each artist's Spotify monthly listeners as shown on November 16, 2026, release dates from Apple's catalogue, and the winners of the 57th GMA Dove Awards held on October 6, 2026. Listener counts move every day, so read them as "about."

## The Short Version

- **Biggest right now:** Elevation Worship holds #1 on Billboard's Hot Christian Songs with "God I'm Just Grateful."
- **Artist of the Year:** Phil Wickham won the 2026 Dove Award.
- **The rising star:** Josiah Queen has the #1 Christian album (*Damascus Road*) and the Dove Song of the Year.
- **Largest audience:** Skillet, at about 11.6 million monthly listeners.
- **For kids and families:** Seeds Kids Worship, Hillsong Kids, Yancy and Rend Collective (at the end).

## Worship Artists

**1. Elevation Worship.** About 10.2 million monthly listeners, and "God I'm Just Grateful" with Chandler Moore is #1 on Hot Christian Songs; it also won the 2026 Dove for Worship Recorded Song. Latest album: *Promise & Presence* (August 2026). [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/3YCKuqpv9nCsIhJ2v8SMix)

**2. Phil Wickham.** The 2026 Dove **Artist of the Year**, with *Song Of The Saints* named Worship Album. "It Really Is Amazing Grace" with Crowder is #7 on Hot Christian Songs; about 4.8 million listeners. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/5d1JhBfyb58upMXCZOdbQu)

**3. Brandon Lake.** The 2026 Dove Songwriter of the Year, with *King Of Hearts* winning Pop/Contemporary Album. "How Good God Is" (August 2026) sits at #4 on Hot Christian Songs and #2 on Christian Airplay; about 6.6 million listeners. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/1bdnGJxkbIIys5Jhk1T74v)

**4. ELEVATION RHYTHM.** Elevation's youth-led project; "Washed" is #2 on Hot Christian Songs, about 4.4 million listeners. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/0qZ8aSF0iMCQI99AAXikF8)

**5. Chandler Moore.** Worship and gospel crossover; his solo "God, I'm Just Grateful" is #2 on Hot Gospel Songs. About 3.9 million listeners. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/6y7frW1RUq3XBBXbYowVpk)

**6. Maverick City Music.** About 6.1 million listeners; latest album *Holy* (June 2026). [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/58r1rB5t3VF5X6yXGPequV)

**7. Bethel Music.** About 7 million listeners, and just released "Noel (He Is Born) [Live]" for Christmas. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/26T4yOaOoFJvUvxR87Y9HO)

**8. Hillsong Worship.** About 7 million listeners; *Great I AM* came out in February 2026 and *Holy Is The Name* is listed for February 2027. (Hillsong UNITED hasn't released an album since 2023.) [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/3SgHzT552wy2W8pNLaLk24)

**9. Chris Tomlin.** About 5.4 million listeners, with a new Christmas album, *Good News of Great Joy* (November 6, 2026). [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/6pRi6EIPXz4QJEOEsBaA0m)

## Contemporary Christian (CCM) and Pop Artists

**12. Lauren Daigle.** About 5.5 million listeners; *Look Up Child* is still #12 on Top Christian Albums years after release, and she just put out a Christmas single with the Preservation Hall Jazz Band. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/40LHVA5BTQp9RxHOQ9JPYj)

**13. for KING & COUNTRY.** Back after a 2025 year of rest, with *the most beautiful colours* (September 2026); "What If I Told You?" is #14 on Hot Christian Songs. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/3sDbKMebVH2VYcRSl7u1VC)

**14. Jamie MacDonald.** The 2026 Dove **New Artist of the Year**; "A Million Chances" is #18 on Hot Christian Songs. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/0cZttbEqSCC76paqdgVLL4)

**15. Jon Reddick.** Holds **#1 on Christian Airplay** with "Holding On (Praise God)," and a Christmas album is listed for November 27. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/7H0gjeKVq6IPXcAUZzzhD2)

**16. Anne Wilson.** Christian country; "Who I Am To You" is #12 on Hot Christian Songs. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/75JvBeqW4BJ4xgnbMAq6MN)

**17. Tauren Wells.** *Here Comes The Church (Live)* (October 2026); about 3 million listeners. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/3SKza3YPBri1k43LB1Tqy4)

**18. Zach Williams.** Southern-rock-flavoured CCM; *Revival Nights At Red Rocks* (June 2026). [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/6g10GEtmIVqIQBhPZh4ScQ)

Also in this week's charts: Leanna Crawford ("Thank God"), Matt Maher ("Gonna Be Alright"), tobyMac ("Lord It Feels Good"), Casting Crowns and Jeremy Camp.

## Great for Kids and Families

If you're picking music for the car with kids in the back:

- **Seeds Kids Worship** sets Bible verses to music, with the reference in every song title; it's how a lot of families memorize Scripture. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/5kV2pC0biCYFh3y9HxgNkS)
- **Hillsong Kids** has Bible-story songs like "Joshua And The 12 Spies" and a new piano lullabies album (October 2026). [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/6B9zcemB6pCVlbLac9pwWF)
- **Yancy** writes worship for kids' ministries and families. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/3hm9cA8c92CwJnCIaGqaEu)
- **Shout Praises Kids** for preschool singalongs. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/0SKVoWXola9WXgw3PwMYpE)
- **Rend Collective** is folk worship that kids and adults both enjoy, with *The Evergreen Season* out in September 2026. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/11Y54BxlxC3UIAUkU2eadQ)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is the most popular Christian artist right now?

By the charts dated November 14, 2026, Elevation Worship holds #1 on Billboard's Hot Christian Songs, and Phil Wickham is the reigning Dove Artist of the Year. By audience size, Skillet leads with about 11.6 million monthly Spotify listeners, followed by Elevation Worship at about 10.2 million.

### Who won Artist of the Year at the 2026 Dove Awards?

Phil Wickham, at the 57th GMA Dove Awards on October 6, 2026 in Nashville. Jamie MacDonald won New Artist of the Year and Josiah Queen's "Dusty Bibles" won Song of the Year.

### Who are the best new Christian artists?

Josiah Queen (#1 Christian album and Dove Song of the Year), Jamie MacDonald (Dove New Artist of the Year), Jon Reddick (#1 on Christian Airplay), ELEVATION RHYTHM, and Leanna Crawford, all charting in November 2026.

### Who are the best Christian rappers?

Lecrae, KB and Andy Mineo all released new projects in 2026, and Hulvey won the 2026 Dove for Rap/Hip Hop Album with *COULD BE TONIGHT*.

### What Christian music is good for kids?

Seeds Kids Worship for Scripture memory, Hillsong Kids and Yancy for kids' worship, Shout Praises Kids for preschoolers, and Rend Collective for the whole family. Many families also play the current worship hits from Elevation Worship and Phil Wickham.
