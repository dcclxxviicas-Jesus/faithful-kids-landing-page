## The Bible Editions of the Classics (Cactus Game Design)

One small publisher carries most of this category: Cactus Game Design licenses the games you already know how to play and reskins them biblically, all at a flat $30 direct from [their store](https://www.cactusgamedesign.com/product-category/board-games/), all verified in stock November 16, 2026.

- **Apples to Apples Bible Edition** ($30, 4-10 players, ages 8+): the judge flips a description card, everyone submits their best-fit Bible person, place, or verse card. The funniest entry usually wins, which is the point.
- **Bible Outburst** ($30): shout out the ten plagues, the twelve disciples, the fruits of the Spirit against a timer. The best pick for big family gatherings where grandparents want in.
- **Taboo Bible Edition** ($30, two teams, ages 10+): describe "Goliath" without saying giant, David, stone, Philistine, or sling. Worth knowing for Christmas shopping: Christianbook showed this backordered until about December 15 when we checked, while the publisher's own store had it ready now.
- **Scattergories Bible Edition** ($30): roll a letter, race to fill Bible categories starting with it.
- **Operation: Noah's Ark Edition** ($30): the steady-hand tweezers game, and the one on this list a four-year-old can genuinely play.

A money tip from their store: Cactus sells damaged-box copies of several titles for $16-18, same game inside.

## The Family Shelf

**Bibleopoly** (around $25 at Walmart as of November 2026, 2-6 players, ages 8+): the Monopoly-style board where you win by building a church in a Bible city, not by bankrupting your family, which fixes Monopoly's actual problem. One honest caveat from our check: the publisher's own site no longer lists it and several indie shops show it sold out, so big-box stock may be the tail end of its print life. If your family wants it, don't wait a year.

**Books of the Bible Game** (Not Consumed, $21.49 at Christianbook, ages 5+): 75 cards, one per book plus instructions, carrying thirteen different games in one deck, from Bible War to Slap It. Kids learn the order and themes of all 66 books by playing, in under 15 minutes a round, and it's the best genuinely kid-first product we verified. It was backordered to about December 15 at Christianbook when we checked; the maker's own store at notconsumed.com also carries it.

## The New Blood (2025-2026)

The newest verified releases come from Doxa Games, a Kickstarter-funded trio that actually shipped and was all in stock at [doxagames.com](https://doxagames.com/) on November 16, 2026: **Bible Clash: Gospels** ($12.99), a Top-Trumps-style head-to-head where Bible characters match up on Faith, Wisdom, Courage, Obedience, Service, and Strategy; **Trials & Triumphs** ($26.99), a family path game where abiding Grapevines (John 15:5) move you forward and Cracked Paths set you back; and **Hurry! Fleeing Disciples** ($34.99), set in Acts, guiding disciples past Roman guards to hidden house churches. Publisher-direct only; there's a bundle, and a Black Friday code was advertised when we looked.

## For Serious Board Gamers

**Deliverance** (Lowen Games, $89 at [playdeliverance.com](https://playdeliverance.com/shop/), listed as pre-order in a reprint cycle; Noble Knight had the Deluxe Edition physically in stock): a cooperative tactical campaign game where 1-4 players are angelic warriors defending a town from demonic forces. It earned mainstream hobby-press reviews, which almost nothing in this niche does. The spiritual-warfare theme makes it a teens-and-up table, not a kids' game.

**Ezra and Nehemiah** (Garphill Games/Renegade, MSRP $60, 1-4 players, ~2 hours): a genuine heavyweight euro about rebuilding Jerusalem's walls and temple after the exile, from the designers serious gamers already respect. Fair framing: it's a biblical-history strategy game from a secular publisher, not a ministry product, and it's the crossover pick for the board-game-club friend. Stock was patchy at our check, with copies at $42-60 across hobby retailers.

## The Dead List: Games You Can No Longer Buy (New)

Verified November 16, 2026, because old lists still recommend all of these:

- **The Settlers of Canaan**, the licensed Bible version of Catan, got one printing and is long out of print; used copies have reached $170. A recommendation to buy it is a recommendation to start a collector hunt.
- **Commissioned**, the cooperative early-church game and arguably the best-reviewed Christian board game ever made: out of print, and its publisher's website now fails to load, which suggests Chara Games is gone (no closure announcement exists, so we'll say "appears inactive"). The honest path: Noble Knight had used copies at $58.50-72.00 by condition when we checked. If you find one under $60, that's still a good game night.
- **Wisdom of Solomon**: the publisher's domain is dead; one Canadian shop and used listings remain.
- **Proverbial Wisdom Bible Edition**: liquidation listings only.

Also, two names that confuse searches: Kingdom Builder and Ark Nova are excellent secular games with no Bible connection, whatever the titles imply.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best Christian board game for families?

For mixed ages in 2026: Bible Outburst or Apples to Apples Bible Edition for groups, Books of the Bible Game for the 5-10 crowd (it teaches while it plays), and Bibleopoly if Monopoly is your family's language. All were verified purchasable in November 2026, which is the question most lists forget to ask.

### Are there Christian board games for young kids?

Operation: Noah's Ark Edition plays from preschool steadiness up, and the Books of the Bible Game is built for ages 5+ with rounds under 15 minutes. For the youngest kids, pairing a simple table game with a playable Bible story in an app beats any single product; that's the shape of family night we'd argue for.

### Is The Settlers of Canaan still available?

Not new. It had a single print run and is out of print; used copies command collector prices (we saw $170). If your family loves Catan, the honest play is regular Catan plus one of the verified-in-print games above, or hunting a used Commissioned copy, which holds up better anyway.

### Where should I buy Christian board games?

For the classic Bible editions, the publisher Cactus Game Design sells direct and had everything in stock at our check, while Christianbook showed Christmas-season backorders (around December 15 restocks on two titles we checked). For the hobby titles, Noble Knight and the price-comparison engines find live stock. Verify the page before you plan a gift around it; this niche's stock moves fast.
