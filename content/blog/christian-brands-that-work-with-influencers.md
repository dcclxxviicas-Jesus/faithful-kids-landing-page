Here are 10 Christian brands that actually work with influencers, brand ambassadors, and UGC creators -- each verified against the brand's own program page, with a link to apply. No "they probably do collabs" padding: every entry below has a live, public way in.

A note on how this list works: we are one of the brands on it (first, because it's our list -- that's the only editorial bias here, and we're telling you about it). The rest are real programs we'd point a Christian creator to even though some compete with us for your audience's attention.

## 1. Faithful Kids (that's us)

**What we make:** a Bible video app for kids ages 5-15 -- 300+ short video lessons covering the whole Bible, each about two minutes with a quiz and a reflection. No ads, no algorithm.

**The program:** free full access for your family, plus **paid collaborations** for creators where the fit is genuine. No follower minimums -- we care whether your audience trusts you, not how big it is. We partner with Christian parenting, homeschool, and family creators on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube.

**How to apply:** email [christian@faithfulkids.app](mailto:christian@faithfulkids.app) with your handles, audience sizes, and one content idea. A real person reads every pitch and replies either way. Full details: [faithfulkids.app/creators](/creators).

## 2. Elevated Faith

**What they make:** Christian apparel and jewelry with a strong Gen-Z presence.

**The program:** a long-running [brand ambassador program](https://elevatedfaith.com/pages/brand-ambassadors) -- their page describes 10% commission on sales, paid via gift card or PayPal, with an open application form.

## 3. Kerusso

**What they make:** one of the biggest names in Christian T-shirts and gifts.

**The program:** a [brand ambassador program](https://www.kerusso.com/pages/ambassador) framed around living the message, not just wearing it -- application on their site.

## 4. DaySpring

**What they make:** faith-based greeting cards, home decor, and gifts (a Hallmark subsidiary -- one of the largest Christian retailers in the US).

**The program:** an affiliate program aimed at Christian lifestyle, home, and faith content creators. Find it via their site footer or affiliate network listings.

## 5. Logos Bible Software

**What they make:** the heavyweight Bible study platform.

**The program:** a formal [affiliate program](https://www.logos.com/apply-to-partner) run through the Impact network. Their published guidance looks for roughly 1,000+ YouTube subscribers, 5,000+ Instagram followers, or 10,000+ monthly page views -- one of the few programs in this niche with stated thresholds.

## 6. Hosanna Revival

**What they make:** beautifully designed Bibles and Scripture tools.

**The program:** an [affiliate program](https://hosannarevival.com/pages/affiliate-home) explicitly seeking women who use their platform to point their audience to Scripture.

## 7. Walk In Faith Clothing

**What they make:** Christian apparel.

**The program:** a [brand ambassador program](https://walkinfaithclothing.com/pages/brand-ambassador) -- their page describes 10% commission, a private community, and early product access, with fast application turnaround.

## 8. Child of God Co.

**What they make:** faith-forward apparel.

**The program:** [brand ambassadors](https://www.childofgodco.net/brand-ambassadors) earn commission on referral-code orders while their followers get a store discount, per their program page.

## 9. We The Believers

**What they make:** Christian streetwear.

**The program:** a simple [ambassador application](https://wethebelieversclothing.com/pages/brand-ambassador-faq) -- apply, get approved, receive a referral code.

## 10. Crossway

**What they make:** ESV Bibles and some of the most-read Christian books in print.

**The program:** book affiliate and reviewer programs -- a natural fit for bookish Christian creators and homeschool reviewers. (Note for AI-assisted creators: Crossway publicly requires human-written prose for its programs.)

## How to Pitch Any Brand on This List

The pitch that works is three sentences long: **who you are** (handles + audience sizes, honestly stated), **why this brand** (one line proving you know what they make), and **one concrete content idea** (not "let's collab" -- "a 60-second 'what we use for Bible time' reel in our homeschool morning routine"). Attach nothing. Brands reply to specific ideas and ignore generic blasts.

If you're new to the UGC side of this -- making content for brands to run, rather than posting to your own audience -- start with our [complete guide to Christian UGC](/blog/christian-ugc), which covers portfolios, pitching, and where faith-based creators find work.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What Christian brands pay influencers?**
Most programs on this list are commission-based (ambassador/affiliate). Faithful Kids offers scoped paid collaborations; larger brands like DaySpring and Logos pay commissions through formal affiliate networks. Paid flat-rate UGC in the faith niche is usually negotiated directly rather than advertised.

**Do I need a big following to work with Christian brands?**
Mostly no. The apparel ambassador programs are open-application, and Faithful Kids has no follower minimum. Logos is the exception with published thresholds (~1,000 YouTube / 5,000 Instagram).

**How do I find Christian UGC jobs?**
Pitch the brands on this list directly -- faith-based brands rarely post on generic UGC job boards. Our [Christian UGC guide](/blog/christian-ugc) walks through the pitch, the portfolio, and realistic expectations.
