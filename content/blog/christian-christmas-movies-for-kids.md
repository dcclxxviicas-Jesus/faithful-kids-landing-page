Finding a Christmas movie that actually tells the Christmas story, and that's right for your kids' ages, gets harder every year, because the streaming services keep shuffling titles. So every movie below was checked for where it streams in the US on November 16 and 17, 2026, with age guidance from Common Sense Media and Focus on the Family's Plugged In.

Two dates to know before you plan movie night:

- **The Star leaves Peacock on December 1.**
- **The Nativity Story leaves Prime Video on January 1.**

## The Short Version

- **Ages 3 and up:** A Charlie Brown Christmas, which is **free for everyone on Apple TV December 12 and 13**, plus the VeggieTales Christmas specials.
- **Ages 6 and up:** The Star (on Peacock until December 1) and The Best Christmas Pageant Ever (**new on Netflix**).
- **Ages 8 and up:** Journey to Bethlehem (Hulu), a live-action musical.
- **Ages 10 and up:** The Nativity Story (Prime Video until January 1).
- **Free with no subscription:** Minno's Laugh and Grow Christmas special, Superbook's "The First Christmas" in the free Superbook Kids app, and the Chosen Christmas specials in The Chosen app.

## For Little Ones (Ages 3 to 6)

**1. A Charlie Brown Christmas** (1965, G, 25 minutes). Charlie Brown goes looking for the meaning of Christmas, and Linus answers by reciting Luke 2 on a bare stage. It's the most-watched Nativity reading in history. Common Sense says 3 and up. **Where:** Apple TV, and **free for everyone on December 12 and 13, 2026**.

**2. VeggieTales: The Toy That Saved Christmas** (1996, G, 33 minutes). A toy escapes the factory to find out what Christmas is really about. **Where:** the Yippee Kids TV channel on Amazon, or rent for $5.99. Note that it isn't on Minno's VeggieTales list.

**3. VeggieTales: The Star of Christmas** (2002, G, 48 minutes). Two showmen discover the true star of Christmas. **Where:** Minno, or rent for $3.99.

**4. VeggieTales: Saint Nicholas, A Story of Joyful Giving** (2009, 46 minutes). The real Nicholas of Myra, and giving out of God's love; the best answer to "is Santa real?" we know of. **Where:** Minno, or rent for $3.99. Minno also streams *Merry Larry*, *The Best Christmas Gift* and *It's a Meaningful Life*, and lists *The Little Drummer Boy* as coming soon.

**5. Laugh and Grow Bible for Kids: Christmas Special** (Minno, 22 minutes). Phil Vischer's retelling of Jesus' birth with the Minno kids, for ages 4 to 8. **Where:** **free on Minno**, including an ASL version.

**6. The Little Drummer Boy** (1968, Rankin/Bass, 25 minutes). An orphan drummer's gift to the newborn Jesus, for all ages. **Where:** Paramount+ and Hulu, or rent for $3.99.

**7. Nestor, the Long-Eared Christmas Donkey** (1977, G, 25 minutes). A mocked little donkey carries Mary to Bethlehem. **Where:** it isn't streaming anywhere this year; rent it for $3.99.

## For Kids (Ages 6 to 10)

**8. The Star** (2017, PG, 86 minutes). Bo the donkey and the stable animals tell the first Christmas, in Sony's animated comedy. Common Sense says 6 and up. Plugged In calls it very mild, with a caution that chase scenes with a soldier and his attack dogs may scare the youngest. **Where:** **Peacock Premium, leaving December 1, 2026**; after that, rent for $3.99 on Amazon, Apple or Fandango.

**9. The Best Christmas Pageant Ever** (2024, PG, 99 minutes). The six worst-behaved kids in town take over the church Nativity play, and end up showing everyone what it means. Common Sense says 6 and up; Plugged In rates it light, noting mild language (including God's name misused) and kids smoking. **Where:** **newly on Netflix this year**, also on Starz, or rent for $3.99.

**10. Superbook: "The First Christmas"** (season 1, episode 8, 26 minutes, TV-Y). Superbook takes Chris and Joy back to Bethlehem; for ages 6 to 12. **Where:** **free in the Superbook Kids app**, and on Prime Video. CBN has also streamed it free on Christmas Day in past years.

**11. Papa Panov's Special Christmas** (2026, Angel, 19 minutes, TV-G). New this season: a Tolstoy-style parable of a cobbler who expects Christ to visit and instead gets three guests in need. **Where:** Angel, for members.

## For Older Kids (Ages 8 and Up)

**12. Journey to Bethlehem** (2023, PG, 99 minutes). A live-action musical of the Nativity with modern pop songs. Common Sense says 8 and up, because Herod's soldiers threaten and hurt people. **Where:** Hulu, or rent for $3.99.

**13. Christmas with The Chosen: Holy Night** (2023, 104 minutes). A remastered blend of The Chosen's Nativity episodes with live music. Plugged In rates it light, noting the shepherds' coarse campfire jokes; about 8 and up. **Where:** Prime Video, and listed in the free Chosen app.

**14. Christmas with The Chosen: The Messengers** (2021). Nativity drama plus Christian artists performing, family-safe. **Where:** not on paid streaming; a 39-minute cut is reported free in The Chosen app.

## For Tweens and Teens (Ages 10 and Up)

**15. The Nativity Story** (2006, PG, 101 minutes). The live-action story of Mary and Joseph, ending at the birth. It's the most faithful film telling, and Common Sense says **10 and up** for the slaughter of the innocents, a mentioned stoning and a painful labour. **Where:** **Prime Video, leaving January 1, 2027**, or rent for $5.99.

**Not for young kids:** Angel Studios' *Zero A.D.: The Birth of Christ* opens in theatres on December 11, 2026. Angel's own synopsis centres on Herod's slaughter of the innocents, and we found no rating beyond one theatre listing PG-13. Teens at most.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where can I watch The Star (2017) this Christmas?

On Peacock Premium until December 1, 2026. After that, rent it for $3.99 or buy it for $7.99 on Amazon, Apple or Fandango.

### Is The Best Christmas Pageant Ever on Netflix?

Yes. The 2024 film is on US Netflix this season, as well as on Starz, and it rents for $3.99.

### What Christian Christmas movies are free to watch?

Minno's Laugh and Grow Bible Christmas Special is free on Minno, Superbook's "The First Christmas" is free in the Superbook Kids app, the Chosen Christmas specials are in the free Chosen app, and A Charlie Brown Christmas is free on Apple TV on December 12 and 13, 2026.

### What is the best Nativity movie for young kids?

For ages 3 to 6, A Charlie Brown Christmas and the VeggieTales specials. For 6 and up, The Star, an animated, gentle retelling (Common Sense says 6+). The live-action Nativity Story is best saved for 10 and up.

### Is The Nativity Story appropriate for kids?

Common Sense Media recommends it for ages 10 and up, because it depicts the slaughter of the innocents, mentions a stoning and shows a painful labour. For younger kids, choose The Star or Journey to Bethlehem (8+).
