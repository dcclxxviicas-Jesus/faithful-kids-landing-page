Search for the best Christian clothing brands and the top results are mostly lists written by clothing brands about themselves, which is how dead names keep getting recommended. Not of This World, the brand most of us grew up wearing to youth group, no longer has a working store. Neither does its parent chain, C28. Sevenly now redirects to a wellness company.

So we checked every brand below on its own store on November 16, 2026: that it's selling, what a tee actually costs today, whether there's a kids line, and what (if anything) it gives away, in its own words. Prices are in-stock adult tees as listed that day, and several stores were running holiday sales, so expect small differences.

## The Short Version

- **Streetwear:** Elevated Faith, Used By God, Thank God.
- **Classic scripture tees at the lowest prices:** Kerusso.
- **Women's, soft and minimalist:** Elly & Grace, Dear Heart Designs.
- **Theology on a t-shirt:** FaithWise + Co.
- **The gym:** Saint Kaizen, HolStrength, SAVD.
- **Biggest kids ranges:** Kerusso Kidz, SAVD, Elly & Grace. Our separate guide to [Christian clothing for kids](/blog/christian-clothing-for-kids) covers the kids brands in depth.

## 1. Elevated Faith

The biggest name in modern Christian streetwear: unisex tees, hoodies and matching sets, plus jewelry. Tees run about $10 to $50, most around $40 (The Cross Heavyweight Tee was $44.99). There's a dedicated kids section. **Giving:** "A portion of every order goes to organizations like Charity: Water, Compassion International" and others, per its site. [elevatedfaith.com](https://elevatedfaith.com)

## 2. Kerusso

The classic: scripture and gentle-humour tees, hats and gifts, and still the best value on this list, with most tees about $22 on sale (the Kerusso John 3:16 tee was $22.49, regularly $29.99). Kerusso also runs sub-brands: **grace & truth** for women, **HOLD FAST** for men, and **Kerusso Kidz**. Its tagline: "Christian Apparel & Gifts Designed to Share the Good News." [kerusso.com](https://www.kerusso.com)

## 3. Elly & Grace

Mostly women's and unisex tees with a warm farmhouse feel, plus a men's line called Anchored and sizes from baby through youth (the Unto Us youth shirt, for instance). Most tees about $25 (As For Me And My House Unisex Shirt, $24.99). "We are on a mission to help YOU share your faith." [ellyandgrace.com](https://www.ellyandgrace.com)

## 4. Used By God (UBG)

Christian streetwear: tees, joggers, hoodies and hats, plus a UBG Kids line. Tees about $14 to $26 (God's Plan My Plan Original Tee, $25.99), though only a handful of adult tees were in stock on the day we checked. UBG says it supports nonprofit organizations worldwide but doesn't name a partner or amount. [ubgclothing.com](https://ubgclothing.com)

## 5. Dear Heart Designs

Soft, minimalist, women's-leaning tees printed in Texas, with a men's collection, jewelry and even Bibles, plus DH Kids and tween and teen bundles. Tees about $22 to $37, most around $34 (Fix Your Eyes Tee). "We want to create conversations about Jesus." [dearheartdesigns.com](https://dearheartdesigns.com)

## 6. FaithWise + Co

Graphic tees that range from theological to witty, and sweatshirts. Tees about $32 to $44 (Hope Dealer T-Shirt, $34.99). No kids section. **Giving:** "we give 10% of profits to trusted Christian organizations," per its site. [faithwiseco.com](https://faithwiseco.com)

## 7. Thank God

Oversized, vintage-wash streetwear and camo, every tee $35 (the Tetelestai Oversized Vintage Tee, for example). **Giving:** "A portion of every purchase directly supports their mission," the mission being the anti-trafficking ministry Exodus Cry. [thankgod.com](https://thankgod.com)

## 8. Child of God Co.

Faith apparel with a strong ambassador community, most pieces about $30 to $55 on its store today. Note the domain: the live store is **childofgodco.net**; the .com is not theirs. If you're a creator, its ambassador program is covered in our [Christian brands that work with influencers](/blog/christian-brands-that-work-with-influencers) guide. [childofgodco.net](https://www.childofgodco.net)

## 9. Saint Kaizen

Christian gymwear from Ireland, founded in 2022: tees, shorts, hoodies and a women's range, plus youth performance shorts. Most tees $42 (Christ is King T-Shirt). Its aim, in its words: "spread the word of Christ through our brand." [saintkaizen.com](https://saintkaizen.com)

## 10. HolStrength

Christian athletic and streetwear for men and women, with youth tees. Tees about $18 to $40 (In God We Trust Premium Oversized Tee, $34). **Giving:** "50% of sales every Veterans Day is donated," plus a monthly 20% charity day. [holstrength.com](https://holstrength.com)

## 11. SAVD

Faith-based premium activewear: men's and women's oversized tees and performance shorts, and the largest kids range of the gym brands (boys' and girls' shorts, hoodies and youth sets). Tees about $33 to $45 ('The King Is Born' Men's Oversized T-Shirt, $40). No giving program stated. [savdclothing.com](https://savdclothing.com)

## 12. The Daily Grace Co.

Best known for women's Bible studies, but its small run of tees is the cheapest on this list after Kerusso, about $17 (Give Me Jesus Tee, $16.99). A good add-on if you're already ordering a study. [thedailygraceco.com](https://thedailygraceco.com)

**Also worth knowing:** Hosanna Revival, famous for its Bible covers, sells a few sweatshirts (about $45) but no tees, and Rodeo in the Sky makes western hats and tees with verse tags.

## Christian Clothing for Men

The men's picks, all verified selling: Kerusso's **HOLD FAST** line (the Fear Not Trust God tee was $22.49), Dear Heart's men's collection, Elly & Grace's **Anchored** line, Used By God, Thank God, and for the gym, Saint Kaizen, HolStrength and SAVD.

## Christian Gym Clothing

Three brands are actually making Christian gymwear right now, each with current stock: **Saint Kaizen** (about $42 tees, Irish), **HolStrength** (about $18 to $40, with charity days) and **SAVD** (about $33 to $45, with the biggest youth range). Plenty of other "Christian athletic" names in older lists had no working store when we checked.

## Brands That Closed or Changed

Older lists still recommend these. What we found on November 16, 2026:

- **Not of This World (NOTW):** its site no longer runs a store; the domain shows only a parked redirect.
- **C28:** the retail chain that owned NOTW is gone; c28.com now points to a domain-holding page.
- **Sevenly:** redirects to an unrelated wellness-wear company.
- **Active Faith Sports:** the store shows "This store is unavailable."
- **316Tees:** the domain no longer resolves.
- **God The Father Apparel:** the site is up, but every tee is sold out and nothing new has been added since December 2023.
- **Equris Clothing:** online but dormant, with nothing new since February 2023.
- **Walk In Faith Apparel:** shows "Launching Soon."
- **Not Yet Home and Upperroom**, both featured in a 2025 roundup of Christian fashion, no longer use faith language on their sites.

And a note on slogans: "Faith Over Fear" and "Jesus Is King" aren't brands. Dozens of print-on-demand sellers use them, so check who's actually behind a listing before you buy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best Christian clothing brands?

Verified selling in November 2026: Elevated Faith and Used By God for streetwear, Kerusso for classic scripture tees at the lowest prices (about $22), Elly & Grace and Dear Heart Designs for soft women's tees, FaithWise + Co for theology tees, Thank God for oversized vintage styles, and Saint Kaizen, HolStrength and SAVD for the gym.

### What happened to Not of This World clothing?

NOTW's own site no longer runs a store, and C28, the Christian retail chain that owned it, is gone too; c28.com now points to a domain-holding page. If you loved the style, Elevated Faith and Used By God are the closest active streetwear brands.

### Which Christian clothing brands give back?

In their own words, as of November 2026: Elevated Faith gives a portion of every order to groups including Charity: Water and Compassion International; FaithWise + Co gives 10% of profits; Thank God supports Exodus Cry; and HolStrength donates 50% of Veterans Day sales plus a monthly 20% charity day.

### Are there Christian clothing brands for kids?

Yes. Kerusso Kidz, Elevated Faith's kids section, Elly & Grace (baby to youth), Dear Heart's DH Kids, UBG Kids and SAVD's youth range all had stock in November 2026. Our [Christian clothing for kids](/blog/christian-clothing-for-kids) guide covers them, plus the brands that make only kids' clothes.

### What is the most affordable Christian clothing brand?

Kerusso, with most tees about $22 on sale, followed by The Daily Grace Co.'s tees at about $17 and Used By God at about $14 to $26. Most streetwear brands run $35 to $45 a tee.
