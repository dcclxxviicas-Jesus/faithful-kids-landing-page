Shopping for Christian clothes for kids is harder than it should be. Search results are mostly single products on Etsy and print-on-demand listings, and several brands that older guides recommend have quietly closed their stores. Tough Christian, Maximus Grace and Kingdom Kids all now show a "store unavailable" page.

So we checked every brand below on its own store on November 16, 2026: which sizes it actually lists, what one real item costs today, what it says about fabric, and whether parents can match their kids. Prices are as listed that day, and a few stores were running holiday sales.

[holypals.com](https://holypals.com)

One shopping tip: many search results for Christian baby pajamas point to a different reseller site rather than Holy Pals' own store. Buy from holypals.com.

## A Note on Pajamas and Safety

Under US Consumer Product Safety Commission rules, children's sleepwear in sizes above 9 months must either be flame-resistant or fit snugly, which is why the bamboo pajamas above are cut close to the body and say so. If a pajama listing doesn't say "snug fit" or describe its fabric, ask the seller before buying for a baby or toddler.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where can I buy Christian baby clothes?

Verified in November 2026: Holy Pals for Bible-story pajamas and onesies (newborn to 24 months, from $34), Elly & Grace for infant bodysuits (from $20), That's Wholesome for onesies and rompers ($25), GraceThread for onesies ($22.95), and Mud Pie for baptism outfits.

Kingdom Buddies makes nothing but toddler tees (2T to 5T, 280+ designs). Kerusso Kidz is the cheapest at about $17, and Elly & Grace, That's Wholesome and Elevated Faith Kids all cover 2T to 5T too.

Yes. Holy Pals sells matching family pajamas (men's and women's sets alongside the kids'), Elly & Grace's Christmas Crew design comes in family sizes, That's Wholesome sells its designs in adult sizes, and The Little Rose Shop's Divine Mercy tee runs from baby to adult 4XL.

### What Christian clothing is good for older kids?

UBG Kids (youth XS to XL), Dear Heart Designs (youth 6 to 14), Kerusso Kidz (youth XS to L), and Elevated Faith Kids for hoodies up to 11/12Y. For teens who've outgrown kids sizes, see our [best Christian clothing brands](/blog/christian-clothing-brands) guide for the adult lines.
