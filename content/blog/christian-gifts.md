## What Changed Since Last Year's Gift Guides

- **DaySpring** is no longer a standalone store: dayspring.com now redirects to Hallmark.com, and in January 2026 HarperCollins Christian Publishing bought DaySpring's books, journals and devotionals, which continue under the DaySpring name. Hallmark kept the cards.
- **Lifeway** sells online only; its retail stores closed in 2019.
- **The "NIV Adorned" Bible** that some guides recommend doesn't appear to exist; the closest real line is the NIV Artisan Collection (#3).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a good Christian gift for someone who has everything?

A personalized Bible (Lifeway imprints a name for $6.99), a five-year prayer journal like One Thing I Ask ($49.90), or an experience such as a Museum of the Bible membership ($60 and up). Things they'll use every day beat things they'll display.

### What are good Christian gifts under $25?

From this list, all verified in November 2026: the New to the Bible Toolkit ($7.90), Advent Storybook ($9.99), Bible Recap Journal ($10.76), DaySpring perpetual calendar ($10.99), Spirit of God Story Cards ($11), Jesus Storybook Bible ($12.99), Theologian Mug ($16.99), Joy of Every Longing Heart ($16.99), Hosanna Revival notebook ($17.90), and Jesus Calling ($23.92).

### What is a good Christian gift for a pastor?

A quality preaching Bible (the ESV Preaching Bible runs $167.99 to $249.99), a Logos gift card so they can choose their own resources, or something small for the whole staff like the Theologian Mug ($16.99). Most pastors will tell you a handwritten note matters more than any of it.

### What is a good Christian gift for kids?

The Jesus Storybook Bible for young children, The Action Bible for 8 to 12 year olds, and a year of a Bible app they'll actually use, like Faithful Kids. Our [Christian gifts for kids](/blog/christian-gifts-for-kids) guide sorts dozens more by age.
