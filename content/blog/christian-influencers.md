## Bible Teaching

**1. BibleProject** ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@BibleProject)). The animated Bible explainers that half of youth ministry runs on: 5.51 million YouTube subscribers and 418K on TikTok, all free from a nonprofit. Recently posted a behind-the-scenes on its *Spirit of God* series.

**2. Tara-Leigh Cobble, The Bible Recap** ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@TheBibleRecap)). Daily recaps of a chronological Bible-reading plan: 517K on YouTube and about 469K on The Bible Recap's Instagram, plus 214K on her own.

**3. Jackie Hill Perry** ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@jackiehillperrychannel)). Bible teaching, spoken word and music, with 2 million on Instagram and 485K on YouTube; she released "Ascension" this month.

**4. Jackie and Preston Perry, With The Perrys** ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@WithThePerrys)). The couple's talk show on faith and marriage, 504K subscribers and 575 videos.

**5. John Mark Comer** ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@practicingtheway)). Spiritual formation and the rule of life, 290K on Instagram, and a new full documentary, *The case for quiet*.

## Apologetics and Hard Questions

**6. Mike Winger** ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@MikeWinger)). Long-form Bible Q&A whose motto is to "think biblically about everything": 1.03 million subscribers, posting weekly.

**7. Allie Beth Stuckey** ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@AllieBethStuckey)). Host of BlazeTV's *Relatable*, on culture, news and theology: 756K on YouTube and 947K on Instagram, with an episode out the day we checked.
