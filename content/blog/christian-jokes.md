## Church Life Jokes

The sanctuary, the fellowship hall, and the eternal mystery of who signed up to bring napkins.

1. What do you call a church potluck with no casseroles? **A test of faith.**
2. Why does fellowship-hall coffee taste like that? **It's set apart. Nobody knows for what.**
3. Why did the worship team start the service exactly on time? **Nobody knows. Scholars are still studying it.**
4. What's the most competitive sport in church? **Racing to the parking lot after the benediction.**
5. How can you spot a first-time visitor? **They sat in the front row on purpose.**
6. What did the usher say to the family arriving twenty minutes late? **"Welcome back! Your usual seats are ready."**
7. Why do church mice never go hungry? **Cheerios under every pew by the second service.**
8. What's the longest sixty seconds in the world? **The "turn and greet your neighbor" minute.**
9. Why did the sound guy get a standing ovation? **The microphone worked two Sundays in a row.**
10. What do you call the coffee line after the service? **Second fellowship.**
11. How many committee members does it take to change a lightbulb? **We'll know after next month's meeting about scheduling the meeting.**
12. What is a potluck casserole's greatest fear? **Being set down next to Sister Margaret's fried chicken.**
13. Why was the church softball team so spiritual? **More sacrifices than runs.**
14. What's the hardest instrument to play in church? **Second fiddle.**
15. What happens after the pastor says "in closing"? **Nothing. Nothing closes.**

## Pastor Jokes

Told with love, usually by the pastor.

16. What are a pastor's three favorite words? **"In closing" and "finally."**
17. Why did the pastor bring sermon notes to the pulpit? **So the congregation could watch him ignore them.**
18. How did the member compliment the sermon? **"It was like the peace of God -- it passed all understanding and endured forever."**
19. What's a pastor's favorite kind of coffee? **Holy grounds.**
20. Why do pastors make terrible poker players? **Every face they make is a shepherd's warning.**
21. What did the pastor say when the projector died mid-sermon? **"Clearly the Lord wants you looking at your Bibles."**
22. Why did the pastor love the church's old van? **It ran on a wing and a prayer, which was also the maintenance plan.**
23. What's the difference between a long sermon and a short one? **About four "in conclusions."**
24. Why don't pastors need an alarm clock on Mondays? **The deacon board provides the wake-up call.**
25. How does a pastor measure a successful men's breakfast? **Nobody quoted the attendance numbers at him while he was eating.**
26. What did the pastor say to the man who slept through every sermon? **"I appreciate your trust."**
27. Why did the visiting preacher get invited back? **He finished early. Twice.**

## Kids in Church

The most reliable comedians in any congregation. All of these have happened somewhere.

28. A boy told the pastor after the service, "When I grow up, I'm going to give you some money." "Well, thank you," said the pastor, "but why?" **"Because my dad says you're one of the poorest preachers we've ever had."**
29. The teacher asked, "Why do we have to be quiet in church?" A girl in the back answered: **"Because people are sleeping."**
30. A kindergartner was drawing furiously in Sunday school. "I'm drawing God," she said. The teacher gently noted that nobody knows what God looks like. **"They will in a minute."**
31. One bedtime prayer, overheard: **"Dear God, thank You for the baby brother. But what I prayed for was a puppy."**
32. "Why does Grandma read her Bible so much?" a boy asked. His dad: **"She's cramming for her final exam."**
33. Another overheard prayer: **"Lord, if You can't make me a better boy, don't worry about it. I'm having a real good time the way I am."**
34. A mom asked what her son learned in Sunday school. "Moses got the people out of Egypt, built a pontoon bridge over the Red Sea, and radioed for air support when the tanks came." "Is that really how they taught it?" **"No, but if I told you what they really said, you'd never believe it."**
35. Heard during the Lord's Prayer, from the kids' row: **"Give us this day our jelly bread."**
36. Also heard: **"And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us some email."**
37. And the classic: **"Our Father, who art in heaven, Harold be Thy name."**
38. A girl asked her mom why she bowed her head before dinner. "I'm asking God to bless our food." **"Doesn't He already know what's in the casserole?"**
39. One more prayer, from a five-year-old at Christmas: **"And please put the vitamins back in the candy, where they belong."**

## Christian Dad Jokes

The thermostat ministry has a sense of humor.

52. How do we know God has a sense of humor? **He made the platypus on purpose.**
53. Why can't you argue with someone reading Proverbs? **They've always got a wise crack.**
54. What's a Christian dad's favorite hymn? **Whichever one is playing when the roast timer goes off.**
55. What's a dad's true spiritual gift? **The thermostat ministry. Guarding it is a calling.**
56. Why do Christian dads love the GPS voice? **It's the closest thing to a still, small voice that recalculates.**
57. What did dad say when he was asked to lead the church grill team? **"Let us meat together."**
58. Why did dad volunteer for the sound booth? **It's the only seat in church where sleeping looks like concentrating.**
59. What's a dad's favorite verse during a long car trip? **"Be still."**
60. Why did dad highlight the whole family Bible yellow? **He said every part was the good part.**
61. What did dad call the leftover potluck plate he brought home? **A love offering.**
62. Why does dad always drive the church van on youth trips? **Because he who is faithful with little will be faithful with much -- and the van is both.**
63. What does a Christian dad say when the offering plate reaches his row? **"Kids, this is where your allowance goes to be with the Lord."**

## Is It Okay to Joke About Church?

Yes -- and the jokes on this page are built on that line being real. There's a difference between laughing at church culture (potlucks, long sermons, the thermostat wars) and mocking what the church believes. Proverbs 17:22 says a cheerful heart is good medicine, and congregations have been telling these jokes about themselves for generations; the pastor usually tells the pastor jokes best. What you won't find here is anything that makes God, Scripture, or anyone's sincere faith the punchline.

That's also why this humor works so well with kids. A kid who laughs at "Harold be Thy name" has just learned the real words, and a family that can laugh together at the drive home after church is a family where church feels like home too.
