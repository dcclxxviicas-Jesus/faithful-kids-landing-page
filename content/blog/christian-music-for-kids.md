Christian music for kids has changed more than most parents realize. Several beloved kids' worship lines haven't released anything in years, a couple of brands quietly renamed, and the most-played kids' act on Spotify is one a lot of parents last heard on cassette. So every artist below was checked on November 16, 2026: monthly listeners as Spotify shows them, subscribers on their official YouTube channel, and their latest release date, so you know which ones are still making new music.

## The Short Version

- **For memorizing Scripture:** Seeds Kids Worship, Slugs & Bugs, The Rizers.
- **For toddlers and preschoolers:** Cedarmont Kids, Go Fish's *Bible Beats*, Wee Sing Bible Songs.
- **For kids' ministry energy:** Yancy, Shout Praises Kids, Hillsong Kids.
- **For the whole family:** Rain for Roots, Ellie Holcomb's *Sing* series.
- **One playlist to start with:** Spotify's own "Christian & Gospel Kids," saved by about 153,000 people.

## Scripture-Memory Songs

**1. Seeds Kids Worship** (formerly Seeds Family Worship). Scripture set to music word for word from the ESV, with the reference in every song title, plus hand-motion videos and lullabies. Still releasing: its newest single, "The Only True God (John 17:3 ESV)," came out November 6, 2026. About 68,000 monthly listeners. If you want your kids memorizing verses without trying, start here. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/5kV2pC0biCYFh3y9HxgNkS) · [seedskidsworship.com](https://seedskidsworship.com)

**2. Slugs & Bugs** (Randall Goodgame). "Word-for-word scripture songs" alongside gloriously silly ones, made to "delight the whole family." Very active: *Sunday Songs* came out in January 2026 with a deluxe edition in July, plus *Sing the Bible*. About 94,000 monthly listeners. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/5OqR2VGSqk60tMFN8eINMN) · [slugsandbugs.com](https://slugsandbugs.com)

**3. The Rizers.** The name is short for "memorizers": upbeat songs titled by verse, like "Proverbs 3:5-6." There's been nothing new since 2011, but the albums hold up and the YouTube channel has about 79,000 subscribers. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/44Jojk3HGoi3Rwg4iA049d)

## Toddlers and Preschoolers

**4. Cedarmont Kids.** The surprise of this list: classic Sunday-school songs in simple arrangements, and by far the biggest kids' act we checked, with about **586,000 monthly Spotify listeners and 2.86 million YouTube subscribers**. Start with *Toddler Bible Songs*. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/0MCU2OpgnSB7rm5UPUMHgt)

**5. Go Fish.** The a cappella group turned preschool Bible-song makers: *Bible Beats (For Little Feets)* and its sequels, with a new single in March 2026. About 110,000 monthly listeners. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/60oY0lmnOyCS2ElXAKCNrR)

**6. Wee Sing Bible Songs.** The long-running kids' music brand's two Bible Songs volumes. Wee Sing itself is mostly secular, so look for those two titles specifically. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/4lzlU08DosVqrpEoOvf3DQ)

## Kids' Worship With Energy

**7. Shout Praises Kids** (Integrity Music). "Worship music for kids, by kids!" Kids sing every track, it's been running for over 20 years, and it's huge on YouTube, with **1.28 million subscribers**. Its newest is a Spanish album, *Buen Dios* (2025). [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/0SKVoWXola9WXgw3PwMYpE)

**8. Yancy.** High-energy kids' ministry worship with motion videos, from a songwriter whose music is in thousands of children's ministries; her site splits music for young children from music for families. About 110,000 monthly listeners, with "What An Awesome God" out in April 2026. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/3hm9cA8c92CwJnCIaGqaEu) · [yancyministries.com](https://yancyministries.com)

**9. Hillsong Kids.** Big-church kids' worship (still called Hillsong Kids, despite rumours of a rename), now adding piano lullabies, and it released new music on October 23, 2026. About 101,000 monthly listeners and 278,000 on YouTube. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/6B9zcemB6pCVlbLac9pwWF)

**10. Lifeway Kids Worship.** The music behind Lifeway's VBS and curriculum, including *VBS 2027 Take Flight* (October 2026). The one to know if your church runs Lifeway VBS. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/6FIaadxhvDeZI4RAd06Dnx)

**11. Saddleback Kids Worship.** Saddleback Church's kids' ministry music, with *Side By Side* (2023) and a new single in July 2026. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/3JmOsw9Th41J8BDZ7QGM63)

## For the Whole Family

**12. Rain for Roots.** A folk collective including Sandra McCracken, making "singable scripture songs for kids and grown ups." *Waiting Songs* is one of the best Advent albums for families. Nothing new since 2020. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/0BMoQeAvgTIVa2il9WIGkA)

**13. Ellie Holcomb's *Sing* series.** Short albums paired with her children's books, from *Sing: Creation Songs* (2018) to *Sing: Spring Songs* (2025), plus a Christmas edition. Her listener count is mostly her adult music, but these EPs are made for kids. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/5hNiAUVPCTgcpy8vljCxzs)

**14. Rend Co. Kids.** Rend Collective's playful folk-pop for kids, *Sparkle. Pop. Rampage.* (2019). There's been nothing new since 2020, but it's still a car-ride favourite. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/5S1TlcaYEYdwtVb5ud3lPK)

**15. Forrest Frank.** Not a kids' act, but the most-played Christian pop artist right now (about 7 million monthly listeners), and his upbeat songs are the ones a lot of older kids are asking for. Preview before you hand over the playlist. More grown-up picks are in our [best Christian artists](/blog/christian-artists) guide. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/1scVfBymTr3CeZ4imMj1QJ)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best Christian music for kids?

For Scripture memory, Seeds Kids Worship and Slugs & Bugs. For toddlers, Cedarmont Kids, the most-played kids' act on Spotify at about 586,000 monthly listeners. For kids' worship, Shout Praises Kids, Yancy and Hillsong Kids, all checked in November 2026.

### What Christian music is good for toddlers?

Cedarmont Kids' *Toddler Bible Songs*, Go Fish's *Bible Beats (For Little Feets)*, Wee Sing Bible Songs, and Seeds Kids Worship's lullabies: simple melodies, short songs, lots of repetition.

### What are good Christian songs to help kids memorize Bible verses?

Seeds Kids Worship sets verses word for word from the ESV, with the reference in each title; Slugs & Bugs and The Rizers do the same in a sillier key. Sung verses are remembered far longer than spoken ones.

### Is there a Christian kids' playlist on Spotify?

Yes. Spotify's editorial "Christian & Gospel Kids" playlist had about 153,000 saves in November 2026, and Spotify also makes "This Is" playlists for Hillsong Kids, Seeds Kids Worship and Shout Praises Kids.
