Christian scholarship lists are full of awards that no longer exist. The best-known example: Thrivent's scholarships for graduating seniors ended in 2022, and lists still recommend them. Aggregator sites also routinely print deadlines that contradict the funders' own pages.

So every scholarship below was checked on the funder's own page on November 16, 2026. Where a funder hasn't posted its next date, we say "not posted yet" rather than guessing. Two dates are calculated from a funder's stated rule (like "third Monday of December"), and they're marked that way.

The honest shape of this market: **most Christian scholarships require membership**, either in a denomination or in a Catholic fraternal society. So start with the ones your family already belongs to.

## Open Now, or With a Posted Deadline

**1. WMU Foundation Ministry and Mission Scholarships** (Southern Baptist). For Southern Baptist students, including children of IMB and NAMB missionaries and those preparing for Baptist missions. The foundation is explicit that applicants with no current Southern Baptist connection "cannot be considered." **Deadline: December 31, 2026, 11:45 pm CST.** Apply online, with two endorsements: one from a WMU leader and one academic. [wmufoundation.com](https://wmufoundation.com/apply-for-scholarships/)

**2. Catholic Scouting America (NCCS) Scholarships.** 16 scholarships worth $2,000 to $5,000 ($43,500 in total) for practicing Catholic high school seniors who are active Scouts. You need Eagle, Summit or Quartermaster rank **plus** a Catholic religious emblem. **Deadline: March 1**, with the window open now. One application covers all of them. [catholicscoutingamerica.org](https://catholicscoutingamerica.org/college-scholarships/)

**3. Christian Missionary Scholarship Foundation.** For children of missionaries attending Calvin, Dordt, Hope, Kuyper or Wheaton, in any major. Awards vary and renew for up to four years; it gave $1.25 million to 127 students for 2026-27. **Deadline: February 1, 2027.** Both student and parent applications are required. [CMSF](https://cmsf.squarespace.com/criteria-eligibility)

**4. UCC Undergraduate Scholarship** (United Church of Christ). For UCC members of at least a year, studying any subject as a sophomore through senior at an accredited four-year US school. **Opens December 1, 2026 and closes March 1, 2027.** [ucc.org](https://www.ucc.org/giving/ways-we-give/scholarships-grants/leadership-development-scholarships/)

**5. University of Dallas Homeschool Scholarship** (Catholic). **$5,000 a year**, renewable for up to eight semesters, with two awards for homeschooled seniors (one from an accredited program, one non-accredited) with "strong commitments to faith, service and achievement." **Deadline: March 1**, for the fall 2027 class. Requires a 200-word essay, an admission application and the FAFSA. It's the only homeschool-specific award on this list. [udallas.edu](https://ugadmis.udallas.edu/register/2027HomeschoolScholarship)

**6. Disciples Home Missions Student Scholarships** (Christian Church, Disciples of Christ). For Disciples members preparing for ministry, with financial need considered. **Applications run January 2 to March 15, 2027**; references and transcripts are due April 30. **7. Leonard M. Perryman Communications Scholarship** (United Methodist Communications). **$2,500**, one award, for a United Methodist racial or ethnic minority junior or senior studying journalism or communications. **Deadline: March 15** each year. [resourceumc.org](https://www.resourceumc.org/en/content/leonard-m-perryman-communications-scholarship)

**8. Samuel Robinson Award** (Presbyterian Church USA). **Up to $2,000** for juniors and seniors at a Presbyterian-related college. You earn it by memorizing the Westminster Shorter Catechism. ## Ended, Renamed, or Not What It Looks Like

- **Thrivent scholarships: ended.** Thrivent's own page says its education fund awarded scholarships to seniors from 2013 to 2022, and since 2023 its grants go to other education efforts.
- **Foundation for College Christian Leaders: appears defunct.** Its site is empty, and its newest listings are for a 2021 deadline.
- **The Christian Connector "Christian College Scholarship" drawing: a marketing drawing, not a merit or need award.** You enter by requesting college information, and the schools you pick receive your contact details. It isn't a scam, but go in knowing that.
- **"National Presbyterian College Scholarship": an old name.** PC(USA)'s current program is the Presbyterian Scholarships for Undergraduates (#14).
- **Catholic United Financial** has merged into Trusted Fraternal Life (formerly Catholic Financial Life), so use #16's page.
- **Courage to Grow** often appears on Christian lists, but it has no faith component. It's a general $1,000 scholarship.

## How to Spot a Scholarship Scam

The Federal Trade Commission's red flags, from its consumer guide:

- "The scholarship is guaranteed or your money back."
- "I just need your credit card or bank account number to hold this scholarship."
- "We'll do all the work. You just pay a processing fee."
- You're told you're "a finalist" in a contest you never entered.

The FTC's rule of thumb: **a real scholarship never costs money to apply for or to receive.** The FAFSA is free, and you should never pay someone to file it or share your FSA ID. Report scams at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What scholarships are available for Christian students?

Verified in November 2026: denominational awards (WMU Foundation for Southern Baptists, UCC, PC(USA), ELCA women, United Methodist, American Baptist, Disciples of Christ), Catholic awards (Knights of Columbus, Catholic Scouting America, the Newman Guide contest, Catholic Order of Foresters), the Christian Missionary Scholarship Foundation for missionaries' children, and the University of Dallas Homeschool Scholarship.

### Are there Christian scholarships that don't require church membership?

A few. The Christian Missionary Scholarship Foundation (for missionaries' children), the University of Dallas Homeschool Scholarship, the Newman Guide contest (practicing Catholics) and the Anne Lowe Scholarship (legally blind students) don't require membership in a particular organization. Most others do.

### Are there scholarships for Christian homeschoolers?

Yes. The University of Dallas offers two $5,000-a-year renewable Homeschool Scholarships, due March 1. Many Christian colleges also run their own homeschool awards, so ask each school's admissions office.

### Does Thrivent still offer scholarships?

No. Thrivent's own page says its education fund gave scholarships to graduating seniors from 2013 to 2022 and has funded other education efforts since 2023.

### Are scholarships that ask for a fee legitimate?

No. The FTC says a real scholarship never charges to apply or to receive the money. A "drawing" that requires you to request college information isn't a scam, but it's a marketing program, not a merit or need award.
