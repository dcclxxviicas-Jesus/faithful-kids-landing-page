The pages that rank for Christian scholarships are aggregators, and aggregators have a problem: they pad their lists with their own sweepstakes, they go stale, and they sometimes get the numbers wrong. In researching this guide we found one listing a major drawing at half its actual amount, and conflicting deadlines for the same award six weeks apart.

So this guide was verified the slow way: every amount, deadline, and eligibility rule below was checked on the sponsor's own page in November 2026, with the lead-generation entries flagged as what they are. One finding shapes the whole list, so it goes first: **most real Christian scholarship money is denominational.** The fastest path to an award is not a national search; it is asking what your own church's denomination offers.

## The Deadline Closing First

**The WMU Foundation** (Woman's Missionary Union, in the Southern Baptist world) closes its scholarship applications **December 31 at 11:45 pm CST**. Three tracks: students preparing for ministry and missions, children of current IMB and NAMB missionaries, and current or former missionaries themselves. Their own page is blunt that applicants need a Southern Baptist connection, which is exactly the pattern of this whole list.

## Denominational Scholarships: Start With Your Own Church

**United Methodist**: the UM Higher Education Foundation awards over $2 million annually to students attending the 100+ United Methodist-related schools and seminaries. The 2027-2028 application opens **January 1, 2027**.

**American Baptist**: ABHMS awards $750-$1,500 typical scholarships (specialized funds run to $3,000) to active members of American Baptist churches with a 2.5+ GPA at any accredited US institution, renewable up to six years. The 2027-2028 window runs **January 15 to April 15, 2027**.

**Presbyterian (PC-USA)**: need-based undergraduate scholarships of **$4,000 up to need** for church members seeking a first degree at any accredited US college, with applications due in May. The same office runs the **Samuel Robinson Award**, the most distinctive entry on this page: up to $2,000, quarterly deadlines, for juniors and seniors at Presbyterian-related colleges who memorize and recite the Westminster Shorter Catechism and write an essay. Scripture memory as scholarship money.

**Disciples of Christ**: HELM's Leadership Fellows Program pays **$2,000 a year for four years** to students active in a Disciples congregation entering any full-time four-year degree, applications due March 15.

**Lutheran (ELCA)**: the Fund for Leaders is the largest single award here, **full seminary tuition** for students entering any of the seven ELCA seminaries on the path to rostered ministry, with roughly 30-35 full-tuition awards a year. Applications for the main track run **December 1, 2026 to February 19, 2027**. Graduate-level, and life-changing if that is your path.

**Fellowship of Evangelical Churches**: the Christian Service Foundation pays **$1,500 a year** to students from FEC churches at CCCU member colleges or accredited Bible colleges, reapplied annually.

## For Missionary Kids

The **Christian Missionary Scholarship Foundation** set aside $1.25 million for 2026-2027 and supports over 100 missionary kids a year with renewable four-year scholarships. Two things their page makes clear: applicants must be children of missionaries currently on or recently returned from the field, and the money travels only to their partner Christian colleges (Calvin, Dordt, Hope, Kuyper, Wheaton among them). Deadline: **February 1, 2027**. The WMU Foundation's missionary-kid track above is the Southern Baptist counterpart.

## Regional and Catholic Options

**Pacific Northwest**: CRISTA Ministries' Royal Brougham Foundation Scholarship funds Christian students who are Washington, Idaho, or Oregon residents attending CCCU-affiliated universities, with priority for kids of parents in full-time Christian service. Applications due May 15 each year.

**Catholic fraternal societies** offer awards with a catch worth stating plainly: eligibility runs through membership, and membership runs through holding one of their insurance or financial products. **Catholic United Financial** pays $500 (Catholic college) or $300 (elsewhere) to students whose families have been members at least two years, applications March 1 to April 30. **FCSLA Life** awards $1,250 for college ($1,000 high school, $1,750 graduate) to members of three-plus years holding a qualifying certificate, due **February 9, 2027**, from a pool over $248,000. If your family already holds these products, free money; if not, do not buy an insurance policy to chase a $500 award.

## Prize Money for Scripture Memory

The **National Bible Bee** is a competition rather than financial aid, and the numbers are remarkable: **$50,000 for the senior division winner**, $20,000 junior, $10,000 primary, over $135,000 given away each year for Scripture memorization and knowledge. For a Bible-loving teenager, this is the biggest faith-related award in the country. Registration happens through their summer study program each year. ## The Flags: Know What You Are Entering

- **The Christian Connector $5,000 drawing** is real, but it is a **sweepstakes, not a scholarship**: entry means submitting your contact information to receive marketing from Christian colleges, winners are drawn at random, and no selection criteria exist. Fine to enter with open eyes. (Aggregators still list it at $2,500; the sponsor's own site says $5,000, which tells you how often aggregators recheck.)
- **Platform micro-awards** on scholarship sites (the $500-$1,000 listings requiring an account on the platform itself) are product funnels for the platform. Some pay out; your time usually earns more in the denominational lists above.
- **"No essay" sweepstakes** that aggregator sites promote alongside real listings are their own lead-generation, not Christian scholarships at all.

## How to Actually Win These

1. **Denomination first.** Match the list above to your church; a WMU or ABHMS applicant pool is thousands of times smaller than a national sweepstakes.
2. **Ask your church itself.** Many congregations and state conventions run small unadvertised scholarships; your pastor knows.
3. **Calendar the real deadlines now**: December 31 (WMU), January 1 (UMHEF opens), February 1 (CMSF), February 19 (ELCA), March 15 (HELM), April 15 (ABHMS).
4. **Reapply annually.** Several of these (ABHMS, Christian Service Foundation) renew only if you ask again.
5. **Strong essays win denominational awards**, and most ask for a faith story. If writing is your gift, the craft in our guide to [how to write a devotional](/blog/how-to-write-a-devotional) transfers directly, and [Christian writing contests](/blog/christian-writing-contests) pay students too.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What scholarships exist for Christian students?

The verified ones cluster by denomination: WMU Foundation (Southern Baptist), ABHMS (American Baptist), UMHEF (United Methodist), PC(USA) scholarships and the Samuel Robinson Award (Presbyterian), HELM (Disciples of Christ), ELCA Fund for Leaders (Lutheran seminary), plus CMSF for missionary kids, CRISTA's Royal Brougham for the Pacific Northwest, and the National Bible Bee's prize money. Start with your own denomination's fund.

### Is the Christian Connector scholarship legitimate?

It is a real $5,000 drawing, but it is a marketing sweepstakes: you enter by requesting information from Christian colleges, and winners are drawn at random rather than selected on merit or need. Enter if you genuinely want the college mail.

### Are there full-ride Christian scholarships?

Yes, at the seminary level: the ELCA Fund for Leaders covers full tuition for an MDiv or MA at the seven ELCA seminaries, roughly 30-35 awards a year. For undergraduates, denominational awards typically run $750-$4,000 a year, with renewable multi-year awards (CMSF, HELM) worth the most over four years.

### What Bible scholarships exist for kids and teens?

The National Bible Bee awards over $135,000 a year in prize money across three age divisions, topping out at $50,000 for the senior winner, for Scripture memorization and Bible knowledge.
