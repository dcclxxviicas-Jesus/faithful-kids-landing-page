There are Christians all over Twitch, but finding them through search is miserable: the results are stat pages, dead channels, and at least one very active streamer named Christian who streams Hearthstone and has no faith content at all (a real entry we had to throw out). So we verified this roster properly in November 2026: every streamer below states their faith in their own bio or content, and every one was live within recent weeks, with follower counts and last-streamed dates checked the day this was written.

Who this list is for: parents whose teens are discovering streaming culture, gamers looking for a stream where the chat won't curse at them, and anyone curious what Christian presence on Twitch actually looks like. ## The Gamer Churches (Yes, Really)

The most distinctive thing on Christian Twitch isn't a streamer; it's churches that exist natively there.

**[XP Church](https://www.twitch.tv/xpchurchforgamers)**, pastored by Matt Souza, calls itself the world's first church for gamers, with Saturday services simulcast to Twitch, YouTube, Discord, and Kick, and a stated mission to help every gamer know God, experience community, and discover their purpose. It streamed over 200 hours in the month before our check, including the day we checked. Worth knowing if you've read about it before: this is the channel formerly known as GodSquad Church, rebranded; old lists that point to the GodSquad name land on the same place.

**[Checkpoint Church](https://www.twitch.tv/checkpointchurch)** is the digital-first church designed, in its own words, for nerds, geeks, and gamers, founded by United Methodist pastor Nathan Webb. Twitch is its lightest surface; the Discord is literally described as the church building, with worship on YouTube.

**[FlatCapDapperPastor](https://www.twitch.tv/flatcapdapperpastor)** streams in Twitch's Read and Learn Bible category; on the day we checked, he was live reading the Bible. Around 1,500 followers and a three-decade marriage in the bio.

## The Streamers

**[BeardedBlevins](https://www.twitch.tv/beardedblevins)**, about 117K followers, is the biggest openly faith-forward channel we verified. His own four-word bio does the work: husband, father, Catholic, bearded. Fortnite and variety gaming, streaming over 140 hours in the past month. (Yes, he's Ninja's brother.)

**[BozGS](https://www.twitch.tv/bozgs)** runs a near-24/7 Zelda and Final Fantasy channel and introduces himself in his bio as a Jesus follower, husband, and father to his cat. About 2,800 followers, online at the moment we checked.

**[CaydenVierra](https://www.twitch.tv/caydenvierra)** is a Christian mom, digital artist, and VTuber whose community describes itself as a cozy, family-friendly place for people who like art, games, and a little bit of faith, actively moderated as a PG space, with a Christian Corner in her Discord for verses and prayer requests. The clearest self-declared family-friendly positioning on this list.

**[GamingEmbers](https://www.twitch.tv/gamingembers)** says it in the first line of the bio: a Christian variety streamer, a place to come have fun and take a break from the world. Minecraft, LEGO, and Kingdom Hearts; streamed the day we checked.

**[OneReson](https://www.twitch.tv/onereson)** hosts The Gospel TakeOver in Twitch's DJ category: live gospel music sets from a self-described follower of Christ, spreading the Gospel through worship, prayer, and fellowship. Streaming the day we checked.

**[Love Thy Nerd](https://www.twitch.tv/lovethynerd)** is the stream arm of the ministry that exists, in its own words, to be the love of Jesus to nerds and nerd culture: retro games and community streams, with the larger ministry publishing daily.

**[Geek Devotions](https://www.twitch.tv/geekdevotions)** is a husband-and-wife devotional ministry using geek culture to tell people they're loved, with sermon notes and devotionals on their site alongside the streams.

**zSarayu (Ysabella Grace)** is the UK's quiet phenomenon: UK Christian press has profiled her as the Christian gamer sharing Jesus with a combined online reach in the hundreds of thousands, praying and running Bible studies on stream. Her live home is Twitch-first; her [YouTube archive](https://www.youtube.com/@zSarayu) is where we verified her activity.

**And the famous one: Ninja.** Tyler Blevins, roughly 19 million Twitch followers, declared "Jesus is King" to his audience on stream in 2025, has said the first thing he now does each morning is pray instead of checking his phone, and attends church weekly with his wife, all per his own on-stream statements as covered by Christian press. The honest framing: Ninja is an outspoken Christian who streams, not a Christian-content channel, and nothing here is a cleanliness rating of his streams. But when teens ask whether any famous gamer is a Christian, he is now the answer.

## Christian Gaming Communities (the Discord Question)

Searches for a Christian gamers Discord outnumber searches for the streamers themselves, so, verified active in November 2026:

- **[Love Thy Nerd](https://lovethynerd.com)**: the 501(c)(3) ministry's public Discord (a couple thousand members), plus podcasts, daily Bible reading, and articles; publishing daily at our check.
- **[XP Church](https://xpchurch.com)**: Discord plus Saturday services; the most stream-active of the gamer churches.
- **[Checkpoint Church](https://checkpointchurch.com)**: the Discord-as-church-building model, with worship on YouTube.
- **[Christian Gamers Guild](https://www.christian-gamers-guild.org)**: the long-running guild, tabletop and RPG-leaning, with a statement of faith and a chaplain's column, posting steadily through late 2026. Note the hyphens; the unhyphenated domain is dead.
- **[Gaming & God's streamer directory](https://gamingandgod.com/resources/streamers/)** and the **[Reach Conference creator directory](https://reachconf.org/contentcreatordirectory/)** both maintain larger lists of Christian streamers; treat them as leads to vet (that's how we found several entries above, and also the Hearthstone guy named Christian), not as endorsements.

One honest status-unclear: **Game Church**, the famous bridge-the-gap-between-the-Gospel-and-the-gamer outreach, still has a live website, but we could find no activity dated 2025 or 2026. Recommend the ones above instead.

## What We Dropped, and Why It Matters

Half the names on existing "Christian streamer" lists failed verification: several channels with lovely faith-forward bios simply stopped streaming months ago, one list entry turned out to be a secular streamer whose first name is Christian, and another's family-friendly bio contained no faith identification at all. This niche's lists rot fast, which is why every entry above carries a checked date. If a Christian streamer you love is missing, they may just stream rarely; the bar here was recent, verifiable activity.

## A Note for Parents

Live content is different in kind from videos: no one, including the streamer, can pre-screen a live chat, and "family friendly" on Twitch is a self-applied label with no enforcement. For teens, the channels above are the good end of the platform, and watching together beats policy-setting from another room. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who are the most famous Christian gamers?

Ninja (Tyler Blevins) is by far the most famous gamer who is openly Christian, per his own on-stream statements since 2025. At mainstream scale on YouTube, EYstreem (13.9M subscribers) opens his channel with Scripture, and Preston's channels describe themselves as fueled by fun, family, and faith; our [Christian YouTubers roundup](/blog/christian-youtubers) covers that side. The biggest faith-forward native Twitch channel we verified is BeardedBlevins.

### Are there family-friendly Christian streamers?

A few state it themselves, which is the standard that matters: CaydenVierra runs an actively moderated, self-described PG community, and the gamer churches (XP Church, Checkpoint) are ministry spaces by design. Remember that any live stream's chat is unscripted; preview before handing it to a younger viewer.

### Is there a Christian gamers Discord?

Several active ones, verified November 2026: Love Thy Nerd's public server, XP Church's Discord (with Saturday services), Checkpoint Church's Discord (their primary gathering place), and the Christian Gamers Guild's forum community for tabletop fans. Start with whichever matches your scene; they're welcoming to newcomers by mission.

### Can watching Twitch be good for a Christian teen?

It can be either the best or worst of the internet, sometimes in the same hour. The channels above show the good version: real believers being normal and public about faith inside gaming culture. The practical guardrails are the same as for any platform: watch together early on, talk about what chat culture rewards, and treat the question of gaming itself seriously, which we do in [is gaming a sin?](/blog/is-gaming-a-sin)
