## Is Grace on Roblox a Christian Game? (The Honest Answer)

This is the question parents are actually searching, so let's answer it properly: **no, Grace is not a Christian game.** It is a fast-paced survival horror game, by its own description "a Rooms game with a focus on chaos, movement and speed," where players run between saferooms avoiding monsters. It's free, enormously popular (over 464 million visits as of November 2026), and its own listed inspirations include horror titles like DOORS, Pressure, and Mandela Catalogue alongside "The Bible."

The Christian-sounding parts are real but decorative: fans read the monsters as representing sins and the titular flower as a symbol of grace. That makes Grace a secular horror game wearing biblical imagery, not a game made to teach or honor the faith. It isn't anti-Christian either; it's simply not what the title suggests. If your kids ask to play it, judge it as you would any monster-chase horror game, not as Bible content.

Worth knowing on the same platform: **Grace Chapel** (no relation) is an actual Roblox church run by Grace Online Ministries, with a scheduled Sunday worship service and about 777,000 visits since 2020; it was updated the day before we checked. The famous Robloxian Christians church, which ran roughly 1,400 in-game services across twelve years, ended live services in 2023. And notably, there is no large actively-maintained *Christian-made game* on Roblox at all; the big "Christian-adjacent" numbers belong to the horror game.

If your kids are on Roblox either way, [Roblox's own parental controls](https://corp.roblox.com/parents/) let you set a maximum content rating, turn chat off entirely, cap monthly spending, and set daily screen-time limits from a linked parent account. Use them.

## The Christian Games You Can Actually Play Tonight

### 1. Superbook Games (CBN): free, and the safest pick

The gold standard for "actually free, actually alive." CBN's [Superbook games page](https://us-en.superbook.cbn.com/games) hosts 21 browser games (Escape to Egypt, Dove Quest, Bible Explorer, and more), and the free [Superbook Kids Bible app](https://secure.cbn.com/superbook/superbook-free-kids-bible-app.aspx) adds 52 full-length animated episodes, 20+ games, and an audio Bible on iOS, Android, and Kindle Fire. Ministry-funded, no paywall, no ads. Ages roughly 6-12.

### 2. TruPlay: the subscription games app

[TruPlay](https://www.truplaygames.com) is a faith-based app of Bible-based games, comics, and animated videos for ages 4-12, set in its original World of Rhym universe: no ads, no chatrooms, no in-app purchases inside the games, up to ten kid profiles. Two transparency notes from our check: their own site publishes no dollar price (the App Store listing shows Premium tiers at $12.99 and $69.99, with a 7-day free trial), and several older articles name games that don't appear in the current app's own listings, so go by what's in the app today, where RhymVerse and Oliver and the Vindictive Vines headline.

### 3. Lightgliders: free, donation-supported

Widely assumed dead, actually alive: [Lightgliders](https://www.lightgliders.com) pairs faith-filled mini-games with a weekly biblical-worldview curriculum for ages 6-14, on the app stores and the web. Its homepage says free to play with no account or credit card required; it runs on donations. A good middle pick between Superbook's simplicity and TruPlay's subscription.

### 4. The Pilgrim's Progress (Steam): promising, with an asterisk

A $24.99 action-adventure RPG of Bunyan's allegory, released December 2024. The caveat comes from its own Steam page: the release contains only the first three of seven chapters, with the rest promised as free updates, and it has almost no user reviews yet. For families who love the book, worth watching; for everyone else, wait for the remaining chapters.

### 5. I Am Jesus Christ (Steam): for teens and adults, not family night

Fully released April 2026 at $19.99 with a "Very Positive" rating (87% of about 1,570 reviews), this first-person "spiritual simulation" walks through Jesus's ministry from baptism to resurrection, with 30+ playable miracles. Two things parents should know from its own page: it depicts the crucifixion in first person, and the developers disclose AI tools were used for voice acting. A genuine curiosity for older teens and adults; not a kids' game, and its store page carries no age rating. The free Prologue that older articles link was removed from Steam; a demo on the main page replaced it.

### 6. Gate Zero: the one to watch for 2027

The ambitious "explore first-century Judea" Bible adventure has been "coming soon" since roughly 2021. Current state from its official site and Steam page: not released, now developed and published by Templar Media (which acquired the original Norwegian studio Bible X in December 2025), with a Spring 2027 window for PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S. Wishlist it; don't trust any list that says you can play it now.

## The Dead List: Games Old Articles Still Recommend

This section is why we verify. Each of these still appears in Christian-game roundups, and none of them can be played today:

- **Guardians of Ancora** (Scripture Union's free Bible quest for ages 8-11): delisted from both the App Store and Google Play, and its website no longer loads. As of November 2026 there is no way to download it. It was genuinely good, which is why lists keep recommending it; it is gone.
- **The Aetherlight: Chronicles of the Resistance** (the steampunk Bible MMO): offline. Its own site now shows only "The Aetherlight is returning" with a fundraising link for a hoped-for revival. You cannot play it today.
- **I Am Jesus Christ: Prologue**: removed from Steam; old free-download links are dead.
- **Though the Heavens Fall** (Christian JRPG): no longer sold on Steam.

One more to not confuse: "Heaven Forest" titles on Steam are vaguely-spiritual meditation games with no Christian content, whatever the thumbnails imply.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best Christian video game for kids?

For most families in 2026: start free with Superbook's games and app (ages 6-12), add Lightgliders if your kids want a world to progress in, and consider TruPlay if a subscription app with original characters fits your budget. All three were verified live in November 2026.

### Is Grace on Roblox appropriate for Christian kids?

Grace is a survival horror game that uses biblical imagery, not a Christian game. Whether it's appropriate is the same judgment you'd make about any monster-chase game: it's built around tension and jump scares, recommends headphones for the full effect, and teaches no Bible. Roblox's parental controls can set a maximum content rating if you'd rather your kids not meet it.

### Are there Christian video games on PS5 or Xbox?

Almost none today, which is an honest gap in the market. Gate Zero is slated for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S in Spring 2027, and I Am Jesus Christ (PC) suits teens and adults. The living Christian-game ecosystem in 2026 is on phones, tablets, and browsers, not consoles.

### Are there any free Christian video games?

Yes, genuinely free: Superbook's 21 browser games and its Kids Bible app (CBN, ministry-funded), and Lightgliders (donation-supported, no account required). Both were verified live in November 2026. Be wary of "free" games from old lists; the two most-recommended free titles of the last decade, Guardians of Ancora and The Aetherlight, are both gone.
