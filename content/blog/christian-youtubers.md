Lists of Christian YouTubers age badly, because channels go quiet without announcing it. Several of the names other roundups still recommend have not uploaded in over a year. So we checked every channel below against its own page and upload feed in November 2026: all 18 are genuinely active, the subscriber counts are what YouTube displayed the day we checked, and the quiet ones are named at the end so you stop recommending them too.

Looking for channels for young kids instead? That's a different list: our [best Christian YouTube channels for kids](/blog/christian-youtube-channels-for-kids) covers Saddleback Kids, Superbook, and friends by age. (Full disclosure while we're here: we run a kids' Bible channel ourselves and make the [Faithful Kids](https://faithfulkids.app/quiz) app; neither is ranked below.)

## Apologetics and Hard Questions

### 1. Mike Winger (BibleThinker)

About 1M subscribers. Verse-by-verse teaching and long-form answers to hard questions, under his own banner of learning to think biblically about everything. Uploading multiple times a week as of mid-November 2026. Best for parents and older teens who want depth over clips.

### 2. THE BEAT by Allen Parr

About 1.4M subscribers, and probably the easiest on-ramp on this list: clear, practical ten-minute Bible answers and discernment videos, weekly. Best for new believers.

### 3. Cross Examined (Frank Turek)

About 956K subscribers. Campus Q&A sessions and classic "I Don't Have Enough Faith to Be an Atheist" apologetics, uploading near-daily. Best for teens heading to college and their parents.

### 4. Give Me An Answer (Cliffe & Stuart Knechtle)

About 968K subscribers. Open-mic dialogues with university students, clipped into the short, respectful exchanges teens actually share with each other. If your teenager already quotes Cliffe clips, this is why.

### 5. Sean McDowell

About 410K subscribers. The Biola professor's interviews cross worldview lines with unusual civility, and he teaches specifically on Gen Z faith, which makes him best-for-parents-of-teens almost by definition. Active same-week at our check.

### 6. Melissa Dougherty

About 417K subscribers. Discernment content from a former New Ager, with interviews and her own brand of gentle satire. Best for parents untangling New Age and relativism influences on their kids.

### 7. Whaddo You Meme?? (Jon McCray)

About 594K subscribers. Apologetics through pop culture, self-described as thoughtful, researched, respectful, and gospel-centric. Best for teens who live in meme language.

### 8. Testify (Erik Manning)

About 232K subscribers. Focused on one question, whether you can trust the New Testament, answered with evidence, steadily. Best for doubters and new believers.

### 9. InspiringPhilosophy (Michael Jones)

About 579K subscribers. The academic end of YouTube apologetics: philosophy of religion, debates, weekly livestreams. Best for intellectually hungry older teens.

## Bible Teaching

### 10. BibleProject

About 5.5M subscribers. Yes, it's in our kids' list too, and it earns a second mention because the adult material is different in kind: book overviews, word studies, and Classroom lectures that are effectively free seminary. Best for parents and small groups who want to actually understand each book.

## Lifestyle and Commentary

### 11. Ruslan KD

About 862K subscribers. Christian culture commentary and creator-economy takes from a Christian hip-hop background, uploading near-daily. Best for older teens and young adults processing Christian news and controversy.

### 12. Coffee and Bible Time

About 390K subscribers. Quiet-time methods, Bible-study walkthroughs, and devotional vlogs; the channel's own invitation is to grab a coffee, a cozy blanket, and your Bible. Best for teen girls and moms building a daily habit.

### 13. With The Perrys (Jackie Hill Perry & Preston Perry)

About 504K subscribers. The married-couple podcast on theology, relationships, parenting, and culture. Worth knowing: this is the live home for both Perrys, whose solo channels have gone dormant, so send people here.

## Music

### 14. Forrest Frank

About 3.15M subscribers, and the biggest crossover force in Christian music right now. Lyric videos and faith shorts, uploading weekly. One hygiene note from our verification: the real channel is @hiforrest; several lookalike "official" Forrest Frank channels are fakes.

## Christian Gaming YouTubers

The most-asked flavor of this question is also the thinnest, so here is the honest state of Christian gaming YouTube in 2026, verified channel by channel. If your kids watch gaming more than they play it, our [family-friendly YouTubers guide](/blog/family-friendly-youtubers) covers the mainstream side, and our [Christian streamers roundup](/blog/christian-twitch-streamers) covers the live side.

### 15. EYstreem

About 13.9M subscribers, mainstream-scale Minecraft entertainment, and the channel description opens with Psalm 89:9. The honest framing: this is a believer succeeding in the mainstream, not a ministry channel; the content is general Minecraft entertainment, some of it spooky-story flavored, so judge episodes like any secular kids' gaming channel.

### 16. Ryan Wright

About 62K subscribers and the most natural "Christian Minecraft YouTuber" answer that exists: the channel's stated mission is teaching the Gospel in comedic and entertaining ways, with videos like church-building competitions in Minecraft. Weekly uploads. Best for teens.

### 17. DeadlyDill

Small on YouTube (under 4K subscribers) and big where it counts: founder of the Deadly Fam, a Christian gaming community of roughly 15,000 members, with a weekly Bible Study for Gamers series that has not missed. Best for gamer teens who would never open a devotional, and for parents looking for a community rather than just videos.

### 18. zSarayu

About 12.6K YouTube subscribers, but Twitch-first, where UK Christian press has profiled her as quietly sharing Jesus with a reach in the hundreds of thousands. Her YouTube is the archive; the live ministry happens on stream. Best for teen girls into streaming culture.

## Channels That Went Quiet (Check Before You Recommend)

Verified silent as of November 16, 2026, despite still appearing on popular lists:

- **Impact Video Ministries** (1.45M subscribers): no upload in 14 months.
- **Milena Ciciotti** (643K): no upload since June 2026.
- **Red Pen Logic with Tim Barnett** (159K): dormant since July 2026; his content now runs on Stand to Reason's main channel (@STRvideos), which is active.
- **Preston Perry's solo apologetics channel**: dormant since 2024; he's active on With The Perrys.
- Most "Christian Minecraft" lists still cite channels whose last uploads were in 2021. The living options are the four in the gaming section above.
