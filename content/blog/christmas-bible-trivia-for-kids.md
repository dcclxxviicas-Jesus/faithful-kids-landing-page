Here are 45 Christmas Bible trivia questions for kids, complete with answers and Scripture references, organized into easy (ages 5-7), medium (ages 8-11), and hard (ages 12+) levels. Every question comes straight from the biblical Christmas story in Matthew 1-2 and Luke 1-2 -- the annunciation, the journey to Bethlehem, the shepherds, the wise men, and the escape to Egypt.

Fair warning: a few of these will stump the adults too. Most of us picture three kings arriving at a stable on Christmas night, and the Bible tells a slightly different story. That's half the fun -- trivia night is a sneaky way to get the whole family reading the actual account.

If your kids are new to the story itself, start with our walkthrough of [Christmas Bible stories for kids](/blog/christmas-bible-stories-for-kids) first, then come back and quiz them.

## How Should You Use These Questions?

Use them anywhere you have five spare minutes in December -- no supplies needed. Some favorites from our own house:

- **Advent countdown:** Ask two questions a night after dinner. Pair them with our [Advent Bible stories for kids](/blog/advent-bible-stories-for-kids) reading plan.
- **Christmas Eve game:** Split into teams before the candlelight service. Winner picks the first cookie.
- **Car rides:** December is full of driving -- to grandma's, to the lights, to church. Quiz as you go.
- **Sunday school or kids' church:** Use the easy round as an icebreaker and the hard round as a review game.

**Tip:** When a question surprises someone ("Wait, the Bible doesn't say there were three wise men?"), pause and read the verse together. The surprise is where the learning sticks.

## Easy Christmas Trivia (Ages 5-7)

These cover the parts of the story even the youngest kids know from nativity scenes and Christmas pageants.

**1. In what town was Jesus born?**
Answer: **Bethlehem** (Luke 2:4-7)

**2. Who was Jesus' mother?**
Answer: **Mary** (Luke 1:30-31)

**3. What was the name of Mary's husband?**
Answer: **Joseph** (Matthew 1:24)

**4. Where did Mary lay baby Jesus after he was born?**
Answer: **In a manger** -- a feeding trough for animals (Luke 2:7)

**5. Why did Mary have to lay Jesus in a manger?**
Answer: **There was no guest room available for them** (Luke 2:7)

**6. Who was out in the fields watching their sheep the night Jesus was born?**
Answer: **Shepherds** (Luke 2:8)

**7. Who told the shepherds that Jesus had been born?**
Answer: **An angel of the Lord** (Luke 2:9-11)

**8. What did the shepherds do after the angels left?**
Answer: **They hurried to Bethlehem to see the baby** (Luke 2:15-16)

**9. What did the wise men follow to find Jesus?**
Answer: **A star** (Matthew 2:2)

**10. What three gifts did the wise men bring?**
Answer: **Gold, frankincense, and myrrh** (Matthew 2:11)

**11. What was the name of the angel who visited Mary?**
Answer: **Gabriel** (Luke 1:26-27)

**12. What name did the angel tell Mary to give her baby?**
Answer: **Jesus** (Luke 1:31)

**13. What do we celebrate at Christmas?**
Answer: **The birth of Jesus**

**14. Was baby Jesus wrapped in blankets from a store?**
Answer: **No -- Mary wrapped him in cloths** (Luke 2:7)

**15. Did one angel or many angels sing praise to God in front of the shepherds?**
Answer: **Many -- a great company of angels appeared, praising God** (Luke 2:13-14)

## Medium Christmas Trivia (Ages 8-11)

Now we get into names, places, and reasons -- the details kids this age love to master.

**16. In what town was Mary living when the angel Gabriel visited her?**
Answer: **Nazareth, in Galilee** (Luke 1:26)

**17. Who was Mary's relative who was also expecting a miracle baby?**
Answer: **Elizabeth** (Luke 1:36)

**18. What was the name of Elizabeth's baby, who grew up to prepare the way for Jesus?**
Answer: **John** -- known as John the Baptist (Luke 1:60)

**19. Why did Mary and Joseph have to travel to Bethlehem?**
Answer: **A census -- the emperor ordered everyone to be counted in their family's hometown** (Luke 2:1-4)

**20. Which Roman emperor ordered that census?**
Answer: **Caesar Augustus** (Luke 2:1)

**21. What were the first words the angel said to the frightened shepherds?**
Answer: **"Do not be afraid"** (Luke 2:10)

**22. What sign did the angel give the shepherds so they could find the right baby?**
Answer: **A baby wrapped in cloths and lying in a manger** (Luke 2:12)

**23. What did the great company of angels say?**
Answer: **"Glory to God in the highest heaven, and on earth peace to those on whom his favor rests"** (Luke 2:14)

**24. From which direction did the wise men come?**
Answer: **The east** (Matthew 2:1)

**25. Which king was disturbed when the wise men asked where the new king had been born?**
Answer: **King Herod** (Matthew 2:3)

**26. What did Herod secretly ask the wise men to do?**
Answer: **Find the child and report back to him** -- he claimed he wanted to worship Jesus too (Matthew 2:8)

**27. How were the wise men warned not to go back to Herod?**
Answer: **In a dream** (Matthew 2:12)

**28. Where did Joseph take Mary and Jesus to escape King Herod?**
Answer: **Egypt** (Matthew 2:13-14)

**29. How did Joseph find out he needed to flee to Egypt?**
Answer: **An angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream** (Matthew 2:13)

**30. Which famous king of Israel was Joseph descended from?**
Answer: **King David** -- the angel even calls him "Joseph son of David" (Matthew 1:20; Luke 2:4)

**31. What does the name Immanuel mean?**
Answer: **"God with us"** (Matthew 1:23)

**32. Why was the baby to be named Jesus?**
Answer: **Because he would save his people from their sins** (Matthew 1:21)

## Hard Christmas Trivia (Ages 12+)

This is the round where the Bible corrects the Christmas card. Read the verses -- several answers surprise almost everyone.

**33. How many wise men does the Bible say there were?**
Answer: **The Bible never gives a number** -- we assume three because of the three gifts, but Matthew only says "Magi from the east" (Matthew 2:1, 11)

**34. When the wise men arrived, where did they find Jesus -- in a stable or in a house?**
Answer: **In a house** -- likely months after the birth, not on Christmas night (Matthew 2:11)

**35. What animal did Mary ride to Bethlehem?**
Answer: **Trick question -- the Bible never mentions an animal.** The donkey is tradition, not Scripture (Luke 2:4-5)

**36. Which two people recognized baby Jesus as the Messiah when his parents brought him to the temple?**
Answer: **Simeon and Anna** (Luke 2:25-38)

**37. What had the Holy Spirit promised Simeon?**
Answer: **That he would not die before he had seen the Lord's Messiah** (Luke 2:26)

**38. What offering did Mary and Joseph bring to the temple, showing the family was poor?**
Answer: **A pair of doves or two young pigeons** (Luke 2:24)

**39. What terrible order did Herod give when the wise men never returned?**
Answer: **To kill all the boys in Bethlehem and its vicinity who were two years old and under** (Matthew 2:16)

**40. Which prophet foretold that the ruler of Israel would come from Bethlehem?**
Answer: **Micah** (Micah 5:2, quoted in Matthew 2:5-6)

**41. Which prophet wrote, "The virgin will conceive and give birth to a son, and will call him Immanuel"?**
Answer: **Isaiah** (Isaiah 7:14, quoted in Matthew 1:22-23)

**42. What happened to Zechariah, Elizabeth's husband, when he doubted the angel's message?**
Answer: **He was unable to speak until his son John was born** (Luke 1:20)

**43. What is Mary's song of praise called, which begins "My soul glorifies the Lord"?**
Answer: **The Magnificat** (Luke 1:46-55)

**44. After returning from Egypt, what town did Jesus' family settle in?**
Answer: **Nazareth** -- which is why Jesus was called a Nazarene (Matthew 2:23)

**45. Does the Bible say Jesus was born on December 25?**
Answer: **No -- Scripture never gives a date.** The church chose December 25 centuries later to celebrate the birth

## How Do You Keep Score Without Tears?

Give points for effort, not just accuracy. A simple system that works for mixed ages: 2 points for a correct answer, 1 point for a close answer or good guess, and 1 bonus point for anyone (right or wrong) who can add one extra detail from the story. Younger kids answer only the easy round; parents must answer from the hard round. Suddenly everyone has a fair shot -- and everyone hears the whole story three times without complaining.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are some good Christmas Bible trivia questions for kids?
Good starter questions include: Where was Jesus born? (Bethlehem), Who visited Mary to announce the birth? (the angel Gabriel), and What gifts did the wise men bring? (gold, frankincense, and myrrh). Start with questions from nativity scenes kids already know, then work up to details like the census, Simeon and Anna, and the flight to Egypt.

### What age is Christmas Bible trivia appropriate for?
Kids as young as 4-5 can answer the easiest questions if they've heard the Christmas story a few times. Ages 5-7 handle the easy round, 8-11 the medium round, and 12+ can tackle trick questions about what the Bible actually says versus tradition. Mixed-age families work best with tiered questions so everyone gets a fair turn.

### Were there really three wise men in the Bible?
The Bible never says how many wise men there were. Matthew 2 only says "Magi from the east" came to Jerusalem, and the number three comes from the three gifts they brought -- gold, frankincense, and myrrh. It's one of the best trick questions in this list because most adults get it wrong.

### Did the wise men visit Jesus at the stable?
No -- Matthew 2:11 says the Magi came to a house, not a stable, likely weeks or months after Jesus was born. Herod's order to kill boys two years old and under (Matthew 2:16) suggests significant time had passed. The shepherds visited on the night of the birth; the wise men came later.

### How can I use Christmas trivia during Advent?
Ask one or two questions per night after dinner or before bed throughout December, moving from easy to hard as Christmas approaches. Pair each question with a short reading from Matthew 1-2 or Luke 1-2 so kids hear the story behind the answer. By Christmas Eve, your kids will know the real story better than most adults.

### Where in the Bible is the Christmas story found?
The Christmas story appears in Matthew 1-2 and Luke 1-2. Luke tells the annunciation, the journey to Bethlehem, the manger, and the shepherds; Matthew tells Joseph's side, the wise men, King Herod, and the escape to Egypt. Mark and John skip the birth and begin with Jesus' adult ministry.
