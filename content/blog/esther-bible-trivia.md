These 27 Esther Bible trivia questions cover the orphan girl who became queen of Persia and saved her people from destruction -- with answers and verse references after every question, sorted into easy, medium, and hard rounds by age. Several are trick questions, starting with the relative who raised Esther (it's not her uncle).

Esther is built for trivia night: a beauty contest for a crown, a villain who builds a 75-foot gallows, a king who can't sleep at exactly the right moment, and a book so unusual it never mentions God by name -- while showing his fingerprints on every page.

## What Is the Book of Esther About?

The book of Esther tells how a young Jewish woman became queen of Persia and risked her life to save her people from a plot to destroy them. The author is unknown -- tradition has suggested Mordecai himself -- and the story is set in Susa, capital of the Persian Empire, during the reign of King Xerxes (called Ahasuerus in Hebrew), around 483-473 BC.

The plot turns on a single dilemma: when Haman schemes to kill every Jew in the empire, Queen Esther must approach the king uninvited -- a capital offense -- or stay silent and watch her people die. The book matters because it shows God's providence working through "coincidences" without a single miracle, and it explains the origin of Purim, the Jewish holiday still celebrated today. Its most famous line is Mordecai's challenge: "Who knows but that you have come to your royal position for such a time as this?" (Esther 4:14).

A timeline anchor helps before you start: Esther takes place between Ezra chapters 6 and 7 -- after the temple was rebuilt in Jerusalem, but among the many Jews who stayed behind in Persia. That's why the danger is empire-wide, and why Mordecai and Esther are living in a Persian capital instead of Jerusalem.

## Easy Esther Trivia Questions (Ages 5-8)

1. What did Esther become in the land of Persia? **The queen** (Esther 2:17)

2. Who was the man who raised Esther after her parents died? **Mordecai** (Esther 2:7)

3. Who was the villain who plotted to destroy Esther's people, the Jews? **Haman** (Esther 3:5-6)

4. What did Mordecai refuse to do that made Haman so angry? **Bow down to Haman** (Esther 3:2-5)

5. What brave thing did Esther do to save her people? **She went to see the king without being invited, which could cost her life** (Esther 4:16-5:2)

6. What did the king hold out to Esther to show she was welcome and safe? **His gold scepter** (Esther 5:2)

7. What did Esther invite the king and Haman to -- twice? **A banquet (a special dinner)** (Esther 5:4-8)

8. What happened to Haman in the end? **He was hanged on the very gallows he built for Mordecai** (Esther 7:10)

9. Were Esther's people saved? **Yes -- the Jews were rescued, and they celebrated with feasting and joy** (Esther 8:16-17)

## Medium Esther Trivia Questions (Ages 9-12)

10. Was Mordecai Esther's uncle? **Trick question -- he was her cousin, who adopted her as his own daughter** (Esther 2:7)

11. What was Esther's Hebrew name? **Hadassah** (Esther 2:7)

12. Which queen lost her crown for refusing to appear at King Xerxes' banquet? **Queen Vashti** (Esther 1:10-12, 19)

13. What secret did Esther keep when she first entered the palace? **That she was Jewish -- Mordecai told her not to reveal her nationality** (Esther 2:10)

14. What plot did Mordecai uncover and report, saving the king's life? **Two officers, Bigthana and Teresh, were planning to assassinate King Xerxes** (Esther 2:21-23)

15. What did Esther ask all the Jews in Susa to do before she approached the king? **Fast for three days, night and day, as she and her attendants did the same** (Esther 4:16)

16. Finish Esther's famous words: "I will go to the king, even though it is against the law. And if I perish..." **"...I perish"** (Esther 4:16)

17. Finish Mordecai's famous challenge: "Who knows but that you have come to your royal position for such a..." **"...time as this?"** (Esther 4:14)

18. Why couldn't the king sleep on the night that changed everything -- and what did he do about it? **He had the book of the records read to him, and heard how Mordecai had never been rewarded for exposing the assassination plot** (Esther 6:1-3)

19. What honor did Haman design, thinking it was for himself? **A royal robe and the king's horse, led through the city streets with a herald shouting, "This is what is done for the man the king delights to honor!" -- and he had to do it all for Mordecai** (Esther 6:7-11)

20. What Jewish holiday celebrates the events of the book of Esther? **Purim, named after the "pur" (the lot) Haman cast** (Esther 9:24-26)

## Hard Esther Trivia Questions (Teens and Adults)

21. Over how many provinces did King Xerxes rule, and from where to where? **127 provinces, stretching from India to Cush** (Esther 1:1)

22. How long did each young woman's beauty treatments last before meeting the king -- and what were they? **Twelve months: six with oil of myrrh and six with perfumes and cosmetics** (Esther 2:12)

23. How much silver did Haman offer to pay into the royal treasury for the right to destroy the Jews? **Ten thousand talents** (Esther 3:9)

24. Haman was called "the Agagite" (Esther 3:1). Why is that a loaded family name? **Agag was the king of the Amalekites -- Israel's ancient enemies -- whom King Saul failed to fully defeat (1 Samuel 15:8-9), making Haman's feud with Mordecai the Benjamite generations old**

25. How tall was the gallows Haman built for Mordecai, at his wife Zeresh's suggestion? **Fifty cubits -- about 75 feet high** (Esther 5:14)

26. Since the king's first decree could not be revoked, how were the Jews actually saved? **A second decree, written by Mordecai with the king's signet ring, gave the Jews the right to assemble and defend themselves** (Esther 8:8-11)

27. What is famously missing from the entire book of Esther? **Any mention of God's name -- yet his providence drives every "coincidence" in the story**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who wrote the book of Esther?

The author is unknown. Jewish tradition has suggested Mordecai -- who "recorded these events" (Esther 9:20) -- or the men of the Great Assembly, but the book itself never names its writer. It was clearly written by someone with detailed knowledge of the Persian court.

### How many chapters does Esther have?

Esther has 10 chapters, covering roughly a decade -- from Vashti's banquet in Xerxes' third year (Esther 1:3) to the establishment of Purim after the Jews' deliverance.

### Why is God never mentioned in the book of Esther?

The book never uses God's name, but readers have always seen his hand in its perfectly timed "coincidences" -- Esther's crown, Mordecai's overheard plot, the king's sleepless night. Mordecai hints at this in Esther 4:14: deliverance for the Jews would arise "from another place" even if Esther stayed silent.

### What is the most famous verse in Esther?

Esther 4:14 -- "Who knows but that you have come to your royal position for such a time as this?" Esther's reply in 4:16, "If I perish, I perish," is a close second.

### Was Esther a real queen of Persia?

Esther is presented as history, set under Xerxes I (Ahasuerus), who reigned 486-465 BC, and the book's Persian court details are strikingly accurate. She isn't named in surviving Persian records, which mostly ignore royal wives, so the Bible is our source for her story.

### What age can kids start learning the story of Esther?

Ages 4-5 can follow the simple version: a brave queen saves her people. The full plot -- with its edict of destruction and Haman's gallows -- is best for ages 8 and up, and teens can dig into the providence theme: where is God in a book that never names him?
