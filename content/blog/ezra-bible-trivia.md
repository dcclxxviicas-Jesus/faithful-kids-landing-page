These 27 Ezra Bible trivia questions cover the great return from exile -- Cyrus's decree, the rebuilding of the temple, and the scribe who taught a nation its own Bible again. Answers with verse references follow every question, sorted into easy, medium, and hard rounds by age.

Ezra is one of the most overlooked books in family Bible trivia, which is a shame, because it has everything: a pagan king funding God's temple, enemies writing angry letters, a lost scroll found in a faraway fortress, and old men weeping while young men shout for joy -- at the same event.

## What Is the Book of Ezra About?

The book of Ezra tells how God brought his people home from exile in Babylon to rebuild the temple in Jerusalem and rebuild their lives around God's law. Jewish tradition names Ezra the scribe as the author -- the book's second half is even written partly in the first person -- and it covers roughly 538 to 458 BC, picking up exactly where 2 Chronicles ends.

The story comes in two waves: chapters 1-6 follow Zerubbabel and about 42,000 returnees who rebuild the temple despite fierce opposition, and chapters 7-10 follow Ezra, a priest and scribe who arrives decades later to teach the people God's law. The book matters because it shows God keeping a seventy-year-old promise through Jeremiah -- and because it introduces the man whose motto every Bible teacher still borrows: Ezra "devoted himself to the study and observance of the Law of the LORD, and to teaching" (Ezra 7:10).

One tip before you start: Ezra spans about eighty years and three Persian kings (Cyrus, Darius, and Artaxerxes), and there's a gap of nearly six decades between chapter 6 and chapter 7 -- Ezra himself doesn't show up until more than halfway through his own book. Kids who catch that timeline detail will breeze through the hard round.

## Easy Ezra Trivia Questions (Ages 5-8)

1. Which king of Persia let God's people go home from exile to rebuild the temple? **Cyrus** (Ezra 1:1-3)

2. What building did the people return to Jerusalem to rebuild? **The temple -- the house of God** (Ezra 1:2-3)

3. Who was Ezra -- what were his two jobs? **He was a priest and a scribe (a teacher of God's law)** (Ezra 7:11-12)

4. What did the people build first, even before the temple foundation? **The altar, so they could offer sacrifices to God again** (Ezra 3:2-3)

5. What did the people do when the temple foundation was laid? **They sang, praised God, and shouted for joy** (Ezra 3:10-11)

6. Did enemies want the temple finished? **No -- they tried to discourage and stop the builders** (Ezra 4:4-5)

7. Was the temple finished in the end? **Yes -- the people completed it and celebrated with joy** (Ezra 6:15-16)

8. What special meal did the people celebrate after the temple was finished? **The Passover** (Ezra 6:19)

9. What book did Ezra love to study and teach? **The Law of the LORD -- God's Word** (Ezra 7:10)

## Medium Ezra Trivia Questions (Ages 9-12)

10. Which prophet's promise came true when Cyrus let the exiles go home? **Jeremiah's -- the LORD moved Cyrus's heart "to fulfill the word of the LORD spoken by Jeremiah"** (Ezra 1:1)

11. Who led the first group of exiles back to Jerusalem? **Zerubbabel, along with Jeshua the high priest** (Ezra 2:2; 3:2)

12. About how many people returned in the first group? **About 42,360, plus over 7,000 servants and 200 singers** (Ezra 2:64-65)

13. What did Cyrus send back to Jerusalem along with the people? **The gold and silver articles Nebuchadnezzar had taken from the temple -- 5,400 in all** (Ezra 1:7-11)

14. Why were some of the older people weeping while everyone else shouted for joy at the foundation ceremony? **They had seen Solomon's first temple and knew the new one was smaller -- the weeping and shouting mixed so loudly no one could tell them apart** (Ezra 3:12-13)

15. When the local enemies offered to "help" build the temple, what did Zerubbabel say? **No -- "You have no part with us in building a temple to our God"** (Ezra 4:1-3)

16. Which two prophets stirred the people to start building again after the work had stopped? **Haggai and Zechariah** (Ezra 5:1-2)

17. Which Persian governor questioned the builders and wrote to King Darius about them? **Tattenai** (Ezra 5:3-6)

18. What did King Darius's officials find that saved the temple project? **Cyrus's original decree, discovered on a scroll in the citadel of Ecbatana** (Ezra 6:1-3)

19. What surprising order did Darius give the enemies of the project? **Don't interfere -- and pay the building costs from the royal treasury** (Ezra 6:6-8)

20. In which king's reign did Ezra himself finally travel to Jerusalem? **Artaxerxes, who gave him a letter of support** (Ezra 7:1, 11-26)

## Hard Ezra Trivia Questions (Teens and Adults)

21. In whose reign, and in which year of it, was the temple finally completed? **The sixth year of King Darius** (Ezra 6:15)

22. Ezra traced his priestly family line back to which famous brother of Moses? **Aaron, the first high priest** (Ezra 7:1-5)

23. How long did Ezra's journey from Babylon to Jerusalem take? **Four months -- he left on the first day of the first month and arrived on the first day of the fifth month** (Ezra 7:8-9)

24. Why did Ezra refuse to ask the king for soldiers to protect his group on the dangerous journey? **He had told the king God protects those who seek him, so asking for troops would have shamed that testimony -- instead the group fasted and prayed at the Ahava Canal** (Ezra 8:21-23)

25. What did Ezra do to his own hair when he heard the people had disobeyed God by intermarrying with idol-worshiping peoples? **He tore his tunic and cloak and pulled hair from his own head and beard** (Ezra 9:3)

26. What was the weather doing while the people assembled in Jerusalem to confess their sin? **It was pouring rain -- the people were trembling from the occasion and the heavy rain** (Ezra 10:9)

27. What is linguistically unusual about the book of Ezra? **Large sections are written in Aramaic, not Hebrew -- including the official letters in Ezra 4:8-6:18 and 7:12-26**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who wrote the book of Ezra?

Jewish tradition names Ezra the scribe as the author, and chapters 7-9 include first-person "I" sections that read like his own memoir. Ezra and Nehemiah were originally counted as a single book in the Hebrew Bible.

### How many chapters does Ezra have?

Ezra has 10 chapters. Chapters 1-6 cover the first return under Zerubbabel and the temple rebuild; chapters 7-10 cover Ezra's own return about 58 years later.

### What is the most famous verse in Ezra?

Ezra 7:10 -- "For Ezra had devoted himself to the study and observance of the Law of the LORD, and to teaching its decrees and laws in Israel." The order matters: study it, live it, then teach it.

### What is the difference between Ezra and Nehemiah?

Ezra rebuilt the people's worship -- the temple and the teaching of God's law -- while Nehemiah rebuilt Jerusalem's walls about 13 years after Ezra arrived. The two men appear together in Nehemiah 8, when Ezra reads the Law aloud to the whole city.

### Was Ezra a priest or a scribe?

Both. He was a priest descended from Aaron (Ezra 7:1-5) and "a teacher well versed in the Law of Moses" (Ezra 7:6) -- the Bible's model scholar-pastor.

### What age can kids start learning the book of Ezra?

Ages 5-8 can follow the big storyline -- God's people go home and rebuild God's house -- especially told as a comeback story. The politics (letters, kings, decrees) clicks around ages 9-12, and the chapter 9-10 material about intermarriage and repentance is best for teens with a parent guiding the conversation.
