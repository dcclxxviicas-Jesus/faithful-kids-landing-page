## What's Inside the App

Everything in Faithful Kids is built around one loop: **watch, answer, talk about it.**

- **300+ video lessons, in order.** The whole Bible from Genesis to Revelation, told as short story videos a few minutes long — the length that fits a bedtime, a car ride, or the gap before dinner.
- **A quiz after every single story.** Three questions, instant feedback, and streaks — so you can see what your child actually retained, not just what they watched.
- **Reflections that start conversations.** After the quiz, tappable reflection prompts connect the story to your child's real life. No typing required.
- **Levels, XP, and streaks.** The same mechanics that keep kids glued to games, pointed at Scripture. Kids climb from Seedling toward Bible Master as they learn.
- **Up to 5 kid profiles.** Netflix-style "Who's learning today?" — each child gets their own progress, level, and avatar, so siblings never fight over an account.
- **A parent dashboard.** PIN-protected, with visibility into what each child watched and how they scored — accountability without hovering.
- **Captions on every video.** Reading skills grow alongside Bible knowledge.
- **Series unlock in order.** Kids can't skip to the flashy stories; knowledge builds the way the Book is written.

## Made for Kids — and Built Like It

Faithful Kids carries Apple's **Made for Kids** designation, which means it meets the App Store's strictest rules for children's apps. In practice:

- **No ads, no algorithm, no rabbit holes.** There is nothing to click away to. The app plays Bible lessons, and that is all it does.
- **A parental gate before anything payment-related.** Kids can't reach pricing or purchases; a birth-year gate keeps checkout in parents' hands.
- **No social features, no external links** in the child's experience.
- **COPPA-compliant** data practices, with a privacy policy written for parents rather than lawyers.

If you've been looking for screen time you don't have to supervise nervously, this is what it looks like.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the Faithful Kids app free?

The download is free and includes a 3-day free trial of everything. After the trial, a subscription ($8.99/month or $79.99/year through Apple) unlocks the full library of 300+ lessons for up to 5 kids. There are no ads at any tier, ever.

### What ages is the app for?

Faithful Kids is built for ages 5-15. The easy early series (Genesis, the life of Jesus) work read-aloud-style for the youngest, while quizzes, streaks, and the full Genesis-to-Revelation arc are what keep 8-15-year-olds engaged — the age range most kids' Bible apps quietly give up on.

### Is there an Android app?

Not yet. Android families use the full web app at [app.faithfulkids.app](https://app.faithfulkids.app) in any browser — it is the same product with the same account, and an App Store-style install isn't required.

### Is the app safe for kids to use alone?

Yes — that is the point of the Made for Kids badge. There are no ads, no autoplay rabbit holes, no chat, no external links in the kids' experience, and a parental gate in front of anything involving payment. The parent dashboard shows you what they watched and learned.

### How is this different from watching Bible videos on YouTube?

YouTube's next video is chosen by an algorithm with no opinion about your child's soul. Faithful Kids plays the Bible in order, checks comprehension after every story, and ends. Families who came to us from YouTube usually say the difference is that kids finish a session having *learned* something — and there was nothing else to wander off to. Our [Christian alternatives to YouTube](/blog/christian-alternatives-to-youtube-for-kids) guide covers the wider landscape.

### Can grandparents or a second household use the same subscription?

Yes — profiles live in one family account, so a child can sign in on Grandma's iPad or the web at Grandma's house and pick up exactly where they left off.
