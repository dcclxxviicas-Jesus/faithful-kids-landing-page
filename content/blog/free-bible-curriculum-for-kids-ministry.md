## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Faithful Kids really free for churches?

Yes — churches and kids ministries get real, full accounts for their children at no cost, not a demo or a time-limited trial. The [churches page](/churches) explains how to request access; there is no card required.

### What's the catch with free curricula?

Usually format, not quality: free curricula tend to be downloadable documents and videos that depend entirely on your volunteers' preparation. The paid products bundle planning tools, media packages, and support. If your team is small, pick the free option whose prep load matches your volunteers — pursueGOD Kids and Bible Adventure are the most open-and-go of the list.

### Can we combine these?

Almost everyone does. A common stack: one free curriculum for the Sunday lesson, Faithful Kids accounts so the kids keep learning at home during the week, and printables for the early-arrivers' table.
