If you run a children's ministry on a budget of nothing, everything in the first two piles is yours this Sunday.

## Full Curriculum, Genuinely Free

**Life.Church Open Network** is the strongest free offer in children's ministry, full stop. Their Bible Adventure preschool curriculum is, in their own words, "100% free... No cost. No catch," a multi-year program with videos, lesson plans, activities, and original worship songs. The elementary-age Konnect HQ curriculum and the two-year Bible App for Kids Curriculum (the church companion to YouVersion's kids app, which says plainly "It's all free!") come through the same door. The only requirement is a free Open Network account to download.

**Ministry-to-Children** states it directly: "Every lesson, sermon, game and coloring page on Ministry-to-Children is free to read and print," a library they count in the thousands. The distinction to know: the site is free; the packaged 52-week curriculum bundles they sell at their Sunday School Store are not. Read and print from the site and you pay nothing.

**Kids Corner** from ReFrame Ministries is donor-funded with no paywall anywhere: audio Bible story dramas, weekly devotionals, videos, and a pile of DIY activities, crafts, and ebooks, all free with optional signup.

**Sermons4Kids** is honestly freemium: the children's sermons and object lessons are free, each with a coloring page, word search, and group activities attached, while the full graded lesson plans and video lessons sit behind their paid tier ($19.99 a month). For a volunteer who needs Sunday's object lesson, the free side is plenty.

For a deeper comparison of the curriculum options specifically, including what the big paid programs cost, see our guide to [free Bible curriculum for kids ministry](/blog/free-bible-curriculum-for-kids-ministry).

## The "Free" Claims to Watch

- **RightNow Media is not free.** You must be connected to a church or organization that pays for a subscription. If your church has one, wonderful; if not, nothing on this page of their catalog is free to you.
- **Orange (Think Orange) offers a free month**, then an annual curriculum license. A good trial; not a free curriculum.
- **Group Publishing** sells its curriculum; its genuinely free property is ChildrensMinistry.com (below).
- **Calvary Curriculum**, long passed around as a free classic, now shows membership tiers on its site with free samples but no free-access statement. Check before you plan a year around it.
- **Bible Pathway Adventures**, despite its reputation, sells its printables (about $2 each).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What children's ministry curriculum is actually 100% free?

Verified in November 2026: Life.Church Open Network (Bible Adventure, Konnect HQ, and the Bible App for Kids Curriculum, behind a free account), everything published on Ministry-to-Children's own site, TrueWay Kids' printable lessons, and Kids Corner from ReFrame Ministries. ### Is RightNow Media free?

No. RightNow Media access comes through a subscription your church or organization purchases; individuals join under that subscription. There is no free general-access tier.

### Is Orange curriculum free?

No; Orange offers a free month of its kids curriculum as a trial, after which it is a paid annual license.
