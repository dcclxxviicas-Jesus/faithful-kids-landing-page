These 22 Hosea Bible trivia questions cover the prophet whose own marriage became a living picture of God's love for unfaithful Israel -- with answers and verse references after every question. They're sorted easy, medium, and hard, so young kids and Bible-savvy adults can play the same round.

Hosea is one of the most quoted-without-knowing-it books in the Old Testament: Jesus quoted it twice, Matthew quoted it about Christmas, and Paul quoted it about the resurrection. The hard round below tests whether your family can spot all four.

## What Is the Book of Hosea About?

Hosea is the story of a prophet whom God told to marry an unfaithful woman named Gomer, so that his heartbreak -- and his decision to buy her back and love her again -- would show Israel exactly how God loved them. Hosea son of Beeri preached to the northern kingdom of Israel in the 8th century BC, during its final prosperous decades before Assyria destroyed it in 722 BC. The nation had traded God for idols, and Hosea's marriage made the betrayal impossible to miss.

But the book's last word is not judgment -- it's a God who says "I will heal their waywardness and love them freely" (Hosea 14:4). For what happened to Hosea's audience when they didn't listen, read [the northern kingdom falls for kids](/blog/the-northern-kingdom-falls-for-kids); it's the sequel this book kept trying to prevent.

## Easy Hosea Trivia Questions (Ages 5-8)

1. Whom did God tell the prophet Hosea to marry? **Gomer** (Hosea 1:2-3)
2. What did Hosea's marriage act out for everyone to see? **God's faithful love for unfaithful Israel** (Hosea 1:2; 3:1)
3. Which kingdom did Hosea preach to -- the northern or the southern one? **The northern kingdom, Israel** (Hosea 1:1)
4. What does the name Hosea mean? **"Salvation"**
5. What did Hosea do when Gomer left him? **He went and bought her back, and loved her again** (Hosea 3:1-2)
6. According to Hosea 6:6, what does God desire more than sacrifice? **Mercy** (Hosea 6:6)
7. How many chapters does Hosea have? **14** (Hosea 1-14)

## Medium Hosea Trivia Questions (Ages 9-12)

8. Who was Hosea's father? **Beeri** (Hosea 1:1)
9. What were the names of Hosea's three children? **Jezreel, Lo-Ruhamah, and Lo-Ammi** (Hosea 1:4-9)
10. What does the name Lo-Ammi mean? **"Not my people"** (Hosea 1:9)
11. What price did Hosea pay to buy Gomer back? **Fifteen shekels of silver plus a homer and a lethek of barley** (Hosea 3:2)
12. Finish the warning: "They sow the wind and reap the..." **"...whirlwind"** (Hosea 8:7)
13. Complete Hosea 4:6: "My people are destroyed from lack of..." **"...knowledge"** (Hosea 4:6)
14. What tribal nickname does Hosea use for Israel throughout the book? **Ephraim** (Hosea 4:17 and throughout)
15. Matthew quotes Hosea 11:1 -- "Out of Egypt I called my son" -- about which event in Jesus' life? **His family's return from Egypt after fleeing Herod** (Matthew 2:14-15)

## Hard Hosea Trivia Questions (Teens and Adults)

16. What does the name Lo-Ruhamah mean? **"Not loved"** (Hosea 1:6)
17. Which four kings of Judah are listed in Hosea 1:1 to date his long ministry? **Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah** (Hosea 1:1)
18. Jesus told the Pharisees, "Go and learn what this means" -- quoting which Hosea verse? **Hosea 6:6, "I desire mercy, not sacrifice"** (Matthew 9:13; also 12:7)
19. Paul echoes Hosea 13:14 -- "Where, O death, is your sting?" -- in which New Testament chapter? **1 Corinthians 15 (verse 55), the resurrection chapter** (Hosea 13:14; 1 Corinthians 15:55)
20. In Hosea 11:1-4, God pictures himself as what kind of figure with young Israel? **A loving parent who taught Ephraim to walk and bent down to feed him** (Hosea 11:3-4)
21. Hosea stands first in which group of Old Testament books? **The twelve Minor Prophets** (Hosea-Malachi)
22. In Hosea 14:5, what does God say he will be like to Israel, so that he "will blossom like a lily"? **The dew** (Hosea 14:5)

## Why Does Hosea Matter for Kids?

Because it answers the question every child eventually asks: does God stop loving people when they mess up? Hosea's answer is a story kids can see -- a husband who pays to bring home the wife who left him, and children whose names change from "Not loved" and "Not my people" to "my loved one" and "my people" (Hosea 2:23). No lecture on grace lands the way those name changes do.

It's also the doorway to the Minor Prophets, the twelve short books at the end of the Old Testament that most families skip entirely. Kids who learn Hosea usually already know one Minor Prophet without realizing it -- [Jonah](/blog/who-was-jonah-for-kids) is in the same group -- and our video lessons at [Faithful Kids](https://faithfulkids.app/quiz) walk kids through this whole era of Israel's story with short animated episodes and a quiz after each one.

## What Are the Most Famous Verses in Hosea?

Hosea 6:6 -- "For I desire mercy, not sacrifice, and acknowledgment of God rather than burnt offerings" -- is the book's most famous line, largely because Jesus quoted it twice when the Pharisees criticized who he ate with and what his disciples did on the Sabbath (Matthew 9:13; 12:7). It's a strong first memory verse from the book because kids can restate it in their own words: God cares more about a kind heart than a religious performance.

Three more that reward memorizing before trivia night:

- **Hosea 11:1** -- "When Israel was a child, I loved him, and out of Egypt I called my son." Matthew applies it to Jesus' childhood (Matthew 2:15), making it one of the Old Testament's great hidden Christmas verses.
- **Hosea 8:7** -- "They sow the wind and reap the whirlwind." The Bible's original "consequences escalate" proverb, and one kids quote for life once they learn it.
- **Hosea 14:4** -- "I will heal their waywardness and love them freely." The book's destination: after fourteen chapters of heartbreak, grace gets the last word.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who wrote the book of Hosea?

The prophet Hosea, son of Beeri, who preached to the northern kingdom of Israel in the 8th century BC (Hosea 1:1). His ministry stretched across the reigns of four kings of Judah -- Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah -- making it one of the longer prophetic careers in the Bible.

### How many chapters does Hosea have?

Hosea has 14 chapters. Chapters 1-3 tell the story of Hosea's marriage to Gomer, and chapters 4-14 deliver the message that marriage illustrated -- Israel's unfaithfulness and God's relentless love.

### What is the main message of the book of Hosea?

That God's love pursues his people even when they abandon him. Hosea's costly choice to buy back his unfaithful wife (Hosea 3:1-2) pictures God's promise to heal, forgive, and restore -- "I will heal their waywardness and love them freely" (Hosea 14:4).

### Why did God tell Hosea to marry Gomer?

So the prophet's life would preach the sermon: "Go, marry a promiscuous woman... because the land is guilty of unfaithfulness to the LORD" (Hosea 1:2). Israel's idolatry was spiritual unfaithfulness, and Hosea's marriage made the nation feel what God felt.

### What age can kids start learning Hosea?

Around ages 8-10 for the storyline, told simply as "a husband who never stopped loving" -- younger kids can start with the memory verse Hosea 6:6. The marriage imagery involves unfaithfulness, so most parents frame it as a story about forgiveness and buying back what was lost.

### Where is Hosea quoted in the New Testament?

At least four notable places: Matthew 2:15 quotes Hosea 11:1 about Jesus' return from Egypt; Jesus quotes Hosea 6:6 in Matthew 9:13 and 12:7; Paul echoes Hosea 13:14 in 1 Corinthians 15:55; and both Paul and Peter draw on Hosea 2:23 -- "I will say to those called 'Not my people,' 'You are my people'" (Romans 9:25-26; 1 Peter 2:10).
