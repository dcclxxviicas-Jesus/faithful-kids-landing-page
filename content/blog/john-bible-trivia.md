These 44 John Bible trivia questions cover water turned to wine, Lazarus walking out of his tomb, the seven "I am" statements, and breakfast on the beach with the risen Jesus -- with answers and verse references after every question. They're sorted into easy, medium, and hard rounds so ages 5 through adult can play the same game.

John is a trivia goldmine because it's full of details no other Gospel records: the only Gospel that names the servant who lost his ear, counts the fish in the net (question 42), and tells us exactly what Thomas said when he stopped doubting. Fair warning on the hard round -- most adults can't answer question 35.

## What Is the Book of John About?

The Gospel of John is the fourth book of the New Testament, an eyewitness account of Jesus' life written so that readers "may believe that Jesus is the Messiah, the Son of God" (John 20:31). It's traditionally attributed to the apostle John, who never names himself in the book -- he simply calls himself "the disciple whom Jesus loved" (John 21:20-24). Instead of parables, John builds his account around seven miraculous "signs" and seven bold "I am" statements.

John is also the most quoted Gospel, home to John 3:16 and "I am the way and the truth and the life" (John 14:6). Its big idea is stated in its famous first line: the Word who was with God in the beginning "became flesh and made his dwelling among us" (John 1:1, 14) -- Jesus isn't just a teacher, he's God come to live with us.

## Easy John Trivia Questions (Ages 5-8)

1. What did Jesus turn water into at a wedding -- his very first miracle? **Wine** (John 2:9-11)
2. Who came to visit Jesus at night with big questions? **Nicodemus** (John 3:1-2)
3. Finish the most famous verse in the Bible: "For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only ___" **Son** (John 3:16)
4. What lunch did a boy share so Jesus could feed 5,000 people? **Five small barley loaves and two small fish** (John 6:9)
5. What did Jesus do on top of the stormy sea that amazed his disciples? **He walked on the water** (John 6:19)
6. Who did Jesus raise from the dead after four days in the tomb? **Lazarus** (John 11:43-44)
7. What is the shortest verse in the Bible, found in John? **"Jesus wept"** (John 11:35)
8. What did Jesus wash at the last supper to teach the disciples to serve? **Their feet** (John 13:5)
9. What animal did Jesus ride into Jerusalem? **A young donkey** (John 12:14)
10. What did the crowd wave as Jesus rode into the city? **Palm branches** (John 12:13)
11. Finish Jesus' words: "I am the good ___" **Shepherd** (John 10:11)
12. Who was the first person to see Jesus alive on Easter morning? **Mary Magdalene** (John 20:14-16)
13. Which disciple said he wouldn't believe unless he touched the nail marks? **Thomas** (John 20:25)

## What Are the Seven Signs in John?

Instead of calling them miracles, John calls Jesus' wonders "signs" -- each one points to who Jesus is. Learn these seven and you've unlocked a third of the questions above:

| # | Sign | Where |
|---|------|-------|
| 1 | Water turned to wine at Cana | John 2:1-11 |
| 2 | Healing the royal official's son | John 4:46-54 |
| 3 | Healing the man at the pool of Bethesda | John 5:1-15 |
| 4 | Feeding the 5,000 | John 6:1-14 |
| 5 | Walking on water | John 6:16-21 |
| 6 | Healing the man born blind | John 9:1-7 |
| 7 | Raising Lazarus from the dead | John 11:38-44 |

Kids love spotting the pattern: the signs get bigger and bigger, from fixing a party problem to defeating death itself. And each sign pairs with a claim -- Jesus feeds 5,000, then says "I am the bread of life" (John 6:35); he raises Lazarus after saying "I am the resurrection and the life" (John 11:25). John built his whole book so the works and the words prove each other.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who wrote the Gospel of John?

The Gospel is traditionally attributed to the apostle John, the son of Zebedee. The book itself says it was written by "the disciple whom Jesus loved," an eyewitness who leaned against Jesus at the last supper (John 13:23; 21:20-24) -- he never names himself anywhere in the book.

### How many chapters does John have?

John has 21 chapters. A simple way to remember the layout: chapters 1-12 cover Jesus' public ministry and the seven signs, chapters 13-17 are his last supper and final teaching to the disciples, and chapters 18-21 tell of the cross, the empty tomb, and the risen Jesus.

### What is the most famous verse in John?

John 3:16 -- "For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life." It's the most memorized verse in the Bible, and Jesus spoke it in a nighttime conversation with a Pharisee named Nicodemus.

### How is John different from Matthew, Mark, and Luke?

John skips the parables, the manger scene, and most of the stories the other three share, and instead records long conversations, seven signs, and seven "I am" statements found nowhere else. About 90 percent of John's material is unique to John -- which is why so many trivia questions come from it.

### What are the seven I Am statements in John?

Jesus says: I am the bread of life (John 6:35), the light of the world (8:12), the gate (10:9), the good shepherd (10:11), the resurrection and the life (11:25), the way and the truth and the life (14:6), and the true vine (15:1). Each one echoes God's name "I AM" from Exodus 3:14 -- which is why Jesus' words "before Abraham was born, I am" (John 8:58) made the crowd pick up stones.

### What age can kids start learning the Gospel of John?

Ages 5-7 can enjoy the miracle stories -- water to wine, feeding the 5,000, walking on water, and Lazarus. Ages 8-11 can follow the full storyline including the cross and resurrection, and the long teaching sections of chapters 13-17 land best with ages 12 and up.
