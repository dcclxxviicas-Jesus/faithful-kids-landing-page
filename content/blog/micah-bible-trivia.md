These 25 Micah Bible trivia questions cover the whole book -- the Bethlehem prophecy, the famous "act justly" verse, and the courtroom scene where God puts his own people on trial -- with answers and verse references after every question. They're sorted into easy (ages 5-8), medium (ages 9-12), and hard (teens and adults) rounds.

Micah is the Minor Prophet your kids already half-know without realizing it: he's the one who named Jesus' birthplace 700 years early, and his most famous verse hangs on church walls everywhere. These questions connect the dots.

## What Is the Book of Micah About?

The book of Micah is a prophecy of judgment and hope, warning Samaria and Jerusalem that their injustice would bring destruction -- while promising a ruler from Bethlehem and a future kingdom of peace. Micah came from Moresheth, a small farm town southwest of Jerusalem, and prophesied during the reigns of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah (Micah 1:1) -- making him a contemporary of Isaiah in the late 8th century BC.

Micah's name is itself a question: it means "Who is like the LORD?" -- and the book ends by answering it: "Who is a God like you, who pardons sin?" (Micah 7:18). The book swings between bad news and good news in cycles: leaders who cheat the poor will lose everything, but God will gather a remnant, hurl their sins "into the depths of the sea" (Micah 7:19), and send a shepherd-king whose origins are "from of old" (Micah 5:2).

## Why Is Micah Famous for Christmas?

Micah is famous at Christmas because Micah 5:2 predicted, seven centuries in advance, that Israel's ruler would come from the little town of Bethlehem. When [the wise men](/blog/the-wise-men-for-kids) arrived asking where the king of the Jews had been born, Herod's chief priests answered by quoting this exact verse (Matthew 2:4-6) -- which means Micah is quietly part of every nativity story your kids have ever heard about the baby [born in a manger](/blog/born-in-a-manger-for-kids).

The other verse every family should know is Micah 6:8: "Act justly, love mercy, walk humbly with your God." It's the whole Christian life in nine words, and it's one of the best verses for [kids to memorize](/blog/bible-verses-for-kids-to-memorize) -- short enough for a 6-year-old, deep enough for a lifetime.

## Easy Micah Trivia Questions (Ages 5-8)

1. What little town did Micah say Israel's great ruler would come from? **Bethlehem** (Micah 5:2)

2. Who was born in Bethlehem hundreds of years after Micah's prophecy? **Jesus** (Matthew 2:1)

3. Finish Micah's famous verse: "Act justly, love mercy, and walk humbly with your ___." **God** (Micah 6:8)

4. What does Micah's name mean? **"Who is like the LORD?"**

5. Micah 5:4 says the coming ruler will stand and do what for his flock, like a shepherd? **Shepherd them -- care for and protect them** (Micah 5:4)

6. In Micah's picture of peace, what will people beat their swords into? **Plowshares -- farm tools** (Micah 4:3)

7. Where does Micah say God will hurl all our sins? **Into the depths of the sea** (Micah 7:19)

8. Was Micah's message good news, bad news, or both? **Both -- judgment for sin, then hope and rescue** (Micah 1-7)

## Medium Micah Trivia Questions (Ages 9-12)

9. What town was Micah from? **Moresheth** (Micah 1:1)

10. Which two capital cities were Micah's prophecies about? **Samaria and Jerusalem** (Micah 1:1)

11. Name one of the three kings of Judah who reigned while Micah prophesied. **Jotham, Ahaz, or Hezekiah** (Micah 1:1)

12. Which famous prophet ministered at the same time as Micah? **Isaiah** (Isaiah 1:1, Micah 1:1)

13. According to Micah 6:8, what three things does the LORD require of you? **To act justly, to love mercy, and to walk humbly with your God** (Micah 6:8)

14. In Micah's vision of peace, everyone will sit safely under their own two plants -- which ones? **Their own vine and fig tree** (Micah 4:4)

15. What did Micah say the wicked were doing at night on their beds? **Plotting evil -- planning to seize fields and houses at morning light** (Micah 2:1-2)

16. Micah 5:2 says the Bethlehem ruler's origins are from where? **"From of old, from ancient times"** (Micah 5:2)

17. What shocking thing did Micah predict would happen to Zion (Jerusalem)? **It would be plowed like a field and become a heap of rubble** (Micah 3:12)

## Hard Micah Trivia Questions (Teens and Adults)

18. Who quoted Micah 5:2 to King Herod when the wise men came looking for the newborn king? **The chief priests and teachers of the law** (Matthew 2:4-6)

19. A century after Micah, elders quoted his "Zion will be plowed" prophecy in court -- to save which prophet's life? **Jeremiah** (Jeremiah 26:18-19)

20. In Micah 6, God presents a formal case against Israel -- and calls on what to act as witnesses? **The mountains and hills, the foundations of the earth** (Micah 6:1-2)

21. Micah 6:7 asks if God would be pleased with thousands of rams, or ten thousand rivers of -- what? **Olive oil** (Micah 6:7)

22. Which of Micah's warnings did Jesus later echo: "a man's enemies are the members of his own ___"? **Household** (Micah 7:6, Matthew 10:36)

23. In Micah 1:8, how did Micah say he would mourn for his people? **Barefoot and naked, howling like a jackal and moaning like an owl** (Micah 1:8)

24. What question does the book's final passage ask -- playing on Micah's own name? **"Who is a God like you, who pardons sin and forgives transgression?"** (Micah 7:18)

25. Micah 4:1-3 shares its swords-into-plowshares vision almost word for word with which other prophet's book? **Isaiah** (Isaiah 2:2-4)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who wrote the book of Micah?

The book of Micah is attributed to the prophet Micah of Moresheth, a town in the foothills southwest of Jerusalem. He prophesied during the reigns of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah of Judah (Micah 1:1), roughly 740-700 BC, making him a contemporary of Isaiah, Hosea, and Amos.

### How many chapters does Micah have?

Micah has 7 chapters and 105 verses. The book alternates between judgment and hope in three cycles, which is why it can feel like it changes mood suddenly -- that back-and-forth is the structure, not a mistake.

### What is the most famous verse in Micah?

Micah 6:8 is the book's most famous verse: "He has shown you, O mortal, what is good. And what does the LORD require of you? To act justly and to love mercy and to walk humbly with your God." Micah 5:2, the prophecy that Israel's ruler would come from Bethlehem, is a close second -- especially at Christmas.

### Did Micah really predict Jesus' birthplace?

Yes -- Micah 5:2 names Bethlehem as the birthplace of Israel's coming ruler about 700 years before Jesus was born there. Matthew 2:4-6 records the chief priests quoting this verse to King Herod when the wise men asked where the Messiah would be born. It's one of the most specific fulfilled prophecies in the Bible.

### What is the main message of Micah?

The main message is that God cares how people treat each other -- injustice brings judgment, but God delights to forgive those who return to him. Micah condemns leaders who cheat the poor, promises a shepherd-ruler from Bethlehem, and ends with God hurling his people's sins into the depths of the sea (Micah 7:19).

### What age can kids start learning the book of Micah?

Kids around 5-6 can start with the Bethlehem prophecy and Micah 6:8, since both connect to stories and habits they already understand. The judgment chapters and courtroom imagery work best for ages 9 and up, when kids can grasp why fairness and justice matter to God.
