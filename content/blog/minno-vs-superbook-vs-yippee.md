The short answer: **Superbook** is the best free option — CBN funds it as a ministry, so all of its animated Bible episodes cost nothing and carry no ads. **Minno** ($10.99/mo or $69.99/yr) has the biggest and most varied Christian streaming library, including What's in the Bible? and VeggieTales classics — the closest thing to a Christian Netflix. **Yippee TV** ($7.99/mo or $49/yr) is the cheapest paid option and the exclusive streaming home of The VeggieTales Show, best for general wholesome screen time rather than Bible teaching. All three are entertainment platforms: kids watch, and that is the whole loop. If your goal is Bible *learning* — where a child watches a story and then proves they understood it — none of the three does that, and we cover the honest fourth option at the end.

## Superbook: The Best Free Option

Superbook is CBN's flagship kids' ministry: a high-production animated series in which two kids and their robot travel back into Bible events. The entire library — roughly 68 episodes across five seasons — is **free** in the Superbook Kids app, with a kid-friendly Bible, devotional "Quests," and games alongside. Nothing else in this category is fully free, and for a lot of families that settles the comparison by itself.

The honest trade-offs: episodes run about 25 minutes, which is a real commitment for a school night; the time-travel framing (two modern kids and a robot inside the story) is fiction wrapped around Scripture, which some families love and others would rather skip; and coverage is the major stories, not the whole Bible. Our full [Superbook vs Faithful Kids](/blog/superbook-vs-faithful-kids) comparison goes deeper on how it teaches.

**Choose Superbook if:** you want genuinely good animated Bible content at zero cost, and your kids are in the 6–10 range where the adventure framing lands best.

## Minno: The Biggest Library

Minno (formerly JellyTelly) is the closest thing to a Christian Netflix: hundreds of episodes across dozens of shows, including VeggieTales classics, Phil Vischer's What's in the Bible? — one of the best kids' theology series ever made — and Minno originals like the Laugh and Grow Bible. It runs $10.99/month or $69.99/year with a 7-day free trial, and it works on practically every screen in the house.

The trade-offs: it is the most expensive of the three month-to-month; quality varies show to show; and it is a browse-and-watch platform — there is no structure deciding what a child watches next and no check on what they took in. Our [Minno vs Faithful Kids](/blog/minno-vs-faithful-kids) comparison covers where streaming ends and learning starts, and our [Minno review](/blog/minno-review) rates the service on its own terms.

**Choose Minno if:** you want maximum variety for family screen time, and What's in the Bible? alone justifies it for families who want real theology in an entertaining wrapper.

## Yippee TV: The VeggieTales Home

Yippee TV's headline is exclusivity: it is the only place to stream The VeggieTales Show, and it holds the largest VeggieTales collection anywhere, alongside shows like Hermie and Friends, Jay Jay the Jet Plane, Danny Go!, and the older Superbook series. At $49/year (about $4.08/month) it is the cheapest paid option here by a wide margin.

The trade-offs: the catalog skews toward general wholesome entertainment rather than Bible teaching — it is the platform you pick to *replace Netflix time*, not to replace Sunday school; and like the others, viewing is passive. Our [Yippee TV vs Faithful Kids](/blog/yippee-tv-vs-faithful-kids) comparison has the full breakdown.

**Choose Yippee if:** VeggieTales is the draw, your kids are under 10, and the goal is safe entertainment at the lowest price.

## Head-to-Head Verdicts

**Minno vs Superbook:** different jobs. Superbook is free, Bible-only, and best 6–10; Minno is paid, far broader, and stretches from toddlers to preteens. If the question is "Bible stories, well animated, no cost" — Superbook. If it's "replace our streaming service with a Christian one" — Minno.

**Yippee vs Minno:** the closest matchup, since both are general Christian streaming. Minno has the bigger, more Bible-forward library (What's in the Bible? is the difference-maker); Yippee has The VeggieTales Show exclusive and costs $21 less per year. VeggieTales-first families pick Yippee; everyone else gets more from Minno.

**Minno vs Angel Studios:** Angel's kids content (like the Tuttle Twins) sits inside a general-audience platform built around its pay-it-forward model, not a walled kids' garden. For a self-contained kids-only environment, Minno and Yippee are the safer shape.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Superbook really completely free?

Yes. The Superbook Kids app — all five seasons of the series, the kid-friendly Bible, devotionals, and games — is free and ad-free, funded by CBN as a ministry. CBN also sells a separate Superbook Academy curriculum subscription for churches and homeschools, but the app your child would actually use costs nothing.

### Which platform has VeggieTales?

Both, differently. Yippee TV is the exclusive streaming home of The VeggieTales Show (the 2019 revival) and carries the largest VeggieTales collection anywhere. Minno carries classic VeggieTales episodes alongside its broader catalog. If new VeggieTales is the priority, that is Yippee; if VeggieTales is one ingredient among many, Minno.

### Is Superbook biblically accurate?

The Bible stories themselves stay close to the text, but every episode wraps them in a fictional frame: two modern kids and a robot time-travel into the events and interact with them. Kids reliably understand the difference between the frame and the story, and each episode points back to the real Scripture — but families who want zero fictional additions should know the frame is central to the format.
