These 27 Nehemiah Bible trivia questions cover the cupbearer who rebuilt Jerusalem's walls in 52 days -- his midnight ride, his sword-carrying builders, and his enemies' failed tricks. Answers with verse references follow every question, sorted into easy, medium, and hard rounds by age.

Nehemiah might be the most kid-friendly leadership story in the Old Testament: a man with a safe palace job hears his hometown is in ruins, prays for four months, and then risks everything to fix it. Half these questions double as conversation starters about courage, prayer, and finishing what you start.

## What Is the Book of Nehemiah About?

The book of Nehemiah tells how a Jewish cupbearer to the king of Persia led the rebuilding of Jerusalem's broken walls in just 52 days, then helped rebuild the people's faith around God's law. Much of it is Nehemiah's own first-person memoir, set around 445 BC -- about 13 years after Ezra arrived in Jerusalem -- and in the Hebrew Bible, Ezra and Nehemiah were originally one book.

The story has two halves: chapters 1-7 cover the wall -- the prayer, the night inspection, the builders working with weapons in hand, and the enemies Sanballat, Tobiah, and Geshem trying everything to stop it -- and chapters 8-13 cover the revival, when Ezra reads the Law aloud and the people recommit to God. It matters because it's the Bible's best case study in praying AND acting: "We prayed to our God and posted a guard" (Nehemiah 4:9).

## Easy Nehemiah Trivia Questions (Ages 5-8)

1. What was Nehemiah's job in the palace of the king of Persia? **Cupbearer to the king** (Nehemiah 1:11)

2. What sad news made Nehemiah cry? **Jerusalem's walls were broken down and its gates burned** (Nehemiah 1:3-4)

3. What did Nehemiah do first when he heard the bad news? **He sat down and wept, fasted, and prayed to God** (Nehemiah 1:4)

4. What did Nehemiah go to Jerusalem to rebuild? **The city walls** (Nehemiah 2:17)

5. What did the builders hold in one hand while they worked with the other? **A weapon -- and every builder wore a sword at his side** (Nehemiah 4:17-18)

6. How many days did it take to finish the whole wall? **Fifty-two days** (Nehemiah 6:15)

7. Who read God's Law out loud to all the people after the wall was done? **Ezra the scribe** (Nehemiah 8:2-3)

8. Finish this famous line Nehemiah's helpers told the people: "Do not grieve, for the joy of the LORD is your..." **"...strength"** (Nehemiah 8:10)

9. When Nehemiah's enemies made fun of the wall and tried to scare the workers, did the people quit? **No -- they kept building, and God helped them finish** (Nehemiah 4:6; 6:15-16)

## Medium Nehemiah Trivia Questions (Ages 9-12)

10. Which king did Nehemiah serve, and what did the king notice about him? **King Artaxerxes noticed Nehemiah's face was sad, which was dangerous in front of a king** (Nehemiah 2:1-2)

11. What did Nehemiah do in the split second between the king's question and his answer? **He prayed to the God of heaven** (Nehemiah 2:4)

12. How did Nehemiah first inspect the broken walls? **Secretly, riding through the ruins at night with a few men, telling no one his plan** (Nehemiah 2:12-16)

13. Who were the three main enemies who opposed the rebuilding? **Sanballat the Horonite, Tobiah the Ammonite, and Geshem the Arab** (Nehemiah 2:19)

14. What insult did Tobiah throw at the builders' work? **"If even a fox climbed up on it, he would break down their wall of stones!"** (Nehemiah 4:3)

15. What two things did Nehemiah's people do when their enemies plotted to attack? **"We prayed to our God and posted a guard day and night"** (Nehemiah 4:9)

16. Who was Nehemiah's brother, who first brought him the news about Jerusalem? **Hanani** (Nehemiah 1:2)

17. What did Nehemiah confront the rich Jews about in chapter 5? **Charging their own poor countrymen interest and taking their fields and children as payment -- he made them give it all back** (Nehemiah 5:6-12)

18. When enemies invited Nehemiah to a "meeting" in the plain of Ono, what was his famous reply? **"I am carrying on a great project and cannot go down"** (Nehemiah 6:3)

19. How did the enemies react when the wall was finished? **They lost their self-confidence, because they realized the work had been done with the help of God** (Nehemiah 6:16)

20. What did the people do when Ezra opened the Book of the Law? **They all stood up, and they listened from daybreak till noon** (Nehemiah 8:3, 5)

## Hard Nehemiah Trivia Questions (Teens and Adults)

21. In which Persian city, and in which year of the king's reign, does the book open? **Susa (the citadel), in the twentieth year of Artaxerxes** (Nehemiah 1:1; 2:1)

22. How many times did Sanballat send the same invitation to Ono -- and what was different about the final message? **Four times the same message, and the fifth time an unsealed letter accusing Nehemiah of plotting to become king** (Nehemiah 6:4-6)

23. What trap did the hired prophet Shemaiah set for Nehemiah? **He urged Nehemiah to hide from assassins inside the temple -- which would have been sin and discredited him -- and Nehemiah refused: "Should a man like me run away?"** (Nehemiah 6:10-13)

24. What perk of being governor did Nehemiah refuse for twelve years, out of reverence for God? **The governor's food allowance that earlier governors had taken from the people** (Nehemiah 5:14-18)

25. Which feast did the people celebrate by building leafy shelters -- something not done like that since the days of Joshua? **The Feast of Tabernacles (Booths)** (Nehemiah 8:14-17)

26. How was the completed wall dedicated? **With two large choirs that walked in opposite directions on top of the wall and met at the temple, with music so loud the joy could be heard far away** (Nehemiah 12:31, 38-43)

27. When Nehemiah returned for a second term and found men married to foreign idol-worshipers, what did he do -- and what is the book's final sentence? **He rebuked them, beat some, and pulled out their hair; the book ends, "Remember me with favor, my God"** (Nehemiah 13:25, 31)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who wrote the book of Nehemiah?

Much of the book is Nehemiah's own first-person memoir ("the words of Nehemiah son of Hakaliah," Nehemiah 1:1), and Jewish tradition credits Ezra with compiling Ezra-Nehemiah into its final form. The two books were originally one in the Hebrew Bible.

### How many chapters does Nehemiah have?

Nehemiah has 13 chapters -- roughly, chapters 1-7 rebuild the wall and chapters 8-13 rebuild the people through the reading of God's Law, confession, and reform.

### How long did it take Nehemiah to build the wall?

Fifty-two days (Nehemiah 6:15) -- a speed so shocking that even Jerusalem's enemies concluded "this work had been done with the help of our God" (Nehemiah 6:16).

### What is the most famous verse in Nehemiah?

Nehemiah 8:10 -- "Do not grieve, for the joy of the LORD is your strength." Close behind is Nehemiah 6:3: "I am carrying on a great project and cannot go down."

### Was Nehemiah a prophet or a king?

Neither -- Nehemiah was a layman: cupbearer to King Artaxerxes of Persia, then governor of Judah for twelve years (Nehemiah 5:14). That's part of the book's appeal for kids: God used an ordinary employee with a burden and a prayer life.

### What age can kids start learning Nehemiah?

Ages 5 and up can enjoy the core story -- a brave man rebuilds a broken wall while enemies try to stop him -- and it's a favorite for teaching perseverance. The economic reforms of chapter 5 and the confrontations of chapter 13 make better material for ages 10 and up.
