The most powerful thing you can do for your child today is pray for them -- and you don't need perfect words to do it. Below are 18 original prayers written for parents to pray over their kids, covering protection, character, faith, friendships, school, health, their future spouse, and the seasons when a child wanders from God.

These aren't prayers to pray with your child (though some work that way too). They're for the quiet moments -- over a sleeping toddler, in the car line, at 2 a.m. when worry won't let you sleep. For building a prayer habit alongside your kids, our guide on [how to pray with kids](/blog/how-to-pray-with-kids) covers that side.

## Prayers for Protection

### 1. A Daily Covering Prayer
"Father, today I place my child in Your hands, because Yours are stronger than mine. Protect their body from harm, their mind from lies, and their heart from wounds I can't see coming. Go where I can't go and see what I can't see. I trust You with them. Amen."

### 2. A Prayer Against Fear
"Lord, when my child is afraid -- of the dark, of failing, of being left out -- remind them that You said, 'Do not fear, for I am with you' (Isaiah 41:10). And Lord, while You're at it, calm my fears about them too. Half my prayers for their protection are really prayers about my own anxiety. Give us both Your peace. Amen."

### 3. A Prayer for Protection Online
"Father, my child is growing up in a world I never had to navigate at their age. Guard their eyes from what they can't unsee and their heart from voices that lie about who they are. Give me wisdom to set boundaries with love, and give them the instinct to walk away from what poisons. Amen."

## Prayers for Character

### 4. A Prayer for Honesty
"Lord, make my child a truth-teller -- even when the truth is embarrassing, even when a lie would be easier. Build in them the deep security that comes from having nothing to hide. And make me the kind of parent who is safe to tell the truth to. Amen."

### 5. A Prayer for Kindness and Courage Together
"Father, give my child a soft heart and a strong spine. Make them kind to the kid eating lunch alone and brave enough to stand up when something's wrong -- even if they stand alone. Kindness without courage folds; courage without kindness wounds. Grow both in them. Amen."

### 6. A Prayer for Humility
"Lord, keep my child teachable. Let them win without gloating, lose without crumbling, and apologize without being cornered into it. 'Clothe yourselves with humility' (1 Peter 5:5) -- start the outfit early, Father. Amen."

### 7. A Prayer for Perseverance
"Father, I can't hand my child grit -- but You can grow it. When schoolwork is hard, when they get cut from the team, when the first try fails, teach them that quitting isn't the only exit. Let every small hard thing now build the endurance they'll need for the big hard things later. Amen."

## Prayers for Faith

### 8. A Prayer for a Real, Personal Faith
"Lord, I don't want my child to inherit my faith like a piece of furniture. I want them to meet You for themselves. Make Yourself real to them -- in a verse that jumps off the page, in an answered prayer, in a moment I could never manufacture. Let their faith be theirs, not a hand-me-down. Amen."

### 9. A Prayer for a Love of God's Word
"Father, plant Your Word deep in my child while the soil is soft. Let the stories they learn now become the anchors they hold later. 'Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path' (Psalm 119:105) -- light their path early, Lord. Amen."

### 10. A Prayer for Their Questions and Doubts
"Lord, my child will one day ask questions I can't answer. When doubt comes, don't let it scare them -- or me. Turn every honest question into a doorway toward You instead of away from You. Give me the grace to say 'I don't know, let's find out' instead of pretending. Amen."

## Prayers for Friendships

### 11. A Prayer for Good Friends
"Father, You said, 'Walk with the wise and become wise' (Proverbs 13:20). Bring my child friends who make them braver, kinder, and more themselves -- and give them the discernment to notice which friends leave them smaller. And make my child that kind of good friend to someone else. Amen."

### 12. A Prayer for the Lonely Seasons
"Lord, when my child eats alone, gets left off the invite list, or watches a best friend move away -- be nearer than the loneliness. Use those seasons to teach them that Your presence is real and that their worth was never up for a vote. Then, in Your kindness, send a friend. Amen."

## Prayers for School

### 13. A Prayer for Learning
"Father, You made my child's mind, and You know exactly how it works -- where it races and where it struggles. Give them curiosity that outlasts frustration, teachers who see their potential, and the freedom to be a learner instead of a performer. Their worth is not a report card. Help us both believe that. Amen."

### 14. A Prayer Before a Hard Day
"Lord, today holds something hard for my child -- a test, a tryout, a conversation, a first day. Go ahead of them into that room. Steady their hands, clear their mind, and whether today ends in victory or disappointment, let them come home knowing they are loved from the rooftops. Amen."

## Prayers for Health

### 15. A Prayer for a Healthy Child
"Father, thank You for this healthy body I get to feed, bathe, and buckle into car seats. I don't take one ordinary day of health for granted. Keep growing them strong, and let this body serve You for a long lifetime. Amen."

### 16. A Prayer for a Sick or Struggling Child
"Lord, my child is suffering and I would trade places in a heartbeat -- which gives me a glimpse of how You love us. I ask plainly: heal them. Guide every doctor, every test, every treatment. You are 'close to the brokenhearted' (Psalm 34:18) -- be close to them in the waiting, and to me, because I am not okay. Amen."

## A Prayer for Their Future Spouse

### 17. The Someday Prayer
"Father, somewhere out there may be a child who will one day marry mine. Watch over them tonight like I'd want their parents watching over my child, and grow them in kindness and faith. Prepare my child, too, to be worth marrying -- faithful, gentle, quick to forgive. And if Your plan for my child is singleness, make it rich and purposeful. Either way, write their story better than I would. Amen."

## Prayers for Prodigal Seasons

If your child has walked away from God -- whether they're 15 or 45 -- these are for you. You are not alone, this is not necessarily the end of the story, and the father in Luke 15 is proof that watching the road is an act of faith.

### 18. A Prayer for a Child Far from God
"Father, my child is far from You right now, and my heart is broken in a way I can't explain to people whose kids are fine. But I remember this: You love them even more than I do, and no one is beyond Your reach. Keep pursuing them in places I'm not allowed into. Send people whose words can land where mine bounce off. Keep my love warm and my door open, so that when they turn around -- and Lord, I'm asking that they turn around -- home is easy to find. I'm still watching the road. Amen."

If this is your season, release yourself from the math of guilt -- faithful parents have prodigal kids, and the parable's father did nothing wrong. Keep praying, keep loving, and refuse ultimatums that close doors. Our post on [how to raise godly kids](/blog/how-to-raise-godly-kids) is honest about what parents can and can't control.

## How Do You Pray Scripture Over Your Kids?

Praying Scripture means taking a Bible verse and turning it into a prayer with your child's name in it -- it's the simplest way to pray big prayers when your own words run dry. You're not performing a ritual; you're agreeing with what God already said He wants.

The method takes ten seconds: read the verse, swap in your child's name, and pray it. Here are seven verses that convert beautifully:

| Verse | Prayed over your child |
|---|---|
| Philippians 1:6 | "Lord, You began a good work in Emma -- carry it on to completion." |
| Proverbs 3:5-6 | "Teach Jack to trust You with all his heart and not lean on his own understanding." |
| Luke 2:52 | "Let Mia grow in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and man." |
| Ephesians 3:17-18 | "Root and establish Noah in love; let him grasp how wide and deep Your love is." |
| Psalm 119:105 | "Make Your Word a lamp for Ava's feet and a light on her path." |
| 1 Timothy 4:12 | "Even though Eli is young, let him set an example in speech, conduct, love, and faith." |
| Numbers 6:24-26 | "Bless Lily and keep her; make Your face shine on her and give her peace." |

To build the habit, attach it to something you already do daily -- the school drop-off line, or each kid's door at lights-out -- and pick one verse per child per season rather than rotating randomly. Verses about home life work especially well; our list of [Bible verses about family for kids](/blog/bible-verses-about-family-for-kids) doubles nicely as a praying-parent list.

And remember that praying for your kids pairs with discipling them, not replaces it. The daily rhythms -- Scripture, conversation, modeling -- are covered in our [Christian parenting 101 guide to biblical principles](/blog/christian-parenting-101-biblical-principles). Some families fold in tools here too; the video lessons at [Faithful Kids](https://faithfulkids.app/quiz) give kids the Bible stories and quizzes while you handle the praying.

## What If I Don't Know What to Pray for My Child?

Start with one sentence: "Father, You know what my child needs today -- please do it." That's a complete prayer, and Romans 8:26 says the Spirit intercedes when we don't know what to pray. From there, three prompts always give you material: what your child is facing this week, what you're worried about (that's usually a prayer wearing a disguise), and what you hope they become. Consistency beats eloquence -- one honest sentence at the bedroom door every day outweighs a beautiful prayer once a month.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a short daily prayer I can pray for my child?
"Father, protect their body, guard their heart, and draw them close to You today. Amen." One sentence, ten seconds, and it covers protection, character, and faith. Attach it to a daily trigger -- the school drop-off, their bedroom door at night -- so it actually happens.

### Does praying for my child really make a difference?
Yes -- Scripture consistently calls believers to intercede, and James 5:16 says "the prayer of a righteous person is powerful and effective." Prayer isn't a formula that overrides your child's free will, but it invites God's activity into their life -- and it changes you: praying parents worry less and notice more.

### How do I pray for a child who has walked away from faith?
Pray persistently for God to pursue them, send other voices into their life, and keep your relationship warm so the road home stays open. Avoid weaponizing prayer ("I'm praying you'll see how wrong you are") -- pray for them, not at them. The father in Luke 15 watched the road without chasing or condemning, and that's the posture to ask God for.

### Should I pray these prayers out loud over my child or privately?
Both have their place. Private prayer lets you be fully honest, including about your fears; praying aloud -- a blessing at bedtime, a hand on their shoulder before a hard day -- lets kids hear they're prayed for, which they never forget. A simple split: honest prayers in private, blessing prayers out loud.

### What scriptures are best to pray over my kids?
Philippians 1:6, Proverbs 3:5-6, Luke 2:52, Numbers 6:24-26, and Psalm 119:105 are the classic starting five. Insert your child's name into the verse and pray it as written. Pick one per child and pray it daily for a season rather than rotating randomly -- repetition is what turns a verse into a lifelong banner over that kid.

### When should I start praying for my child?
Now -- it is never too early or too late. Hannah prayed for Samuel before he was conceived (1 Samuel 1), and the prodigal's father hoped on behalf of a grown man. Whether your child is unborn, seven, or thirty-seven, today is the right day to start.
