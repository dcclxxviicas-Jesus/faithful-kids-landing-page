These 40 Proverbs Bible trivia questions cover the Bible's book of wisdom -- ants and sluggards, gold rings in pigs' snouts, and the most quoted parenting verse in Scripture -- sorted into easy, medium, and hard rounds. Every answer comes with its verse reference so you can check it on the spot.

Proverbs is a trivia goldmine because it's built out of one-liners: short, vivid, and often funny on purpose. Kids remember "go to the ant, you sluggard" for life -- and the hard round hides a few facts about the book itself that most adults have never noticed.

## What Is the Book of Proverbs About?

The book of Proverbs is a collection of short, memorable sayings that teach wisdom -- how to live skillfully with God, family, money, words, and work. Most of it comes from King Solomon, the wisest man of his era (Proverbs 1:1), who spoke three thousand proverbs in his lifetime (1 Kings 4:32), though the book also includes sayings from Agur, King Lemuel, and other unnamed wise teachers.

Its theme verse sits right at the front: "The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge" (Proverbs 1:7). With 31 chapters -- famously one for each day of the month -- Proverbs matters because it's the Bible's most practical book: almost every verse can change how a kid acts on an ordinary Tuesday.

## Easy Proverbs Trivia Questions (Ages 5-8)

1. Which famously wise king wrote most of Proverbs? **Solomon** (Proverbs 1:1)
2. "Trust in the LORD with all your ___." **Heart** (Proverbs 3:5)
3. Which tiny insect does Proverbs tell lazy people to watch and learn from? **The ant** -- "Go to the ant, you sluggard" (Proverbs 6:6)
4. "A gentle answer turns away ___." **Wrath** (Proverbs 15:1)
5. What kind of heart "is good medicine"? **A cheerful heart** (Proverbs 17:22)
6. "A friend loves at ___ ___." **All times** (Proverbs 17:17)
7. What goes before destruction? **Pride** -- "and a haughty spirit before a fall" (Proverbs 16:18)
8. When God offered Solomon anything he wanted, what did he ask for? **Wisdom** -- a discerning heart (1 Kings 3:9)
9. What does Proverbs 22:1 say is more desirable than great riches? **A good name** (Proverbs 22:1)
10. "The fear of the LORD is the beginning of ___." **Knowledge** (Proverbs 1:7)
11. What sharpens iron? **Iron** -- "as iron sharpens iron, so one person sharpens another" (Proverbs 27:17)
12. How many chapters does Proverbs have -- one for each day of the month? **31**
13. Above all else, what does Proverbs 4:23 tell you to guard? **Your heart** -- "for everything you do flows from it" (Proverbs 4:23)

## Medium Proverbs Trivia Questions (Ages 9-12)

14. In Proverbs 1 and 8, wisdom is pictured as what, calling out in the streets? **A woman** (Proverbs 1:20-21)
15. How many proverbs did Solomon speak in his lifetime? **Three thousand** (1 Kings 4:32)
16. "The name of the LORD is a fortified ___; the righteous run to it and are safe." **Tower** (Proverbs 18:10)

19. Proverbs 20:1 calls wine a what? **A mocker** -- "and beer a brawler" (Proverbs 20:1)
20. What simple meal does Proverbs say is better than a fattened calf served with hatred? **A small serving of vegetables with love** (Proverbs 15:17)
21. Humans plan their course in their hearts, but who establishes their steps? **The LORD** (Proverbs 16:9)
22. What does Proverbs 16:31 call gray hair? **A crown of splendor** (Proverbs 16:31)
23. "Walk with the wise and become wise," but a companion of fools does what? **Suffers harm** (Proverbs 13:20)
24. Proverbs 25:11 compares the right word at the right time to what beautiful picture? **Apples of gold in settings of silver** (Proverbs 25:11)
25. What does Proverbs 27:1 warn you not to boast about? **Tomorrow** -- "for you do not know what a day may bring" (Proverbs 27:1)
26. Proverbs 18:24 says there is a friend who sticks closer than a what? **A brother** (Proverbs 18:24)
27. What animal is made ready for the day of battle, though victory rests with the LORD? **The horse** (Proverbs 21:31)

## Hard Proverbs Trivia Questions (Teens and Adults)

28. Proverbs 6 lists things the LORD hates. How many? **Six -- seven that are detestable to him** (Proverbs 6:16-19)
29. Name two of the four small-but-extremely-wise creatures in Proverbs 30. **Ants, hyraxes (rock badgers), locusts, and lizards** (Proverbs 30:24-28)
30. Besides Solomon, which two named men contributed sayings to Proverbs? **Agur son of Jakeh (chapter 30) and King Lemuel (chapter 31)**
31. Who taught King Lemuel the sayings in Proverbs 31? **His mother** (Proverbs 31:1)
32. Which king's men copied out Proverbs 25-29 about 250 years after Solomon? **Hezekiah's** (Proverbs 25:1)
33. Even fools are thought wise if they do what? **Keep silent** (Proverbs 17:28)
34. As a dog returns to its vomit, so a fool returns to what? **His folly** (Proverbs 26:11 -- quoted in 2 Peter 2:22)
35. Proverbs 11:22 compares beauty without discretion to what strange picture? **A gold ring in a pig's snout** (Proverbs 11:22)
36. Giving your hungry enemy food and water heaps what on his head? **Burning coals** (Proverbs 25:21-22 -- quoted in Romans 12:20)
37. Agur asked God for neither poverty nor riches, but only what? **His daily bread** (Proverbs 30:8)
38. Proverbs 31:10-31 is an acrostic poem. What does each of its 22 verses begin with? **A successive letter of the Hebrew alphabet**
39. According to Proverbs 12:10, the righteous care for the needs of their what? **Their animals** (Proverbs 12:10)
40. Which two back-to-back verses famously seem to give opposite advice about answering a fool? **Proverbs 26:4 and 26:5** -- "Do not answer a fool according to his folly" and "Answer a fool according to his folly"

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who wrote the book of Proverbs?

Most of Proverbs comes from King Solomon (Proverbs 1:1), who spoke three thousand proverbs in his lifetime (1 Kings 4:32). The book also includes sayings from unnamed wise teachers (Proverbs 22:17), Agur son of Jakeh (chapter 30), and King Lemuel (chapter 31), and chapters 25-29 were compiled by King Hezekiah's men long after Solomon died.

### How many chapters does Proverbs have?

Proverbs has 31 chapters, which is why so many families read one chapter a day for a month -- the chapter number matches the date. It's one of the easiest Bible reading habits to start with kids.

### What is the most famous verse in Proverbs?

Proverbs 3:5-6 -- "Trust in the LORD with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding" -- is the book's most famous passage and one of the most memorized verses in the Bible. Proverbs 22:6 ("Start children off on the way they should go") is the best-known parenting verse.

### What is the main message of Proverbs?

The main message is that wisdom starts with honoring God: "The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge" (Proverbs 1:7). Everything else in the book -- advice about words, money, work, friends, and anger -- flows from that one starting point.

### What age can kids start learning Proverbs?

Ages 5-8 can memorize single proverbs like "A gentle answer turns away wrath" (Proverbs 15:1) because they're short and concrete. Ages 9-12 can handle the comparisons and word pictures, and teens are ready for the money, friendship, and self-control themes that run through the whole book.

### What is the difference between Proverbs and Psalms?

Psalms is Israel's songbook -- prayers and praises addressed to God -- while Proverbs is a wisdom manual addressed to people, especially the young. A simple way to tell kids: Psalms teaches you how to talk to God, and Proverbs teaches you how to live for him.
