Rejected in Nazareth is a Bible story from Matthew/Mark/Luke/John in the New Testament. This retelling is designed for kids ages 5 and up, using simple language they can understand and lessons they will remember.

## What Is the Story of Rejected in Nazareth for Kids?

JESUS BEGINS HIS MINISTRY — EPISODE 5 "Rejected in Nazareth" Based on Luke 4:14-30

I went home. Back to Nazareth — the town where I grew up, where people watched me play in the streets as a kid, where they knew me as the carpenter's son. I walked into the synagogue on the Sabbath, just like I'd done my whole life. But this time was different.

They handed me the scroll of Isaiah. I unrolled it and found the exact passage I was looking for. Then I read these words out loud: "The Spirit of the Lord is on me, because He has anointed me to proclaim good news to the poor. He has sent me to proclaim freedom for the prisoners and recovery of sight for the blind, to set the oppressed free, to proclaim the year of the Lord's favor."

Every eye in the room was locked on me. I rolled up the scroll, handed it back, sat down, and said one sentence that changed EVERYTHING: "Today this Scripture is fulfilled in your hearing."

This story comes from Matthew/Mark/Luke/John and is part of the New Testament.

## Parent's Guide: Why This Story Matters

Every Bible story is an opportunity to talk with your child about faith in a natural, low-pressure way. After watching, ask an open-ended question like 'What was your favorite part?' or 'What do you think God was trying to teach us?' These conversations do not need to be long or perfect. The goal is simply to make the Bible feel like a living, relevant part of your family's life, not something dusty on a shelf.

## Quiz: Test What Your Child Learned

After watching the video, see if your child can answer these questions:

### What was Nazareth to Jesus?

**His hometown where He grew up.** 

### Which prophet's scroll did Jesus read from in the synagogue?

**Isaiah.**

### What did Jesus say after reading the Scripture?

**"Today this Scripture is fulfilled in your hearing".**

### Why did the people in the synagogue become furious?

**Jesus reminded them that God sent prophets to help foreigners, not just Israelites.**

### What happened when the crowd tried to throw Jesus off a cliff?

**He walked right through the crowd and went on His way.**

## Discussion Questions for Families

Use these questions at the dinner table or during bedtime:

1. What was your favorite part of the story of Rejected in Nazareth?
2. What do you think God was teaching us through this story?
3. How can we be more like Rejected in our own lives?
4. If you were in this story, what would you have done differently?
5. What does this story teach us about trusting God?
