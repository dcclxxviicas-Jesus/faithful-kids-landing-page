Remote Christian jobs are real, but the lists recommending them are full of stale reputations: the most famous "remote Christian tech employer" had zero remote openings on its own board the day we checked, while a Nashville publishing house most people picture as office-bound turns out to run a work-from-anywhere culture. So this guide was verified the only way that works: every employer's remote policy below is quoted from its own careers pages, and every job board was opened and checked on November 16, 2026.

(Disclosure: we make Faithful Kids, a Bible app for kids. We're a tiny team not hiring staff; the ways to work with us are at the end.)

## Employers With Verified Remote Policies

**Compassion International** is the strongest remote story in Christian work, in its own FAQ's words: "We're a remote-first workforce, and as such, most of our positions are 100% remote." Pair that with verified benefits few secular employers match: a 10% retirement contribution on top of salary, minimum 25 paid days off plus 15 holidays, and a full-time chaplain. Their ask: candidates qualified in their field with "an active spiritual life." Live remote US roles at our check included senior engineering and grants positions. [Careers](https://www.compassion.com/about-us/careers/).

**Lifeway Christian Resources** states a "strong Work from Anywhere (WFA) culture," with most of its sixteen current openings US-remote at our check (editorial, IT security, Salesforce, Oracle roles) and travel provided for team gatherings. [Careers](https://www.lifeway.com/about/careers).

**Faithlife / Logos Bible Software** describes a "remote-friendly workforce," and every one of its four openings was remote-tagged at our check, including a senior account manager role with a posted $80-90k base. Four openings is a small slate; watch the page. [logos.com/careers](https://www.logos.com/careers).

**World Vision US** says it directly on its benefits page: "Choose from hybrid or remote roles and achieve a healthy work-life balance." Note the hiring bar, also stated directly: agreement with their Statement of Faith or the Apostles' Creed. [Careers](https://www.worldvision.org/careers).

**CBN** lists "remote working options" among its benefits, alongside daily chapel as part of the paid workday and paid mission-trip leave; one featured US-remote producer role was visible at our check. [cbn.com/careers](https://cbn.com/careers).

**Interstate Batteries** posts a handful of field-sales roles as "Remote Office" by territory; its corporate and tech roles are Dallas-based. Worth knowing because it had 102 open jobs at our check, the most of any faith-forward employer we verified. [Careers](https://www.interstatebatteries.com/about/join-our-team).

## The Reputation Corrections

Two findings that contradict what most lists say, both from the employers' own pages on November 16, 2026:

- **Life.Church / YouVersion**: famous as the remote-friendly Christian tech employer, but every one of its 47 current openings, including all YouVersion engineering and product roles, was marked on-site in Edmond, Oklahoma. Apply if Oklahoma works for you; don't apply expecting remote.
- **Ramsey Solutions** is deliberately not remote, by design and in its own words: 900+ team members "under one roof" in Franklin, Tennessee.

## The Job Boards, Checked

- **[ChristianTechJobs.io](https://christiantechjobs.io)**: the best genuine remote filter we found for marketplace roles (tech, design, marketing at Christian orgs), advertising that most of its listings are remote; live examples at our check included Compassion's senior AWS engineer role. The first batch of listings is free to browse; full access is paid.
- **[ChristianCareerCenter.com](https://www.christiancareercenter.com/states/remote-jobs/)**: the broadest church-plus-marketplace mix, running since 1997, with a dedicated remote page (five remote listings the day we checked, plus a hybrid category).
- **[ChurchStaffing.com](https://www.churchstaffing.com)** and **[ChristianJobs.com](https://www.christianjobs.com/jobs)**: the big ministry boards (ChurchStaffing alone had 160+ worship and youth roles). Both include remote listings by default with an exclude-remote checkbox; basic accounts free, premium around $15/month.
- **[JustChurchJobs.com](https://www.justchurchjobs.com)**: free, church roles only, 426 listings at our check, no remote filter, because church staff work is overwhelmingly location-tied, which is the honest caveat for this whole category.
- **[MinistryJobs.com](https://www.ministryjobs.com)**: ministry-only board with healthy category volume; no remote filter visible.
- **[Vanderbloemen](https://www.vanderbloemen.com/job-openings/)**: an executive search firm rather than a board; its open searches (five at our check) are senior, location-tied roles.

## Remote Teaching for Christian Schools

Online Christian academies hire remote teachers, and one was verifiably hiring at our check: **Veritas Scholars Academy** listed openings tagged "Fully remote" in its own words, including a contract teacher role, via [veritaspress.com/employment](https://veritaspress.com/employment). Enlightium Academy's careers page describes some positions as entirely remote-capable, though its main site was unreachable to our checker, so verify directly. If you hold a teaching background, this is the most reliably remote corner of Christian work after the big ministries.

## The Honest Shape of the Market

Remote Christian work clusters into four lanes: ministry operations and development (Compassion, World Vision), Christian publishing and media (Lifeway, CBN), Christian tech (Faithlife, Compassion engineering; not YouVersion right now), and online teaching (Veritas, Enlightium). The bulk of listings on every ministry board, pastoral and worship roles, are tied to a building full of people, and no filter changes that. If you need fully remote, aim at the four lanes rather than scrolling church boards.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Christian companies hire remote workers?

Verified on their own pages in November 2026: Compassion International (remote-first, most positions 100% remote), Lifeway (work-from-anywhere culture), Faithlife/Logos (all current openings remote), World Vision (stated hybrid and remote roles), and CBN (remote options). Treat older lists' other names skeptically; the most famous one (YouVersion) had zero remote openings at our check.

### Are there work-from-home Christian jobs for moms?

The realistic lanes: remote roles at the ministries above (Compassion's minimum 25 days off plus 15 holidays is notably family-friendly), remote teaching at online Christian academies like Veritas, freelance Christian writing (our [verified rates guide](/blog/christian-writing-jobs)), and creator or affiliate work, including our own 50% [affiliate program](/affiliates). All are legitimate and schedule-flexible to different degrees; none is passive income, whatever the ads say.

### Do remote Christian jobs require a statement of faith?

Often, and the employers are upfront about it: World Vision requires agreement with its Statement of Faith or the Apostles' Creed, Compassion looks for an active spiritual life, while a company like Interstate Batteries states employees need not be Christian. Read each employer's own careers language; it's the clearest signal of what working there is like.

### How much do remote Christian jobs pay?

Posted examples from our verification day: a senior account manager role at Logos listed $80-90k base plus commission, and ministry nonprofits typically pay below market salary but above market benefits (Compassion's 10% retirement contribution and 40 paid days off being the standout). Christian tech and senior nonprofit roles pay professional rates; entry ministry-admin roles don't. Judge each posting, not the category.
