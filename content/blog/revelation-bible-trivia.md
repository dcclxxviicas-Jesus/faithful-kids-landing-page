These 36 Revelation Bible trivia questions cover the last book of the Bible -- with answers and verse references included after every question. They're sorted into easy (ages 5-8), medium (ages 9-12), and hard (teens and adults) rounds, and they focus on the parts of Revelation families enjoy most: the seven churches, heaven's throne room, and the new heaven and new earth.

Revelation gets a scary reputation, but it's actually the Bible's biggest worship scene and its happiest ending -- gates made of single pearls, a street of gold, and God wiping away every tear. These questions stick to that ground, so the whole family can play without anyone needing a nightlight afterward.

## What Is the Book of Revelation About?

The book of Revelation is a vision given to a man named John on the island of Patmos, showing that Jesus wins in the end -- evil is defeated, God's people are kept safe, and history finishes with a new heaven and a new earth where God lives with his people forever. The word "revelation" translates the Greek *apokalypsis*, which simply means "unveiling" -- pulling back the curtain. The book opens with letters from Jesus to seven real churches in Asia Minor (modern Turkey) and closes with the most detailed picture of heaven in the Bible.

The author names himself John four times (Revelation 1:1, 1:4, 1:9, 22:8), and church tradition has identified him as the apostle John, though some early Christians attributed it to another John, sometimes called John the Elder. It was likely written near the end of the first century, when Christians faced pressure and persecution -- which is exactly why its message is comfort, not fear: "Look, I am coming soon!" (Revelation 22:12).

## Is Revelation Too Scary for Kids?

Revelation doesn't have to be scary for kids if you start where the book itself lands: the throne room and the happy ending. The visions in the middle use dramatic symbols, and Christians have honestly understood them in different ways for centuries -- some read them as pointing mainly to future events, some to the sweep of history, some to the struggles of John's own day, and some as timeless pictures of good defeating evil. Good families and good churches land in different places, so it's fair to tell kids, "Christians see this part differently -- but everyone agrees on the ending."

For young kids, three scenes carry the whole book: Jesus among the lampstands saying "Do not be afraid" (Revelation 1:17), the throne room where every creature sings (Revelation 4-5), and the new creation where "there will be no more death or mourning or crying or pain" (Revelation 21:4). Kids who meet Revelation through those doors usually come away comforted, not frightened -- and our walkthroughs of [the throne room](/blog/the-throne-room-for-kids) and [the new heaven and new earth](/blog/a-new-heaven-and-new-earth-for-kids) are built for exactly that.

## Easy Revelation Trivia Questions (Ages 5-8)

1. Where does Revelation come in the Bible? **It's the very last book** (Revelation 22:21)

2. What is the name of the man who saw and wrote down the visions? **John** (Revelation 1:1)

3. On what island was John when he received the vision? **Patmos** (Revelation 1:9)

4. How many churches receive letters at the start of the book? **Seven** (Revelation 1:4, 11)

5. Finish Jesus' famous title: "I am the Alpha and the ___." **Omega** (Revelation 1:8)

6. What animal picture shows Jesus in heaven's throne room? **A Lamb** (Revelation 5:6)

7. In the new heaven and new earth, what will God wipe away from every eye? **Every tear** (Revelation 21:4)

8. What is each gate of the new Jerusalem made of? **A single pearl** (Revelation 21:21)

9. What is the great street of the city made of? **Gold, "as pure as transparent glass"** (Revelation 21:21)

10. What does Jesus say he is doing at the door in Revelation 3:20? **Standing and knocking** (Revelation 3:20)

11. Does the new city need the sun or moon to shine on it? **No -- the glory of God gives it light, and the Lamb is its lamp** (Revelation 21:23)

12. Which tree from the Garden of Eden appears again at the very end of the Bible? **The tree of life** (Revelation 22:2)

## Medium Revelation Trivia Questions (Ages 9-12)

13. Which church does Jesus call "lukewarm -- neither hot nor cold"? **Laodicea** (Revelation 3:15-16)

14. Which church had worked hard but "forsaken the love you had at first"? **Ephesus** (Revelation 2:4)

15. Which church is promised "the crown of life" for being faithful even to the point of death? **Smyrna** (Revelation 2:10)

16. What surrounds God's throne, "resembling an emerald"? **A rainbow** (Revelation 4:3)

17. How many elders sit on thrones around God's throne? **Twenty-four** (Revelation 4:4)

18. The four living creatures look like a lion, an ox, an eagle -- and what else? **One has a face like a man** (Revelation 4:7)

19. What three-word song do the living creatures never stop singing? **"Holy, holy, holy"** (Revelation 4:8)

20. What do the twenty-four elders do with their crowns? **They lay them before the throne** (Revelation 4:10)

21. Who is the only one worthy to open the scroll with seven seals? **Jesus -- the Lion of the tribe of Judah, who appears as the Lamb** (Revelation 5:5-6)

22. What do the golden bowls of incense in heaven stand for? **The prayers of God's people** (Revelation 5:8)

23. The great crowd in white robes comes from where? **Every nation, tribe, people and language** (Revelation 7:9)

24. What comes "down out of heaven from God" in Revelation 21? **The Holy City, the new Jerusalem** (Revelation 21:2)

25. Why is there no temple in the new Jerusalem? **Because the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple** (Revelation 21:22)

26. Whose names are written on the city's twelve gates and twelve foundations? **The twelve tribes of Israel on the gates, and the twelve apostles on the foundations** (Revelation 21:12-14)

## Hard Revelation Trivia Questions (Teens and Adults)

27. What does the Greek word behind "revelation" (*apokalypsis*) actually mean? **An unveiling or revealing** (Revelation 1:1)

28. What special promise does Revelation 1:3 make -- unique among Bible books? **A blessing on the one who reads its words aloud and on those who hear and take it to heart** (Revelation 1:3)

29. In Jesus' own explanation, what do the seven lampstands represent? **The seven churches** (Revelation 1:20)

30. On what day was John "in the Spirit" when he heard the loud voice? **The Lord's Day** (Revelation 1:10)

31. Which church is promised hidden manna and a white stone with a new name on it? **Pergamum** (Revelation 2:17)

32. Which church has "a reputation of being alive," though Jesus says it is dead? **Sardis** (Revelation 3:1)

33. "Hallelujah" appears in only one New Testament book -- this one. In which chapter does it ring out four times? **Revelation 19** (Revelation 19:1-6)

34. What two names is the rider on the white horse called in Revelation 19? **Faithful and True** (Revelation 19:11)

35. How often does the tree of life bear fruit, and what are its leaves for? **Twelve crops, one each month -- and its leaves are for the healing of the nations** (Revelation 22:2)

36. What is the last verse of the entire Bible? **"The grace of the Lord Jesus be with God's people. Amen."** (Revelation 22:21)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who wrote the book of Revelation?

The book names its author as John, a servant of Jesus exiled on the island of Patmos "because of the word of God" (Revelation 1:1, 9). Church tradition has identified him as the apostle John, though some early Christians attributed the book to another John, sometimes called John the Elder -- so it's fair to say "traditionally the apostle John."

### How many chapters does Revelation have?

Revelation has 22 chapters and 404 verses, making it the longest of the New Testament's non-Gospel books after Acts. It's the only New Testament book that is primarily prophecy and vision rather than narrative or letter -- though it opens with seven short letters from Jesus to churches.

### What are the seven churches of Revelation?

The seven churches are Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea (Revelation 1:11) -- all real cities in the Roman province of Asia, in what is now western Turkey. Each receives a short letter from Jesus with encouragement, correction, and a promise "to the one who is victorious."

### Is it "Revelation" or "Revelations"?

It's Revelation, singular -- the book describes itself as "the revelation from Jesus Christ" (Revelation 1:1), one unveiling, not several. Knowing that is a trivia question in its own right, since "Revelations" may be the most common Bible-name mistake there is.

### Is the book of Revelation appropriate for kids?

Yes, with a parent choosing the doorway. The letters to the churches, the throne-room worship of chapters 4-5, and the new heaven and new earth of chapters 21-22 are vivid, hopeful, and very kid-friendly, while the symbolic judgment visions in the middle are better saved for older kids with an adult alongside. Christians interpret those middle visions in different ways, so it's honest to tell kids the ending is what everyone agrees on.

### What is the most famous verse in Revelation?

Two verses share the crown: Revelation 3:20 -- "Here I am! I stand at the door and knock" -- and Revelation 21:4 -- "He will wipe every tear from their eyes. There will be no more death or mourning or crying or pain." The first is one of the most quoted invitations in Scripture; the second is read at bedsides and funerals the world over.
