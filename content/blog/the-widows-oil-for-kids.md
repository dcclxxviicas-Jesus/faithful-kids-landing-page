# The Widows Oil for Kids: The Bible Story Explained Simply

**Series:** Elijah and Elisha (Episode 02 of 10) | **Scripture:** 1 & 2 Kings | **Ages 6+**

The Widows Oil is a Bible story from 1 & 2 Kings in the Old Testament. This retelling is designed for kids ages 5 and up, using simple language they can understand and lessons they will remember.

## What Is the Story of The Widows Oil for Kids?

THE WIDOW'S OIL Series: Elijah & Elisha | Episode 2 Scripture: 1 Kings 17:8-24

Last time, Elijah was camped by a brook, eating room service delivered by ravens. But then the brook dried up. No water. No plan. Just a prophet and an empty stream. So what did God do next? He sent Elijah somewhere even MORE unlikely.

God said: "Go to a town called Zarephath. I've commanded a WIDOW there to feed you." Now hold on — a widow in those days was one of the poorest people in society. No husband meant no income. God was sending Elijah from one impossible situation to another. That's just how My Father rolls.

So Elijah arrives at Zarephath and finds this woman gathering sticks. He calls out, "Hey, could you bring me some water? And while you're at it — a piece of bread?"

This story comes from 1 & 2 Kings and is part of the Old Testament.

## Quiz: Test What Your Child Learned

After watching the video, see if your child can answer these questions:

### After the brook dried up, where did God tell Elijah to go?

**To the town of Zarephath.** 

### Who did God say would provide food for Elijah in Zarephath?

**A widow.**

### What did the widow have left when Elijah found her?

**A handful of flour and a little oil.**

### What did Elijah ask the widow to do FIRST?

**Make him a small cake before feeding herself.**

### What did God promise about the jar of flour and jug of oil?

**They would never run out until rain returned.**

### What happened to the widow's son later in the story?

**He got sick and died.**

### How many times did Elijah cry out to God for the boy?

**Three times.**

### What is the main lesson of this story?

**When God asks you to give what you think you can't lose, trust Him — He multiplies it.**
