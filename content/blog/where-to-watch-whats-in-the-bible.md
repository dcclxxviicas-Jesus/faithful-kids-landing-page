The short answer: the most reliable home for **What's in the Bible? with Buck Denver** today is **Mr Phil TV** — Phil Vischer's own subscription service, which carries the entire library. Beyond that, the series shows up on **Prime Video through channel subscriptions** (Minno Kids and Great American Pure Flix channels), and the **official store at whatsinthebible.com sells every volume** at $12.99 each, digital or DVD, with the complete set at $149.99.

Unlike [Superbook](/blog/where-to-watch-superbook), none of it streams free — but the buy-it-once option makes this one of the few shows where ownership beats subscribing.

Availability on the streaming side has moved around over the years — Vischer has said publicly that he sold the series and now licenses access to his own creation — so the store and Mr Phil TV are the two routes least likely to vanish mid-series.

## What it actually is (and why people still ask for it)

What's in the Bible? is what Phil Vischer made after VeggieTales: thirteen volumes that walk straight through the whole Bible — Genesis to Revelation — with a cast of puppets led by newscaster Buck Denver, plus songs and whiteboard-style explanations.

The reason it still gets recommended fifteen years on is that almost nothing else does what it does. VeggieTales tells parables with vegetables. Superbook drops kids into individual stories. What's in the Bible? explains the *book* — who wrote it, how it fits together, what a covenant is, why the Old Testament matters to the New. It is the rare kids' series that answers the questions kids actually ask: not just "what happened?" but "why is this in the Bible at all?"

It also skews older than you might expect. The puppets read as 5-year-old material, but the content lands best around 7-12 — and plenty of parents admit they learned from it themselves.

## Buy it or stream it?

If your kids are in the 7-12 window and you want the whole arc, **buying beats subscribing** here. Thirteen volumes at $12.99 — or the set at $149.99 — is yours forever, and this is a series families genuinely rewatch and pass around in a way that justifies owning it. Church and homeschool use is exactly why the store also sells a full curriculum version.

If you would rather sample first, Mr Phil TV's subscription is the lowest-commitment way in, and it adds Vischer's newer content around the library.

## The honest limitation

What's in the Bible? explains Scripture brilliantly, but it is long-form television: episodes run around 25 minutes, the volumes assume you watch in order, and there is no way of knowing what your child retained beyond asking over dinner. It teaches *about* the Bible better than anything in its category; checking whether the teaching landed is left entirely to you.
