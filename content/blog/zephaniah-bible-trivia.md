These 20 Zephaniah Bible trivia questions cover all three chapters -- the day of the LORD, the judgment of the nations, and the famous verse where God rejoices over his people with singing -- with answers and verse references after every question. They're sorted into easy (ages 5-8), medium (ages 9-12), and hard (teens and adults) rounds.

Zephaniah may be the least-quizzed book in the Old Testament, but it hides one of the Bible's most-loved verses: Zephaniah 3:17, where God is pictured singing over his children. Get your kids to that verse and the rest of the book comes alive.

## What Is the Book of Zephaniah About?

The book of Zephaniah is a prophecy about "the day of the LORD" -- a coming day of judgment on Judah and the surrounding nations, followed by joyful restoration for a humble remnant. Zephaniah prophesied during the reign of King Josiah of Judah (Zephaniah 1:1), which places the book around 640-609 BC, in the generation just before Babylon destroyed Jerusalem.

Zephaniah opens with one of the most unusual family trees among the prophets: his ancestry is traced back four generations to a man named Hezekiah (Zephaniah 1:1), possibly the famous king of that name -- which would make Zephaniah a prince preaching against his own royal city, though the Bible doesn't confirm the link. His name means "the LORD hides" or "the LORD protects," and that's the book's arc in miniature: judgment sweeps through, but God shelters the humble who seek him (Zephaniah 2:3).

## What Was Happening in Zephaniah's Time?

Zephaniah preached during the early years of King Josiah, when Judah was still full of idol worship left over from wicked kings like Manasseh -- Baal altars, star worship on the rooftops, and people who figured God would do "nothing, either good or bad" (Zephaniah 1:12). Josiah became king at just 8 years old (2 Kings 22:1), and he grew into one of the great reformers; many scholars think Zephaniah's fiery preaching helped prepare the ground for Josiah's cleanup, which exploded after the Book of the Law was found in the temple (2 Kings 22:8). Kids who know our post on [good kings and bad kings](/blog/good-kings-and-bad-kings-for-kids) will recognize Josiah as one of the best.

The reforms were real but the repentance didn't stick, and within a generation [Judah fell to Babylon](/blog/judah-falls-to-babylon-for-kids) -- the very "day of the LORD" Zephaniah had warned about, with a worldwide final Day still beyond it. That's what makes his closing chapter so striking: after all the smoke, the book ends in singing.

One timeline fact makes great bonus trivia: Zephaniah preached in the same generation as young Jeremiah, whose ministry also began under Josiah (Jeremiah 1:2), and just before Habakkuk raised his questions about Babylon. Three prophets, one doomed kingdom, three very different voices -- Zephaniah the announcer, Jeremiah the weeper, Habakkuk the questioner. Kids who can place those three names in Josiah's Jerusalem understand the end of the Old Testament story better than most adults do.

## Easy Zephaniah Trivia Questions (Ages 5-8)

1. Which king of Judah was on the throne while Zephaniah prophesied? **Josiah** (Zephaniah 1:1)

2. How old was Josiah when he became king? **Eight years old** (2 Kings 22:1)

3. What does Zephaniah say God will do over his people -- something that might surprise you? **Rejoice over them with singing** (Zephaniah 3:17)

4. What big "day" does Zephaniah warn is coming near? **The day of the LORD** (Zephaniah 1:14)

5. What does Zephaniah 3:17 call God -- a Mighty what, who saves? **A Mighty Warrior who saves** (Zephaniah 3:17)

6. Does the book of Zephaniah end with sadness or with joy? **Joy -- singing, celebration, and God bringing his people home** (Zephaniah 3:14-20)

## Medium Zephaniah Trivia Questions (Ages 9-12)

7. What does Zephaniah's name mean? **"The LORD hides" or "the LORD protects"**

8. What three things does Zephaniah 2:3 tell the humble of the land to seek? **The LORD, righteousness, and humility** (Zephaniah 2:3)

9. Which false god, worshiped in Judah, does Zephaniah say God will cut off every remnant of? **Baal** (Zephaniah 1:4)

10. Where were people in Jerusalem bowing down to worship the starry host? **On the rooftops** (Zephaniah 1:5)

11. What did the complacent people of Jerusalem think the LORD would do? **"Nothing, either good or bad"** (Zephaniah 1:12)

12. How does God say he will search Jerusalem to find the complacent? **With lamps** (Zephaniah 1:12)

13. Which enemy capital city does Zephaniah say will become utterly desolate, "dry as the desert"? **Nineveh, capital of Assyria** (Zephaniah 2:13)

## Hard Zephaniah Trivia Questions (Teens and Adults)

14. Through how many generations is Zephaniah's family line traced, and to whom? **Four generations, back to Hezekiah -- possibly the king** (Zephaniah 1:1)

15. Name two of the four Philistine cities Zephaniah pronounces judgment on. **Gaza, Ashkelon, Ashdod, and Ekron** (Zephaniah 2:4)

16. Which two nations east of the Jordan does Zephaniah say will become like Sodom and Gomorrah? **Moab and Ammon** (Zephaniah 2:9)

17. In Zephaniah 3:9, what does God promise to purify so that all peoples may call on his name? **Their lips** (Zephaniah 3:9)

18. What kind of people does God say he will leave within Jerusalem as a remnant? **The meek and humble, who trust in the name of the LORD** (Zephaniah 3:12)

19. Zephaniah 1:14-15 piles up words for the day of the LORD -- name two. **Distress, anguish, trouble, ruin, darkness, gloom, clouds, blackness** (Zephaniah 1:15)

20. What happened during Josiah's reign, recorded in 2 Kings, that supercharged the reforms Zephaniah's preaching pointed toward? **The Book of the Law was found in the temple** (2 Kings 22:8)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who wrote the book of Zephaniah?

The book was written by the prophet Zephaniah son of Cushi, whose genealogy is traced back four generations to Hezekiah (Zephaniah 1:1) -- possibly King Hezekiah, which would give Zephaniah royal blood, though the text doesn't say so outright. He prophesied in Judah during the reign of King Josiah, roughly 640-609 BC.

### How many chapters does Zephaniah have?

Zephaniah has 3 chapters and 53 verses. Chapter 1 announces judgment on Judah, chapter 2 turns to the surrounding nations, and chapter 3 moves from Jerusalem's sin to the joyful restoration of a humble remnant.

### What is the most famous verse in Zephaniah?

Zephaniah 3:17 is by far the book's most famous verse: "The LORD your God is with you, the Mighty Warrior who saves. He will take great delight in you; in his love he will no longer rebuke you, but will rejoice over you with singing." It's one of the only places in the Bible where God is pictured singing.

### What is the day of the LORD in Zephaniah?

The day of the LORD in Zephaniah is a coming day when God personally steps in to judge sin -- first through the historical fall of Judah and its neighbors, and ultimately in a final worldwide judgment (Zephaniah 1:14-18). But the same day brings rescue: God promises to shelter the humble who seek him (Zephaniah 2:3) and to restore and rejoice over his people (Zephaniah 3:17-20).

### What is the main message of Zephaniah?

The main message is that God judges pride and complacency but delights to save the humble. The book sweeps from a burning-hot warning in chapter 1 to one of Scripture's warmest endings in chapter 3, where God quiets his people with his love and sings over them.

### What age can kids start learning the book of Zephaniah?

Kids as young as 5 can learn Zephaniah 3:17 -- God singing over his children is one of the most comforting pictures in the Bible for little ones. The judgment chapters and the "day of the LORD" theme are better suited to ages 9 and up, when kids can hold justice and mercy together.
