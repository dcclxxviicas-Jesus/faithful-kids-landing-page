Christian Alexander is the founder of Faithful Kids, a Bible video app for children ages 5 to 15. A Christian father, he built it for his own kids: more than 300 short lessons running in order from Genesis to Revelation, each followed by a quiz and a reflection question. He writes about faith formation and screens in family life for The Christian Post, and answers the support email himself at christian@faithfulkids.app.
