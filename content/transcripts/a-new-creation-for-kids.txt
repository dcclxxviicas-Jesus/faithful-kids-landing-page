Before we finish this series, I need to tell you the thing that ties it all together. Every truth we've explored — the gospel, being more than conquerors, the armor of God, love, faith, hope, the fruit of the Spirit, running the race, joy, worry — all of it leads here.

Paul wrote it in just one sentence: "If anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: the old has gone, the new is here."

Read that again in your mind. The old has gone. The new is here.

Every mistake you've made, every label anyone has ever slapped on you — "not smart enough," "not cool enough," "too much," "not enough" — none of it defines you anymore. In me, you are brand new.

Not improved. Not patched up. Not a slightly better version of your old self. New. Like a caterpillar becoming a butterfly. You don't have wings glued on — you are a completely different creature.

Paul said it another way in his letter to the Galatians: "I have been crucified with Christ. It is no longer I who live, but Christ who lives in me. The life I now live in the body, I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave himself for me."

The old you — the one controlled by fear, shame, sin, and selfishness — that person went to the cross with me. And when I rose from the dead, you rose too. A new you. My life living inside of yours.

I know some of you are carrying things from your past. Maybe you did something you're ashamed of. Maybe someone told you who you are and you believed them. Maybe you look in the mirror and still see the old version.

But that's not who I see.

When I look at you, I don't see your worst day. I see who you're becoming. I see my Spirit at work inside you. I see someone worth dying for — and I already proved that.

You are not who you used to be. In me, every day is a fresh start. The guilt? Gone. The shame? Broken. The old labels? Ripped off and thrown away.

This is the whole message of every letter we've studied. You are loved. You are forgiven. You are equipped. You are not alone. And you are new.

Walk out of here different than you walked in. You are a new creation. And the old? It's gone for good.

I'll see you soon. And remember — I'm with you. Always.
