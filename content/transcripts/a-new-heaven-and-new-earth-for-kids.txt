TITLE: A New Heaven and a New Earth
SERIES: The Ending & the Beginning
EPISODE: 8
SCRIPTURE: Revelation 21:1-7

Close your eyes and imagine the most beautiful place you've ever been. The most stunning sunset. The clearest water. The greenest mountain. Now multiply it by a billion. You're not even close.

What I'm about to show you is the future. Not a fairy tale. Not wishful thinking. A promise.

My apostle John — the one I loved, the one who leaned against me at dinner, the one who stood at the foot of my cross — he was old now. Exiled to a lonely island called Patmos. And there, I gave him a vision that would change everything.

He saw a new heaven and a new earth. The first heaven and the first earth — the broken ones, the ones stained with sin and death and tears — they had passed away. Gone. And in their place? Something so perfect, so breathtaking, that John could barely find words.

He saw the Holy City — the New Jerusalem — coming down out of heaven from God. Picture it: a city made of pure gold, so clear it looks like glass. Walls made of jasper. Twelve gates, each one carved from a single pearl. Foundations decorated with every precious stone you can imagine — sapphire, emerald, topaz, amethyst. Streets of gold stretching as far as the eye can see.

But here's the thing — the gold and the jewels aren't even the best part.

A voice thundered from the throne: "Look! God's dwelling place is now among the people. He will dwell with them, and they will be His people, and God Himself will be with them and be their God."

Do you understand what that means? No more separation. No more wondering if God is there. No more praying into what feels like silence. God — right there. Face to face. Forever.

And then the voice said the words that will make every heart that's ever ached finally breathe a sigh of relief: "He will wipe every tear from their eyes. There will be no more death, or mourning, or crying, or pain, for the old order of things has passed away."

No more death. No more funerals. No more cancer. No more bullying. No more loneliness. No more goodbyes. All of it — gone.

Then I spoke from the throne and said, "I am making everything new."

Not just fixing the old. Not patching things up. Making everything — NEW.

There's a river of life running through that city, crystal clear. And on each side, the tree of life — bearing twelve kinds of fruit, one for every month. The leaves of the tree are for the healing of the nations.

This is where the story is headed. This isn't the end — this is the REAL beginning. The one everything has been building toward. And the best part? There's a place there for you.
