Hey there, friend. I'm so glad you're here. My name is Jesus — and today I want to tell you the most personal story I've got. The story of how I came into the world. And it starts with the bravest young woman I know — my mother.

Her name was Mary. She was a teenager living in a tiny town called Nazareth. Nothing fancy. No mansions, no red carpets. Just a quiet girl who loved God with her whole heart. She was engaged to a man named Joseph — a carpenter — and life was pretty normal.

Until one day, it wasn't.

God sent an angel named Gabriel — one of Heaven's top messengers — straight into Mary's house. Picture it. You're folding laundry or making bread, and suddenly there's a blazing, glorious angel standing in your room. Gabriel looked at her and said, "Greetings, favored one! The Lord is with you."

Mary was terrified. Wouldn't you be? Her mind was spinning. But Gabriel said, "Don't be afraid, Mary. God has chosen you for something incredible. You are going to have a baby — a son. You will name Him Jesus. He will be called the Son of the Most High, and His kingdom will never end."

Never. End.

Mary's heart was pounding. She asked the most honest question anyone could ask: "How is this possible? I'm not even married yet." And the angel said, "The Holy Spirit will come upon you. The power of the Most High will overshadow you. Nothing is impossible with God."

And here is the part that gets me every time. Mary didn't argue. She didn't run. She looked that angel in the eye and said seven words that changed everything: "I am the Lord's servant. Let it be."

She was young. She knew people would talk. She knew it wouldn't be easy. But she said yes to God anyway.

Then Mary rushed to visit her older cousin Elizabeth, who was also miraculously pregnant — with John the Baptist. The second Elizabeth heard Mary's voice, the baby inside her leaped! Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit and cried out, "Blessed are you among women!"

And my mother? She started singing. A song so beautiful they still sing it today — the Magnificat. "My soul magnifies the Lord! He has done great things for me!"

Here's what I need you to know: God doesn't always choose the people the world expects. He chose a teenage girl from a nowhere town. He might just choose you too.

I'll see you in the next one!
