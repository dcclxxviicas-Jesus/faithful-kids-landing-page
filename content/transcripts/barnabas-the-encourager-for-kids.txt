THE EARLY CHURCH — EPISODE 9: BARNABAS THE ENCOURAGER
Based on Acts 4:36-37, 9:26-27, 11:22-26
Series: The Early Church | Episode 9 of 10
Target audience: Kids ages 7-15

Today I want to tell you about someone who doesn't always make the highlight reel. But without this man, the story of my church would look completely different.

His name was Joseph — but everyone called him Barnabas. The apostles gave him that nickname. It means "Son of Encouragement." He was SO encouraging that people literally renamed him for it.

First time we see Barnabas, the church is brand new. Barnabas owned a field — and he sold it. Brought every penny and laid it at the apostles' feet. No fanfare. Just quiet, radical generosity.

But here's where Barnabas becomes truly legendary.

Remember Saul? After his conversion, he tried to join the believers in Jerusalem. And guess what? They were TERRIFIED of him. "Nice try, Saul — you're probably just here to get our names and throw us all in prison." Nobody would go near him.

Enter Barnabas.

Barnabas saw what nobody else could see. He took Saul to the apostles and vouched for him. "This man is for real. He met the Lord. He's been preaching boldly in Damascus. He's one of us."

Because Barnabas believed in Saul — the apostles accepted him. Without that moment, Paul might never have been welcomed into the church. One act of encouragement changed the entire future of Christianity.

But Barnabas wasn't done.

Later, when the gospel spread to Antioch, the apostles sent Barnabas. He arrived, saw what God was doing, and encouraged everyone to remain true to the Lord. Then he traveled to find Saul and brought him to Antioch. For a whole year, they taught that young church together. Barnabas saw Saul's potential and pulled him into the mission.

Here's what I want you to hear today: encouragement is a superpower. You don't need to be the loudest voice. You don't need to be on stage. Sometimes the most powerful thing you can do is walk up to someone the world has written off and say, "I believe in you."

One person who believes in you can change your entire life. Barnabas proved that.

Be a Barnabas. The world is desperate for one.
