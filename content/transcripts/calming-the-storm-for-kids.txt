Hey kids! Come sit down, because I want to tell you about a night my disciples will NEVER forget.

It had been a long day of teaching by the Sea of Galilee. Thousands of people pressing in, hanging on every word. By evening, I was exhausted. So I told my disciples, "Let's cross to the other side of the lake."

We climbed into the boat. The water was calm. Stars were coming out. And I found a cushion in the back of the boat, laid my head down, and fell fast asleep.

Now here's where things get WILD.

Out of nowhere — and I mean nowhere — a MASSIVE storm slammed into that lake. We're not talking a little rain shower. This was a FURIOUS squall. The kind of storm that makes experienced fishermen go pale. Waves were crashing OVER the sides of the boat. Water was pouring in. The wind was howling like nothing they'd ever heard.

And my disciples? They were PANICKING. Bailing water as fast as they could. Gripping the sides. Screaming over the roar of the wind.

And where was I? Still asleep. On that cushion. In the middle of the chaos.

Finally they couldn't take it anymore. They shook me awake and shouted, "Teacher! Don't you CARE if we DROWN?!"

I stood up. Wind whipping my robes. Waves slamming the boat. And I looked out at that storm — and I spoke three words:

"QUIET. BE STILL."

And just like THAT — silence. The wind stopped. The waves flattened. The lake became smooth as glass. Like someone hit the mute button on the entire sky.

Then I turned to my disciples and asked them a question they didn't expect: "Why are you so afraid? Do you STILL have no faith?"

They just stared at each other, absolutely TERRIFIED. Not of the storm anymore — of ME. They whispered to each other, "Who IS this? Even the wind and the waves OBEY Him!"

And here's what I want you to understand: the same Jesus who was sleeping through your storm? He's the one who CONTROLS it. I wasn't worried because I knew exactly who I was — and what I could do. Every storm you face, every scary moment, every time life feels out of control — I am RIGHT THERE with you. And I have authority over ALL of it.

You don't need to panic. You just need to trust me.

Until next time — stay brave, stay faithful, and remember: no storm is bigger than your God.
