Well, friend — here we are. The final episode. And honestly? This might be the most important one of all.

Joshua is old now. His hair is gray, his body is tired, and he knows his time is almost up. But before he goes, he gathers the entire nation — every man, woman, and child — for one final, unforgettable speech.

And what does he do? He takes them on a journey through their own history. Can you imagine being there?

"Remember Abraham? God called him out of a land full of false gods and said, 'I'm going to make a great nation from you.' Remember how your ancestors went down to Egypt and became slaves for four HUNDRED years? Remember how God sent Moses? Remember the plagues? Remember Passover night — the blood on the doorposts — when death passed over your homes? Remember the Red Sea splitting in two and you walking through on dry ground?"

He keeps going.

"Remember FORTY YEARS in the wilderness? Remember how God fed you manna every single morning? Remember how He brought water from a rock? Remember crossing the Jordan River? Remember Jericho — those walls crashing down? Remember the sun standing still in the sky?!"

Joshua is saying: LOOK at everything God has done for you. Look at His faithfulness. Not once — NOT ONCE — did He break a promise. Everything He said He would do, He did.

And then comes the moment. Joshua looks the people straight in the eyes and says:

"Choose for yourselves THIS DAY whom you will serve. Will you serve the gods your ancestors served? The gods of this land? Or will you serve the Lord?" And then he drops the mic: "But as for me and my house — WE WILL SERVE THE LORD."

And the people? They shout back, "We will serve the Lord! He is our God!"

Here's why this matters for YOU. Faith isn't a one-time decision. It's not something you decide once and coast through life. Every single day you choose. Who am I going to follow today? What am I going to trust? Whose voice am I going to listen to?

The world will give you a THOUSAND other things to follow. Popularity. Money. What everyone else is doing. But I'm standing here asking you the same thing Joshua asked all those years ago: Who will you serve?

Choose today. And choose Me. Not because I'll make your life easy — but because I'll make your life MATTER. I'll walk with you, I'll never leave you, and I will keep every single promise I've made.

So — what do you choose?
