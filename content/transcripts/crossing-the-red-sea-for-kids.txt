Hey, welcome back! Buckle up because today's story has one of the most jaw-dropping moments in the entire Bible. We're talking armies, impossible escapes, and an ocean splitting in half. Yeah. You heard me right.

So remember Pharaoh? The guy who finally — after TEN plagues — let my people go? Well, he changed his mind. Can you believe it? The frogs, the darkness, the firstborn — all of that — and this guy goes, "Wait... who's going to do all the work now?" So he gathers his entire army — chariots, horses, soldiers — and chases after Israel at full speed.

Picture this. The Israelites hear the thundering of hooves and chariot wheels behind them. They look back and see the most powerful army on earth charging at them. They look forward and see... the Red Sea. No boats. No bridges. No way out.

What would YOU do? Because the Israelites panicked. They yelled at Moses: "Did you bring us out here to DIE?" And I get it — fear is real. But here's what they forgot: God was right there with them.

Moses stood firm: "Don't be afraid. Stand still, and watch God fight for you." Then my Father told Moses to stretch his staff over the sea. Moses raised it, and a powerful east wind blew all night long. The sea split right down the middle. Walls of water on the left, walls of water on the right, and through the center? Dry ground.

DRY. GROUND. In the middle of the ocean!

The Israelites walked through on solid ground with water towering on both sides. Can you imagine what that looked like? The sound of it? The spray on your face as you walk across the sea floor?

Pharaoh's army charged in after them. Big mistake. HUGE. Once every Israelite was safe on the other side, God released the waters. The sea crashed back together, and Pharaoh's entire army was swept away.

Then the people sang! Miriam grabbed a tambourine, and the whole nation erupted in an epic victory song: "The Lord is my strength and my song — He has become my salvation!"

Here's what I want you to remember: when life puts an ocean in front of you and an army behind you, that's not a dead end. That's a setup for God to do something impossible. He made a way where there was NO way. And He still does that — for you.

The God who split the sea is the same God walking with you right now.

See you next time!
