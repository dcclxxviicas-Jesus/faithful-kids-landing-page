TITLE: Cyrus Sets Them Free
SERIES: The Return Home — Episode 1
SCRIPTURE: Ezra 1 (Isaiah 44:28–45:1)

Hey — have you ever been away from home for so long that you almost forgot what it looked like? Maybe a long trip, summer camp, or staying at a relative's house way longer than planned?

Now imagine seventy YEARS away from home. Not seventy days. Seventy YEARS. That's how long My people were stuck in Babylon. A whole generation was born and grew old never having set foot in Jerusalem. They sang songs about a city most of them had never even seen. They wept by the rivers of Babylon and whispered, "Will we EVER go back?"

And the answer was yes — because I had a plan, and it involved somebody nobody expected.

His name was Cyrus, king of Persia — the most powerful man on the planet. He didn't worship My Father. He didn't grow up reading the scrolls. He didn't even know who he was working for. But here's what blows people's minds: the prophet Isaiah wrote down Cyrus's name ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTY YEARS before Cyrus was even born! My Father said, "Cyrus — yeah, THAT guy — he's the one I'm going to use to set My people free."

And that's exactly what happened.

Cyrus conquered Babylon, and the very first thing he did was issue a decree. Picture a royal announcement echoing through every corner of the empire: "The God of Israel told me to rebuild His temple in Jerusalem. Whoever belongs to His people — GO. Return home. And everyone around you — load them up with silver, gold, and supplies!"

Can you imagine the Jewish families hearing that news? Tears streaming down faces. Parents grabbing their children. Grandparents shaking their heads in disbelief. FIFTY THOUSAND people packed up and began the long journey home.

Think about it — the king of Persia became a delivery driver for God's plan, and he didn't even have the app downloaded!

Here's what I need you to understand: My Father can use ANYONE to accomplish His purposes. Your teacher. Your coach. A government leader. A person who doesn't even believe in Him yet. Nobody — and I mean NOBODY — is outside God's reach. He moves hearts like water in His hand.

So the next time things seem impossible, remember Cyrus. God had his name picked out before he took his first breath.

And God knows YOUR name too.
