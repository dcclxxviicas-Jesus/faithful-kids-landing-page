EXILE & FAITH — EPISODE 6: DANIEL IN THE LIONS' DEN
Based on Daniel 6 | Narrated by Jesus

Okay — you know Daniel by now. He survived Babylon. He interpreted dreams. He read writing off a wall. But what I'm about to tell you might be the most intense night of his entire life. And by this point, Daniel isn't young anymore. He's an old man. But he's still got the same fire he had as a teenager.

Here's the situation. Babylon fell, remember? The Persians are running things now, and the new king — Darius — actually LIKES Daniel. In fact, Daniel is so good at his job that Darius plans to put him in charge of the entire kingdom. Second only to the king himself.

And that's when the jealousy kicks in.

The other officials are furious. They start digging into Daniel's record, looking for anything — a scandal, a mistake, a bad decision. But they find NOTHING. Daniel is completely clean. So they come up with a sneaky plan. They convince King Darius to sign a law that says for thirty days, no one can pray to ANY god or any person except the king. Anyone who breaks it gets thrown into a den of hungry lions.

Darius signs it. He doesn't realize it's a trap aimed at Daniel.

Now here's the part that gives me chills. Daniel hears about the law. He knows exactly what will happen if he prays. And you know what he does? He goes home, opens his windows — wide open, facing Jerusalem — gets on his knees, and prays. Three times a day. Same as always. He doesn't hide. He doesn't whisper. He doesn't wait for the law to expire.

He chose God over safety. That's what courage looks like.

The officials catch him, run to the king, and Darius is DEVASTATED. He tries everything to save Daniel, but the law can't be undone. So Daniel is thrown into the lions' den. A stone is rolled over the mouth of the pit.

Darius can't eat. Can't sleep. He's up all night pacing.

At the crack of dawn, he RUNS to the den and calls out: "Daniel! Did your God save you?"

And from the darkness, Daniel's voice rises: "My God sent His angel and shut the lions' mouths. I am not hurt."

The lions never touched him. Not a scratch.

Here's your lesson — when doing what's right has a cost, pay it. Don't compromise who you are because of pressure. God honors faithfulness. Every single time.

And I should know — because I paid the ultimate cost, and My Father honored it too.
