THE RISE OF KINGS — Episode 5: David and Goliath
Based on 1 Samuel 17 | Narrated by Jesus

THIS. IS. THE. STORY. The most famous underdog moment in ALL of history. You probably already know it — but trust me, it hits different when you really understand what's going on. Let's go!

The Philistine army lined up on one hill. Israel's army on the other. A valley between them. And out of the Philistine camp stepped a MONSTER — Goliath of Gath. Nine feet tall. Bronze helmet. Armor weighing 125 pounds. A spear with an iron tip that weighed 15 pounds alone. Walking. Tank.

Every morning and evening for FORTY DAYS, Goliath stomped into that valley and shouted: "Send me one man to fight! If he wins, we're your servants. If I win, you're OURS!" And every day, the entire army of Israel — including King Saul — shook with fear. Nobody moved. For forty days.

Enter David. He wasn't there to fight — his dad sent him to deliver bread and cheese to his brothers. Lunch duty! But when David heard Goliath's voice booming across that valley, something IGNITED inside him. He didn't see a giant. He saw a Philistine trash-talking the army of the LIVING GOD.

David said, "Who IS this guy? Why is everyone just standing here?"

His brother Eliab got mad: "Why are you even here? Go back to your sheep!" But David kept talking until word reached Saul. Saul said, "You can't fight him — you're just a boy!" David fired back: "I've killed a lion AND a bear protecting my sheep. God delivered me from them, and He'll deliver me from this Philistine too."

Saul put his armor on David — helmet, coat of mail, sword. David could barely move. "I can't use these." He took it all off. Instead, he grabbed five smooth stones from a brook and his shepherd's sling. Five rocks against nine feet of bronze-covered fury.

And then — David didn't walk toward Goliath. He RAN. Full sprint. Straight at the giant. And he shouted, "You come with sword and spear and javelin, but I come in the name of the LORD of hosts, the God of the armies of Israel, whom YOU have defied!"

One stone. One sling. One shot. Goliath crashed face-first into the dirt. The Philistines ran. Israel erupted.

Here's what I need you to carry with you: the size of your God matters MORE than the size of your problem. David didn't win because he was strong. He won because he knew WHO was fighting with him. Your giants might look different — fear, doubt, bullying, anxiety — but the same God who dropped Goliath is standing with YOU.

Run toward your giants. God's already ahead of you.
