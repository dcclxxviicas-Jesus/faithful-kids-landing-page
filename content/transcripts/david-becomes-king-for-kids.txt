THE RISE OF KINGS — Episode 10: David Becomes King
Based on 2 Samuel 5-6 | Narrated by Jesus

We made it! This is the episode we've been building toward since the very beginning. After years of waiting, running, hiding, fighting, grieving — David is FINALLY crowned king.

But it didn't happen all at once. First, the tribe of Judah anointed him king in Hebron. He ruled there for seven years. Then, one by one, the rest of the tribes came to him. ALL of Israel united under David. The shepherd boy from Bethlehem was king over the entire nation. God's promise — fulfilled.

David's first big move? He conquered Jerusalem. The Jebusites who held it taunted him: "Even the blind and the lame could keep you out!" But David's men found a way in through a water shaft, and the city fell. David made it his capital — the City of David. That city is STILL one of the most important places on earth. That's where I would walk, teach, and change everything.

But the BEST part of this story is what happened next. David decided to bring the Ark of the Covenant to Jerusalem. The Ark was the symbol of God's presence — the most sacred object in Israel. David wanted God at the CENTER of his kingdom. Not on the outskirts. Not as an afterthought. Right in the heart of the capital.

And when the Ark entered Jerusalem, David DANCED. I'm not talking about a polite little sway. The Bible says he danced before the LORD "with ALL his might." He was leaping, spinning, shouting — wearing simple linen instead of his royal robes. The KING of Israel, looking like a regular person, going absolutely all out for God.

His wife Michal watched from a window and was disgusted. She told him he looked undignified. And David's response? "I will celebrate before the LORD. I will become even MORE undignified than this!"

I LOVE that. David didn't care what he looked like. He was so full of joy and gratitude that nothing else mattered. The God who called him from the sheep fields, who protected him from Goliath, who kept him alive through the wilderness — THAT God deserved everything David had.

And that's the lesson I want to leave you with for this series. Worship isn't about looking cool. It isn't about being polished or impressive. It's about giving God EVERYTHING you've got — your whole heart, your whole life, holding nothing back.

David waited years for this moment. And when it arrived, he didn't hold back. Don't you hold back either.

Thanks for walking through the Rise of Kings with me. I'll see you in the next series!
