TITLE: Esther Becomes Queen
SERIES: The Return Home — Episode 7
SCRIPTURE: Esther 1-2

Alright, we're starting one of the most dramatic stories in the entire Bible. It's got a king, a beauty contest, a secret identity, and a plot twist you won't see coming. Let's go.

We're in the Persian Empire — the biggest, most powerful kingdom on earth. King Xerxes throws a massive banquet. We're talking 180 DAYS of showing off. Gold everywhere, fine wine, the works. At the end of it, the king calls for Queen Vashti to come display her beauty. But she refuses. And the king? He is NOT happy. Vashti is out — removed as queen.

So what does the most powerful man in the world do when he needs a new queen? He holds a contest. Young women from every province in the empire are brought to the palace. They get an entire YEAR of beauty treatments. Twelve months of preparation just to stand before the king.

Now here's where it gets incredible.

Among those women is a young Jewish orphan named Esther. She lost both her parents and was raised by her older cousin Mordecai, who loved her like a daughter. She had no wealth, no royal connections, no political power. She was a nobody by the world's standards.

But here's what the world didn't know: My Father had His hand on her.

Esther won the favor of everyone who met her. The man in charge of the candidates? He gave her the best room, the best food, the best attendants — before the king even laid eyes on her. And when Esther's turn came to stand before King Xerxes? He chose HER. Out of every woman in the empire, he placed the royal crown on Esther's head.

But she was hiding something. Mordecai had told her: "Don't tell anyone you're Jewish. Not yet." So there she was — an orphan Jewish girl sitting on the throne of the most powerful empire on earth, and nobody knew who she really was.

She didn't know WHY she was there yet. She didn't see the bigger picture. She just knew God had moved her from invisible to impossible.

And that's the lesson: where God places you is NEVER random. It might not make sense right now. You might look around and think, "Why am I here? What's the point?" But God is positioning you — for something so much bigger than you can see.

Esther's biggest moment hadn't come yet. But she was exactly where she needed to be.

And so are you.
