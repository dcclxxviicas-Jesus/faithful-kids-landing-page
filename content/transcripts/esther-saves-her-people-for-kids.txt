TITLE: Esther Saves Her People
SERIES: The Return Home — Episode 9
SCRIPTURE: Esther 6-8

This is the episode where EVERYTHING flips. And it starts with something small — a sleepless night.

The night before Esther's big moment, King Xerxes can't sleep. He's tossing, turning, staring at the ceiling. So he does what any king would do — he calls for the royal records to be read to him. Basically, ancient bedtime stories. And buried in those scrolls is a forgotten entry: Mordecai once uncovered an assassination plot against the king and SAVED HIS LIFE. But nobody ever rewarded him.

The king asks, "What honor was given to Mordecai for this?"

The answer? "Nothing, your majesty. Nothing at all."

Now watch this timing. The very next morning, Haman shows up at the palace EARLY. Why? Because he's come to ask the king's permission to hang Mordecai on the gallows he just built — a massive seventy-five-foot pole. He's giddy. He thinks today is the day he destroys his enemy.

The king sees Haman and asks, "What should be done for a man the king wants to honor?" Haman thinks, "He MUST be talking about me!" So he goes ALL out: "Put royal robes on him! Set him on the king's horse! Have someone lead him through the streets shouting, 'This is what the king does for the man he honors!'"

And then the king says: "Great idea. Go do all of that... for MORDECAI."

I wish I could have painted Haman's face in that moment. The man he wanted to KILL — he now has to parade through the city like a hero. The humiliation was total.

But it gets even better. That evening, Esther hosts her banquet. The king and Haman are both there. And the king asks, "Queen Esther, what is your request? Whatever it is, I'll give it to you."

And Esther drops the bomb: "If I have found favor with you, my king — spare my life. And spare my people. Because we have been sold to be destroyed. And the man who plotted this... is sitting right here." She points at Haman. "It's HIM."

The king is FURIOUS. He storms out to the garden. When he comes back, Haman is begging Esther for mercy. The king orders Haman executed — on the VERY gallows Haman had built for Mordecai.

A new decree goes out: the Jewish people are allowed to defend themselves. They are SAVED.

What the enemy planned for evil, God turned completely around for good. The trap was sprung on the trapper. The gallows caught the builder. The whole story FLIPPED.

And that's what My Father does. He doesn't just rescue — He reverses.
