TITLE: Faith Hall of Fame
SERIES: The Ending & the Beginning
EPISODE: 1
SCRIPTURE: Hebrews 11

Imagine a hall — not made of marble or stone, but of faith. A hall stretching back thousands of years, filled with the most extraordinary people who ever lived. Not because they were perfect. Because they believed.

Let me walk you through it.

Abel — the very first one. He brought God his best offering, not his leftovers. And even though his own brother killed him for it, his faith still speaks today.

Enoch walked so closely with God that one day he just... wasn't there anymore. God took him. He never even died.

Noah heard something absolutely ridiculous — "Build a boat. A flood is coming." There was no flood. There was no rain. But Noah picked up his hammer and started building. For years. While everyone laughed. And when the waters rose, that faith kept his whole family alive.

Abraham. God told him, "Leave your home and go to a land I'll show you." He didn't get a map. He didn't get a timeline. He just packed up and went. And Sarah — his wife — she laughed when God said she'd have a baby at ninety years old. But she had that baby. Because God keeps His word.

Moses grew up in a palace. He had everything Egypt could offer — wealth, power, comfort. But he chose to suffer with God's people rather than enjoy the luxury of sin. He saw something more valuable than treasure — he saw Me.

Rahab was an outsider. She wasn't from Israel. She didn't have a perfect past. But when the moment came, she hid the spies and put her faith in the God of Israel. And she was saved.

Then there was David — the shepherd king. Samuel — the boy who heard God's voice. The prophets who spoke truth when nobody wanted to hear it. People who were mocked, beaten, chased, and killed — and never stopped believing.

Here's what's incredible: every single one of them was commended for their faith. But none of them received what was promised in their lifetime. They were looking forward — to Me. To the cross. To the resurrection. To everything I came to do.

They ran their leg of the race. And now? It's your turn.

You are part of this same hall of fame. Your faith — right now, at your age — connects you to Abel, to Abraham, to Moses, to David. You're not too young. You're not too small. You belong here.

So run your race. Keep believing. The God who was faithful to every single one of them is the same God who is faithful to you.
