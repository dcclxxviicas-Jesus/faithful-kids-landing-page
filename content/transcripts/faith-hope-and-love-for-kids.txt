At the end of his famous chapter on love, Paul writes one final line that sums up everything: "And now these three remain: faith, hope, and love. But the greatest of these is love."

Three things that last forever. Everything else fades — money, popularity, trends, technology — but faith, hope, and love? They endure. They're eternal. And today I want to show you why these three are the foundation of everything.

Let's start with faith.

The book of Hebrews puts it like this: "Faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen." That's a big sentence, so let me break it down. Faith means trusting something is real even when you can't see it. You can't see the wind, but you feel it. You can't see gravity, but you trust it every time you take a step. Faith in me is like that. You can't see me with your eyes — but you trust me with your life. You believe my promises are true, even before they come true.

Faith is how you walk with me. Every day, you make choices based on trust. Do I believe God is good — even when life is hard? Do I believe his plan is better than mine — even when I can't see the road ahead? That's faith. And it pleases me more than you know.

Now hope.

Biblical hope isn't like wishing on a birthday candle. It's not crossing your fingers and saying, "I hope it works out." Biblical hope is confident expectation. It's knowing that because God has promised, it will happen. Hope says, "I don't know when, and I don't know how — but I know God keeps his word." Hope looks forward to what I've promised — a future with no tears, no pain, no death. And it gives you strength to get through today, because you know tomorrow is in my hands.

And then love.

We spent a whole episode on this, and Paul says it's the greatest of the three. Why? Because faith will one day become sight — you'll see me face to face. Hope will one day be fulfilled — everything I've promised will come true. But love? Love never ends. It's eternal. It's the very nature of God. Love is what holds everything together.

Here's how these three work in your daily life. Faith trusts me. When you can't see the answer, faith says, "God's got this." Hope looks forward. When the day is dark, hope says, "Morning is coming." And love puts it all into action. Love is faith and hope with hands and feet — going out into the world and making a difference.

Build your life on these three. Not on grades. Not on likes. Not on what other people think of you. Faith, hope, and love. They will never let you down.

And the greatest of these is love.
