Hey, friends! Welcome to the final episode of our Exodus journey. We've saved the best for last. Everything — the plagues, the Passover, the Red Sea, the manna, the commandments, even the golden calf — it all leads to this moment.

After the golden calf mess, Moses did something bold. He said to God, "If your presence doesn't go with us, don't send us anywhere." He didn't ask for an easier road or a bigger army. He asked for GOD. Without God's presence, nothing else mattered.

Then Moses went further: "Show me your glory." He wanted to SEE God. And God said, "I'll pass by you, but you can't see my face — no one can and live. I'll put you in a crack in the rock and cover you with my hand, and you'll see my back." And that's what happened. God's glory passed by Moses, and God declared His own name: "The Lord — compassionate, gracious, slow to anger, overflowing with love and faithfulness!" THAT is who God is. Not angry and distant. Compassionate. Overflowing with love.

God gave Moses new stone tablets to replace the shattered ones. A fresh start. That's what God does — He restores what's broken.

Then came the building project. God gave detailed instructions for the Tabernacle — a beautiful tent where He would meet His people. The people brought offerings — gold, silver, fabric, wood, oil, spices — so much that Moses had to tell them to STOP giving. Can you imagine? "Please, no more generosity!" That's what happens when hearts are truly changed.

Skilled workers built the Tabernacle exactly as God designed it. Every detail mattered. And when it was finished, something breathtaking happened.

The cloud of God's glory came down and filled the Tabernacle. So thick, so powerful, that even Moses couldn't go inside. God — the Creator of the universe — moved in. Right in the center of their camp.

THAT is the whole point of Exodus. It's not just about escaping Egypt. God rescues His people so He can live WITH them. He doesn't save you and say, "Good luck, you're on your own!" He says, "I'm staying. I'm with you — every step, every day, forever."

That Tabernacle was pointing to me. One day, I put on flesh and moved into your neighborhood — Emmanuel, God WITH you. And now, through my Spirit, I don't just live near you. I live IN you.

You are never alone. The God of the universe has made His home with YOU.

What an adventure! Thanks for walking through Exodus with me. I'll see you soon!
