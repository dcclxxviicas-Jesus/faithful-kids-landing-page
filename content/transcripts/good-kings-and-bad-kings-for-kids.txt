Hey everyone — strap in! After the kingdom split, centuries of kings followed — some incredible, some terrible. The contrast is stunning.

The Northern Kingdom — Israel — spiraled almost immediately. Jeroboam was worried his people would go to Jerusalem to worship and return to Rehoboam. So he built two golden calves — one in Dan, one in Bethel — and said, "Here are your gods who brought you out of Egypt!" Sound familiar? It's EXACTLY what Israel did at Mount Sinai. They didn't learn. From that point, nearly every king of Israel was evil — worshipping idols, ignoring prophets, pulling people further from God.

Down south in Judah? Different story. Not perfect — kings like Ahaz sacrificed his own children to false gods. But God raised up remarkable leaders too.

Take Hezekiah. The Assyrian empire — the most terrifying army on earth — surrounded Jerusalem. Their commander mocked God: "No god has ever stopped Assyria. What makes YOUR God different?" Hezekiah didn't panic. He took that threatening letter into the temple, spread it before God, and prayed: "Lord, You alone are God. Save us so the world may know You are the Lord."

That night, God sent an angel who struck down 185,000 Assyrian soldiers. The empire retreated. One man's faith moved the hand of God.

Then there's Josiah — maybe my favorite. He became king at EIGHT years old. And he loved God with his whole heart. When he was about twenty-six, workers repairing the temple found a scroll — the Book of the Law, lost and forgotten for generations.

When Josiah heard it read, he tore his robes and wept. He realized how far the nation had drifted. And he didn't just feel bad — he DID something. He tore down every idol. He removed pagan altars. He led the greatest spiritual revival Judah had ever seen. An eight-year-old kid grew up to change an entire nation.

Here's the lesson: one person's faithfulness can change EVERYTHING. You might think, "I'm just one kid. What difference can I make?" Ask Hezekiah. Ask Josiah. Age doesn't disqualify you — it never did.

You could be the one who changes your family, your school, your community. Don't underestimate what God can do through ONE person fully devoted to Him.
