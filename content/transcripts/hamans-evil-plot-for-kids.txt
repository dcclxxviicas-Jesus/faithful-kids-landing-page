TITLE: Haman's Evil Plot
SERIES: The Return Home — Episode 8
SCRIPTURE: Esther 3-5

Things are about to get VERY intense. Because the villain just walked onto the stage — and he's not playing around.

His name is Haman. He's the king's right-hand man — the highest official in the empire. And here's the deal: when Haman walks by, EVERYONE bows. It's the rule. Heads down, knees bent, total respect.

Everyone bows... except one man. Mordecai.

See, Mordecai refused to bow to Haman. Not because he was rude. But because Mordecai bowed to God alone. And this absolutely INFURIATED Haman. His ego couldn't handle it. One man wouldn't kneel, and it ruined his entire day.

But here's what's terrifying — Haman didn't just want revenge on Mordecai. He decided to destroy ALL the Jewish people in the entire empire. Every man, woman, and child. He went to King Xerxes and said, "There's a certain group of people who don't follow your laws. They're dangerous. Let me deal with them." And the king — not knowing Haman was talking about the Jews, not knowing his own QUEEN was Jewish — signed the decree. A date was set. The order went out across 127 provinces: on that day, every Jewish person would be killed.

When Mordecai heard, he tore his clothes, covered himself in ashes, and wailed in the streets. The entire Jewish community was in shock. And Mordecai sent an urgent message to Esther: "YOU have to go to the king. You have to stop this."

But here's the problem. In Persia, you don't just walk up to the king. If you approach the throne without being invited, you die — unless the king extends his golden scepter. Esther hadn't been called to the king in THIRTY DAYS.

Then Mordecai delivered one of the most powerful lines in the entire Bible: "Who knows — perhaps you were made queen for such a time as this?"

THAT line. That's the moment Esther realized this wasn't about a crown or a palace. This was about purpose. This was why God put her there.

And Esther's response? Just as legendary: "Gather all the Jews. Fast for me for three days. Do not eat or drink. And then I will go to the king. And if I perish — I perish."

No guarantees. No safety net. Just raw, unfiltered courage.

Here's the truth: the moment you were made for might be the scariest moment of your life. Courage isn't optional — it's the whole point. You don't have to feel brave. You just have to show up.
