Hey kids! Today's story is about a man who had never — not once in his entire life — seen a sunrise, a face, or a single color. Born blind. And what happened to him changed EVERYTHING.

My disciples and I were walking along when we passed a man blind from birth. His whole world was darkness. And my disciples asked a question people STILL ask: "Rabbi, who sinned? This man or his parents? Why was he born like this?"

And I stopped them right there. "NEITHER," I said. "This man wasn't born blind because someone messed up. This happened so that God's WORK could be displayed in his life."

Read that again. Sometimes the hardest things in your life aren't punishments. They're SETUPS for God to show off.

Then I did something nobody expected. I spit on the ground, mixed it into mud with my fingers, and smeared it right onto the man's eyes. I know — not exactly what you'd find in a medical textbook! Then I told him, "Go. Wash in the Pool of Siloam."

Now think about this: he couldn't SEE me. He didn't know what I looked like. He just had mud on his face and a stranger's voice telling him to go wash in a specific pool. He had every reason to say, "This is ridiculous." But instead — he WENT.

He stumbled his way to the Pool of Siloam, knelt down, splashed that water on his face, and when he opened his eyes...

He could SEE.

For the FIRST TIME in his life, the world exploded into light and color and shapes and faces. Can you even imagine? The first sunset. The first time seeing your own mother's face. Everything brand new.

But here's where it gets wild. The Pharisees — the religious leaders — got FURIOUS. They interrogated the man. They interrogated his PARENTS. They refused to believe because I had healed him on the Sabbath, and that broke their rules.

And this formerly blind man with no theological training delivered one of the greatest lines in the entire Bible:

"I don't know all the theology. I don't have all the answers. But I know ONE thing: I was blind, and NOW I SEE."

BOOM. That's it. That's the whole testimony. You don't need a seminary degree to share what God has done in your life. You just need your story.

Your experience with me is POWERFUL. If you've been changed, SAY SO. Your story might be the exact thing someone else needs to hear.
