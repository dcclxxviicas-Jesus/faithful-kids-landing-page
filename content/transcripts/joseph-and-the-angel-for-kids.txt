Welcome back! Last time I told you about my mom — how an angel appeared to Mary and she said yes to God's incredible plan. But today I need to tell you about someone else. Someone who doesn't always get the spotlight, but without him, my story wouldn't be the same. I'm talking about Joseph. My earthly dad.

Joseph was a carpenter. Strong hands. Honest heart. The kind of guy who always tried to do the right thing. And he was deeply in love with Mary. They were engaged — and in those days, an engagement was basically as serious as marriage. You didn't just break it off.

Then Joseph found out Mary was pregnant.

Think about how that hit him. He knew the baby wasn't his. He didn't know about the angel or God's plan yet. All he knew was that the woman he loved appeared to have betrayed him. It crushed him.

But here's what tells you everything about Joseph's character — he didn't explode. He didn't try to humiliate Mary in public. The Bible says he planned to divorce her quietly, to protect her reputation. Even when his heart was breaking, he was thinking about what was best for her.

That right there? That's a real man.

But God wasn't going to leave Joseph in the dark. That night, an angel appeared to him in a dream and said, "Joseph, son of David, don't be afraid to take Mary as your wife. The child inside her is from the Holy Spirit. She will have a son, and you will name Him Jesus — because He will save His people from their sins."

Jesus. My name means "God saves." That wasn't an accident. It was a mission statement.

Now put yourself in Joseph's sandals. He wakes up from this dream. Nobody else heard the angel. Nobody else saw a thing. If he goes through with this — marrying a woman who's already pregnant — everyone in town is going to talk. His reputation, his business, his friendships — all on the line.

What did Joseph do? He got up and obeyed. He took Mary as his wife. No questions. No conditions. Just trust.

Here's the lesson that hits me right in the heart: sometimes doing the right thing doesn't make sense to anyone else. People might judge you. They might not understand. But when God tells you to move — you move. Joseph trusted God in the dark, and because he did, he got to be part of the greatest story ever told.

See you next time!
