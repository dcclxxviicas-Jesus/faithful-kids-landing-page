Hey there, friend! Have you ever had to step up and do something that felt WAY too big for you? Maybe take over a project, lead a team, or just walk into a room where you didn't know a single person? Yeah. Multiply that feeling by about a million, and you'll understand what Joshua was going through.

See, Moses — the greatest leader Israel had ever known — was gone. The man who stood before Pharaoh, who parted the Red Sea, who talked with God face to face on a mountain covered in fire and thunder... gone. And now guess who had to fill those sandals?

Joshua.

Can you imagine? "Hey Joshua, you know that guy who did all those impossible things? Yeah, you're up next. Good luck!" I mean, talk about pressure! The entire nation of Israel — millions of people — was looking at him like, "So... what's the plan?"

But here's where it gets good. Because God didn't just hand Joshua the job and walk away. No way. My Father pulled Joshua close and spoke directly to him. And what did He say? "Be strong and courageous."

Once? Nope. He said it THREE times. Be strong and courageous. Be strong and courageous. BE STRONG AND COURAGEOUS. When God repeats Himself like that, you better believe He means it!

And then came the promise that changed everything: "I will be with you wherever you go. I will never leave you. I will never abandon you."

Do you hear that? God didn't say, "Joshua, you're so talented, you'll be fine." He didn't say, "Don't worry, it'll be easy." He said, "I will be WITH you." That's the difference. Joshua's courage wasn't supposed to come from Joshua. It was supposed to come from the God standing right beside him.

So Joshua stood up. He squared his shoulders. He looked at the people of Israel and said, "Get ready! In three days, we cross the Jordan River. We're going into the land God promised us!"

And the people? They rallied. They said, "Whatever you command us, we will do. Wherever you send us, we will go!"

After four hundred years of slavery and forty years of wandering in the desert, they were FINALLY about to step into the Promised Land.

Here's what I want you to hold onto: courage is NOT the absence of fear. Joshua was probably terrified. But courage is trusting God even when your knees are shaking. So whatever feels too big for you right now? Remember — I am with you wherever you go. And I never, ever break My promises.
