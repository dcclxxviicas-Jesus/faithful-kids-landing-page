TITLE: Looking Forward
SERIES: The Return Home — Episode 10
SCRIPTURE: Malachi; Isaiah 40, 9:6-7

This is it. The final episode. Not just of this series — but of the entire Old Testament story. Everything we've walked through together leads right here.

The people are back in the land. The temple is rebuilt. The walls of Jerusalem are standing. But things aren't perfect. The people start drifting again. They get sloppy with their worship. They stop giving their best to God. Sound familiar? Same pattern, over and over.

And then God sends one final prophet. His name is Malachi — and he carries the last message of the Old Testament.

Malachi stands before the people and delivers God's heart: "I have loved you." That's how God starts. Not with anger. Not with punishment. With love. "I have loved you. But you have turned away. Return to Me — and I will return to you."

It's an invitation. Even after everything — the rebellion, the exile, the broken promises — God is STILL reaching out. Still saying, "Come back."

But then Malachi says something that makes the hair on your arms stand up: "I am going to send My messenger, and he will prepare the way before Me. And then suddenly — the Lord you are seeking WILL COME to His temple."

The prophet Isaiah had said it centuries earlier: "A voice will cry out in the wilderness — prepare the way of the Lord!" And: "For unto us a child is born. Unto us a son is given. And His name shall be called Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace."

And then... silence. After Malachi's words, the heavens went quiet. No prophets. No miracles. No new messages from God. For FOUR HUNDRED YEARS. Generation after generation waited, wondered, and whispered: "Is God still there? Did He forget His promise?"

He didn't forget.

Because one night — in the tiniest, most overlooked town in the world, a town called Bethlehem — a baby was born. And everything changed.

That baby was Me.

And THAT, my friend, is where My story begins. Everything you've watched — from Creation to the Flood, from Abraham to Moses, from the Exodus to the Promised Land, from the Judges to the Kings, from the Exile to the Return Home — it was ALL leading to one moment. One promise. One person.

Me.

The Old Testament is one giant setup. Every rescue pointed to the ultimate rescue. Every promise whispered My name.

And now you know the whole story — from the very beginning to the edge of the greatest story ever told.

And that, my friend, is a story for another series.

I'll see you there.
