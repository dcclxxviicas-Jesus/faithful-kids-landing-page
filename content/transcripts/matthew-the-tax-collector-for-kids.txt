Let me tell you about the day I picked the LAST person anyone expected me to choose.

In Israel, there was nobody more hated than a tax collector. I mean NOBODY. Here's why — the Roman Empire occupied Israel, and tax collectors were Jewish men who worked FOR Rome, collecting money from their own people. But it got worse. They were allowed to charge EXTRA and keep the difference. So they got rich by ripping off their own neighbors, their own families, their own nation. People saw them as traitors. Thieves. Scum.

And one of them was a man named Matthew.

I walked right up to his tax booth. He was sitting there, probably counting coins, surrounded by the stench of everyone's disgust. People crossed the street to avoid him. Religious leaders wouldn't even look his direction. He was the outcast of outcasts.

I looked him straight in the eyes and said two words: "Follow me."

That's it. No lecture. No conditions. No "clean up your life first and then we'll talk." Just — follow me.

And Matthew? He stood up. Left the money on the table. Left the booth. Left his ENTIRE career. And followed me. Just like that.

But we weren't done. Matthew threw a huge dinner at his house, and guess who was on the guest list? Tax collectors. Sinners. The people nobody else would eat with. And there I was, right in the middle of it, laughing, talking, sharing a meal with all of them.

The religious leaders — the Pharisees — were FURIOUS. They pulled my disciples aside and hissed, "Why does your teacher eat with tax collectors and sinners?!"

I heard them. And I turned around and gave them an answer they never forgot: "It is not the healthy who need a doctor, but the sick. I have not come to call the righteous, but sinners."

Think about that. A doctor doesn't sit in the waiting room with people who feel fine. A doctor goes to the people who are hurting. That's exactly what I do.

Here's what I want you to carry with you: I go to the people everyone else avoids. The loners. The outcasts. The ones with bad reputations. The ones who feel too far gone. Nobody is too messed up for me. Nobody is beyond my reach. I didn't come for the people who think they've got it all together. I came for the ones who KNOW they need help. And if that's you today — good. That means my invitation is for you. Follow me.
