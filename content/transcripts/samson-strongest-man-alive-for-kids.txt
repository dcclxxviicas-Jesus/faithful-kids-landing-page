Hey, everyone! Okay, buckle up because today we're meeting the most physically powerful person in the entire Bible. A man who could rip a lion apart with his bare hands. A man who defeated an entire army single-handedly. His name is Samson, and his story is equal parts amazing and heartbreaking.

Before Samson was even born, an angel appeared to his parents: "You're going to have a son, set apart for God from the moment he's born." Samson was to be a Nazirite — specific rules. No wine, no unclean food, and absolutely no cutting his hair. His hair was the symbol of his dedication to God, and through that dedication, God gave him supernatural strength.

And I mean SUPERNATURAL.

As a young man, Samson is walking when a full-grown, roaring lion charges straight at him. Does he run? Nope. The Spirit of God rushes upon him and he tears that lion apart with his bare hands. No weapon. No armor. Just raw, God-given power.

Later, when the Philistines — Israel's biggest enemies — come after him, Samson picks up a jawbone of a donkey and defeats a thousand soldiers. One man. One jawbone. A thousand enemies. The dude is unstoppable.

But here's where the story starts to crack.

Samson is reckless. He's impulsive. He constantly breaks his Nazirite vows. He touches dead things he shouldn't touch. He goes to parties he shouldn't attend. He chases relationships that pull him away from God. He has all this incredible strength, but he can't seem to control himself. He keeps pushing the boundaries, thinking the rules don't apply to him.

It's like watching someone with the fastest car in the world who refuses to learn how to steer. All that horsepower, and he's headed straight for a wall.

See, Samson had external strength — the kind everyone could see. But he was missing internal strength — the discipline, the self-control, the wisdom to use his gifts the right way. And that gap between his power and his character? That's what's eventually going to bring him down.

Here's the lesson. Strength without self-control is dangerous. It doesn't matter how talented, gifted, or strong you are — if you can't master yourself, your gifts will eventually master you.

True power isn't about what you CAN do. It's about what you CHOOSE to do — and what you choose NOT to do. Discipline isn't the enemy of freedom. Discipline is what makes freedom possible.

I gave Samson everything he needed. What he did with it? That was up to him. And the same is true for you.

What are you doing with what I've given you?
