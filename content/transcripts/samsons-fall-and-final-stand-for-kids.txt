Hey friend! Last time we met the strongest man who ever lived — Samson. Ripped apart a lion. Defeated armies single-handedly. Unstoppable. But today? Today you're going to see what happens when the strongest person in the room makes the weakest choices. This story is both heartbreaking and incredible. Let's go.

After all of his victories, Samson fell in love with a woman named Delilah. And the Philistine rulers saw their chance. They went to Delilah and said, "Find out the secret of his strength. We'll each pay you eleven hundred pieces of silver." That is a FORTUNE. And Delilah said yes.

So she started asking. "Samson, tell me — what makes you so strong?" At first, Samson lied. He made up fake answers three different times. Each time Delilah tested it, and each time Samson broke free. You'd think after three tries he'd realize — she's working against me!

But Delilah didn't stop. Day after day she nagged and pressured him until his soul was "vexed to death." And finally, Samson told her everything. "I'm a Nazirite. Set apart for God since birth. A razor has never touched my head. If my hair is shaved, my strength will leave me."

That night, while Samson slept on Delilah's lap, she called for a man to shave off his hair. And when he woke up and tried to fight — nothing. His strength was gone. The Bible says the most devastating sentence: "He did not know that the Lord had left him."

The Philistines grabbed him, gouged out his eyes, chained him up, and made him grind grain like an animal. The strongest man alive — blind, broken, and enslaved. All because of his choices.

But here's where it gets powerful. While Samson was grinding away in that dark prison, his hair started growing back. And one day, the Philistines threw a massive celebration to their false god, and they brought Samson out to mock him. Thousands of them packed into a huge temple, laughing at him.

Samson asked the boy leading him to place his hands on the two main pillars holding the building up. And he prayed one final prayer: "Lord God, remember me. Strengthen me just once more."

Then Samson pushed with everything he had. The pillars cracked. The roof came crashing down. Samson took more enemies with him in his death than he had in his entire life.

Here's the lesson, and it's a big one: your choices have real consequences. Samson's bad decisions cost him his eyes, his freedom, and his life. BUT — and don't miss this — God still answered when Samson called out to Him. Even after failure, God doesn't throw you away. You might face consequences, but you are NEVER beyond His reach.

Remember that. Always.
