THE RISE OF KINGS — Episode 2: Saul — The First King
Based on 1 Samuel 9-11 | Narrated by Jesus

Welcome back! Last time, Israel demanded a king. And God said, "Alright — you'll get one." So buckle up, because Israel's first king is about to walk onto the stage, and he is NOT what you'd expect.

Meet Saul, son of Kish, from the tribe of Benjamin. This guy was tall — I'm talking head and shoulders above EVERYONE in Israel. Strong jaw. Impressive build. If you were casting a movie for "guy who looks like a king," Saul would get the part instantly.

But here's what's wild — Saul was actually humble at first. When Samuel told him privately that God had chosen him to be king, Saul said, "Me? I'm from Benjamin — the smallest tribe! And my family is the least important in the whole tribe!" He genuinely couldn't believe it.

Samuel anointed Saul by pouring oil on his head — that was how you set someone apart for God's purpose. And the Spirit of God came on Saul in power. He even started prophesying! People who knew him were shocked: "Wait — is SAUL with the prophets now?"

Then came the big public moment. Samuel gathered all of Israel to reveal their new king. They cast lots, and it pointed to the tribe of Benjamin... then to Saul's family... then to Saul himself. But when they went to find him — he was HIDING. Among the baggage! Can you picture it? The tallest guy in Israel, crouching behind luggage, hoping nobody would notice.

They dragged him out, and Samuel said, "Look at the one God has chosen! There's no one like him!" And the people shouted, "Long live the king!"

Saul started strong, too. When the Ammonites threatened the city of Jabesh-Gilead, Saul rallied 330,000 soldiers overnight and crushed the enemy. The nation went wild! It seemed like they had made the right choice.

But here's what I need you to hear. There were cracks forming beneath the surface. Small ones. The kind you don't notice until the whole thing crumbles.

And that's the lesson: how you START isn't nearly as important as how you FINISH. Saul looked perfect. He sounded humble. He won early battles. But the real test wasn't in the spotlight — it was coming in the quiet moments when nobody was watching.

How you handle the small, hidden moments — that's what reveals who you really are.

See you in the next episode — because things are about to go very, very wrong.
