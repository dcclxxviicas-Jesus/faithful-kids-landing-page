Hey everyone. Today's story is a hard one. Because the wisest man who ever lived is about to make the dumbest mistake imaginable.

We've watched Solomon shine — the wisdom, the temple, the proverbs, the fame. But now everything unravels. And it starts in a place you might not expect — his heart.

Solomon loved many foreign women. Not two or three. The Bible says SEVEN HUNDRED wives and three hundred concubines. Many came from nations God specifically warned about — Moab, Ammon, Edom, Sidon. God said, "Do not intermarry with them, because they WILL turn your hearts after their gods."

And that's exactly what happened.

As Solomon got older, these wives turned his heart toward their gods. The man who built the Temple of the Living God started building altars to foreign idols. Ashtoreth of the Sidonians. Chemosh of Moab. Molek of the Ammonites. The same hands that dedicated the temple were now building shrines to fake gods.

How does that happen? The way it always does — slowly. One small compromise at a time. Solomon didn't wake up and say, "I'll abandon God today." It was a slow drift. "It's no big deal." "It's just one altar." But small compromises lead to big collapses. Every. Single. Time.

God was grieved. He told Solomon directly: "Because you have not kept My covenant, I will tear the kingdom away from you. However — for David's sake, I will not do it during your lifetime. I will tear it from the hand of your son."

Even in anger, God showed mercy. But consequences were set in motion.

Here's what breaks my heart: Solomon KNEW better. He literally wrote the book on wisdom! But knowing what's right and DOING what's right are two very different things.

That's the lesson. Wisdom without obedience is meaningless. You can ace every Bible quiz and know all the right answers — but if you don't LIVE it out, it means nothing.

Guard your heart. Watch the small compromises. The drift always starts small.
