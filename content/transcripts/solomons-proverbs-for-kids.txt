Hey — welcome back! Here's something wild: Solomon didn't just USE his wisdom — he wrote it down. Over three THOUSAND proverbs! And these aren't dusty old sayings. These are God's cheat codes for life. Let me walk you through the greatest hits.

First — Proverbs 3:5-6. "Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and lean not on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge Him, and He will make your paths straight." You know that feeling when you're facing a big decision and you have no idea what to do? This proverb says — don't rely only on what YOU think. Bring it to God. He sees what you can't.

Next — Proverbs 9:10. "The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom." Now "fear" here doesn't mean being scared of God like He's a monster. It means deep respect — standing in awe of how powerful and good He is. THAT's where real wisdom starts. Not with straight A's or followers. It starts with knowing God.

Here's one for when someone gets under your skin — Proverbs 15:1. "A gentle answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger." Someone says something mean to you at lunch. Fire back nastier — and the whole thing explodes. OR respond calmly — and watch the tension melt. That gentle answer isn't weakness. It's power under control.

Proverbs 22:6 — "Train up a child in the way they should go, and when they are old, they will not depart from it." That's about YOU. The habits you build right now — prayer, kindness, honesty — they're shaping who you'll become forever. What you practice today, you become tomorrow.

And finally — Proverbs 27:17. "As iron sharpens iron, so one person sharpens another." A blade gets sharper grinding against another blade. That's what real friendship looks like. Good friends challenge you to be better. They tell you what you NEED to hear, not just what you want to hear.

Bottom line: Solomon's proverbs are a gift from God — practical, powerful, and timeless. Tuck them into your heart. Because wisdom isn't just about knowing things — it's about living right.
