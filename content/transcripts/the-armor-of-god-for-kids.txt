I need to tell you something that might surprise you. You're in a battle. Right now. Every single day. Not a battle with fists or swords — a spiritual battle. There's an enemy who wants to steal your peace, wreck your confidence, and pull you away from me.

But here's the good news: I've already won the war. And I've given you everything you need to stand firm. Paul described it like a suit of armor. Let me walk you through it.

First — the Belt of Truth. A soldier's belt held everything together. Truth does the same thing for your life. When you know what's true — that God loves you, that his Word is real, that you have a purpose — lies can't knock you off balance. Buckle up with truth every day.

Second — the Breastplate of Righteousness. A breastplate protects your heart. When the enemy whispers, "You're not good enough. You've messed up too many times" — righteousness is your answer. Not your own righteousness — mine. I've made you right with God. Your heart is protected.

Third — Shoes of the Gospel of Peace. A soldier needs solid footing. Your feet are fitted with peace — the peace that comes from knowing the gospel. No matter what's shaking around you, you stand firm because you know who you belong to. You don't have to be anxious. You're grounded.

Fourth — the Shield of Faith. This one stops the enemy's flaming arrows — the doubts, the fears, the lies that come out of nowhere. When you hold up faith, you're saying, "I trust God no matter what." And those arrows? They go out like sparks hitting water.

Fifth — the Helmet of Salvation. Your helmet protects your mind. The enemy loves to attack your thoughts — telling you you're worthless, unloved, hopeless. But when you wear the helmet of salvation, you remember who you are: saved, chosen, and loved. Guard your mind.

And finally — the Sword of the Spirit, which is the Word of God. This is your only offensive weapon. Everything else is defense. But the Word of God? It fights back. When you know Scripture, you can cut through every lie the enemy throws at you. I used it myself when I was tempted in the wilderness.

You're in a battle whether you know it or not. But I've given you everything you need. Put on this armor every single day. Belt, breastplate, shoes, shield, helmet, sword.

You are equipped. You are ready. Now stand firm.
