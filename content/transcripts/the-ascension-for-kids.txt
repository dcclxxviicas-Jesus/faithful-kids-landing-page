Let me tell you about the day I left earth — and why it was the best thing that could have happened.

Forty days. That is how long I stayed after the resurrection. Forty days of walking with my disciples, eating with them, teaching them, proving beyond any shadow of doubt that I was alive. I appeared to Peter, to the eleven, to more than five hundred people at once. No one could say it was a dream or a trick. I was ALIVE — and I made sure everyone knew it.

But the time came for me to go home.

I gathered my disciples together one final time near the village of Bethany, just outside Jerusalem. And I gave them one last instruction — the most important instruction they had ever received.

"Do not leave Jerusalem. Wait for the gift my Father promised — the one I told you about. John baptized with water, but in a few days, you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit."

They were excited but confused. They asked me, "Lord, are you going to restore the kingdom to Israel NOW?"

I told them: "It is not for you to know the times the Father has set. But you WILL receive power when the Holy Spirit comes on you. And you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth."

Jerusalem — your own backyard. Judea — your region. Samaria — the places that feel uncomfortable. The ends of the earth — EVERYWHERE.

And then — right in front of their eyes — I was lifted up. Up into the sky. Higher and higher until a cloud received me and I disappeared from their sight.

They just stood there. Staring up. Mouths wide open.

Then two angels appeared in white robes and said: "Men of Galilee, why do you stand here looking into the sky? This same Jesus, who has been taken from you into heaven, will come back in the same way you have seen Him go."

I did not leave you alone. I left so something BETTER could come. The Holy Spirit — God himself — living INSIDE you. Not walking beside you like I did with the twelve. INSIDE you. Every hour. Every moment.

And one day — and I mean this — I am coming back. Same Jesus. Same way. Count on it.

LESSON: Jesus left so the Holy Spirit could come and live inside every believer. He did not abandon us — He upgraded the plan. And He promised to return.
