So the Israelites are still trudging through the wilderness, and — you guessed it — they start complaining. Again. "Why did you bring us out of Egypt? There's no food! There's no water! And we HATE this miserable manna!" They're trashing the food that God literally drops out of the sky for them every morning. Imagine your mom making you breakfast every day for free and you saying, "This is garbage." Not cool.

This time, God sends venomous snakes into the camp. And these aren't little garden snakes — these are fiery serpents, and their bites are deadly. People are getting bitten left and right. People are dying. Suddenly, nobody's complaining about the manna anymore. Funny how that works.

The people rush to Moses, terrified. "We sinned! We spoke against God and against you! Please, PRAY — make the snakes go away!"

Moses prays. And here's what God tells him to do — and this is the part that should make every hair on your arms stand up. God says, "Make a bronze serpent and put it on a pole. Anyone who has been bitten just needs to LOOK at it, and they will live."

That's it. No medicine. No magic. No complicated ritual. Just look — and live.

Moses makes the bronze snake, lifts it up on a pole, and every single person who looked at it was healed. Every one.

Now here's why this story means so much to Me.

Hundreds of years later, I told a man named Nicodemus this: "Just as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, so must the Son of Man be lifted up, that whoever believes in Him may have eternal life." That's from John chapter 3, verse 14.

Do you see it? The bronze serpent on that pole was a picture — a preview — of Me on the cross. The Israelites were dying from snake bites, and the cure was to look up at what God provided. You and I? We have a sin problem. And the cure is the same — look to Me, lifted up on the cross, and live.

You don't have to earn it. You don't have to be perfect. You just have to look to Me and believe.

The Israelites didn't heal themselves. They just looked up. That's what faith is — not trusting in your own strength, but looking to what God has provided.

So let me ask you this: where are you looking? Because the cure for everything that's broken is right here. Just look up.
