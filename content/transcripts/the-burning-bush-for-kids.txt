Okay, so last time we left Moses running for his life, right? He went from living in a palace to hiding out in the middle of nowhere -- a place called Midian. And what's he doing there? Herding sheep. Yep, sheep. The guy who grew up as Egyptian royalty is now chasing fluffy animals around the desert. Life is funny like that sometimes.

But here's the thing -- Moses spent FORTY YEARS out there. Forty! That's not a quick timeout. That's a whole career of walking around dusty hills with a bunch of sheep who don't exactly have great conversation skills.

Then one day, everything changes.

Moses is leading his flock near Mount Horeb when he sees something that makes zero sense. A bush is on fire. Okay, not that weird -- it's the desert, things get dry. BUT THIS BUSH ISN'T BURNING UP. The flames are dancing, the bush is blazing, but not a single leaf turns to ash. Moses thinks, "I gotta check this out."

And as he walks toward it, a voice comes from the bush: "Moses! Moses!"

He says, "Here I am."

"Take off your sandals. You're standing on holy ground."

Whoa. This isn't just a weird nature moment. This is GOD. The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, speaking directly to Moses from a burning bush.

And God says something incredible: "I've seen how my people are suffering in Egypt. I've heard them crying out. And I'm going to rescue them. And guess what, Moses -- YOU'RE the one I'm sending to do it."

Can you imagine Moses' face right now? He's like, "Me?! I'm a SHEPHERD. I'm a fugitive! Pharaoh wants me dead!" And then he hits God with every excuse in the book: "Who am I to go to Pharaoh?" "What if they don't believe me?" "I'm not a good speaker -- I stumble over my words!"

Every single excuse. Sound familiar? Ever told God -- or yourself -- all the reasons you CAN'T do something?

But God doesn't budge. He tells Moses His name: "I AM WHO I AM." Not "I was" or "I might be." I AM. Present tense. Always here, always powerful, always enough.

Then God gives Moses signs -- his staff turns into a snake, his hand turns leprous and back again. And for the speaking problem? God says, "Fine, your brother Aaron can be your spokesperson. But YOU are going."

No more excuses.

Here's what I want you to know: God doesn't call the qualified -- He qualifies the called. Moses felt completely unready. But God didn't need Moses to be perfect. He just needed Moses to show up.

Same goes for you. Whatever you think disqualifies you? It doesn't. I can use anyone who's willing. Even a shepherd with a stutter standing in front of a bush that won't stop burning.

Are you willing? That's the only question that matters.
