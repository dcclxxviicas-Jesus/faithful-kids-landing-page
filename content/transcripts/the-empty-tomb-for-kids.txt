Let me tell you about the greatest morning in the history of the world.

It was Sunday. The sky was still dark. My body had been in a sealed tomb since Friday — wrapped in burial cloths, behind a massive stone, guarded by Roman soldiers. Everyone thought the story was over.

They were SO wrong.

Before the sun came up, the earth started shaking. A violent earthquake. And an angel — blazing like lightning, clothes white as snow — came down from heaven and rolled that enormous stone away. Now here is something I want you to understand: the angel did not roll the stone away to let me OUT. I was already gone. He rolled it away to let people IN — so they could see for themselves.

The Roman guards? Tough, trained soldiers? They shook with fear and dropped like dead men. Fainted. Out cold.

Then came the women — Mary Magdalene, the other Mary, and others — walking through the dawn carrying burial spices. They expected to find a body. Instead they found an empty tomb and an angel sitting right on top of that rolled-away stone, casual as can be.

The angel said: "Do not be afraid. I know you are looking for Jesus who was crucified. He is not here — He has RISEN, just as He said! Come, see the place where He lay."

The women ran. They were trembling, scared, amazed, bursting with joy all at the same time. Can you imagine?

Meanwhile, Peter and John heard the news and SPRINTED to the tomb. John was faster — he got there first and peeked inside. Peter, being Peter, charged straight in. And what did they find? The grave clothes, neatly folded. No body. Just the evidence that something impossible had happened.

Here is what I want you to know: death thought it had me. The grave thought it could hold me. It could not. I am alive. I walked out of that tomb under my own power because death has no authority over the Author of Life.

And because I live — YOU will live too. That is my promise. The resurrection changes everything. Death is not the end. It is just a door.

And I have the keys.

LESSON: The resurrection of Jesus changes everything. Death is real, but it is not the end. Because Jesus is alive, everyone who trusts in Him has the promise of life that never ends.
