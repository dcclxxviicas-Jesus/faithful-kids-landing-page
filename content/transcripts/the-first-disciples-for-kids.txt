If you were starting a world-changing mission, who would you pick for your team? The smartest people? The richest? The most powerful? I didn't pick ANY of those. Let me tell you how I built my crew.

It started by the Jordan River. John the Baptist — my cousin, the wild man who wore camel hair and ate locusts — he saw me walking by and shouted to his own followers: "LOOK! The Lamb of God!" Two of John's disciples heard that, and they started following me. Just like that. They left John and came after me.

I turned around and asked them, "What do you want?" They said, "Rabbi, where are you staying?" And I said, "Come and see." That's it. No application form. No interview. Just — come and see. They spent the whole day with me, and their lives were never the same.

One of those two was a man named Andrew. And the FIRST thing Andrew did? He ran to find his brother Simon. He grabbed him and said, "We have found the Messiah!" When Simon came to me, I looked at him — really LOOKED at him — and said, "You are Simon son of John. But you will be called Peter." That means "rock." He didn't look like a rock yet. He was rough, loud, impulsive. But I could see who he was going to BECOME.

Then I found Philip. I just walked up to him and said two words: "Follow me." And he did. Philip turned around and found Nathanael, telling him, "We've found the one Moses and the prophets wrote about — Jesus of Nazareth!" Nathanael was skeptical. He actually said, "Nazareth? Can anything good come from THERE?" But Philip gave him the same invitation I gave: "Come and see."

When Nathanael walked up to me, I said, "Here is a true Israelite, in whom there is no deceit." He was shocked. "How do you KNOW me?" he asked. I told him, "I saw you under the fig tree before Philip called you." His jaw dropped. "Rabbi — you are the Son of God! You are the King of Israel!"

And I told him, "You believe because of THAT? You're going to see much greater things."

Here's what I want you to know: I didn't pick the most impressive people. I picked the most WILLING. Fishermen. Ordinary guys. Skeptics, even. God doesn't need your resume. He doesn't need you to be perfect or popular or have it all figured out. He just needs your YES. So — what's YOUR answer?
