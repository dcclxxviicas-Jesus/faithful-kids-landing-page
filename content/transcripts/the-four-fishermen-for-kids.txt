Have you ever worked SO hard at something — given it everything you've got — and still come up with nothing? That's exactly where Simon Peter was the morning I met him at the lake.

The Sea of Galilee was buzzing. Crowds of people had followed me to the shore, pressing in so close I could barely breathe. They wanted to hear the Word of God, but there was no room to teach. That's when I spotted two boats sitting at the water's edge. The fishermen were out washing their nets — tired, frustrated, and completely empty-handed after fishing ALL night long.

I climbed into Simon Peter's boat and asked him to push out a little from shore. Now picture this — his boat became my pulpit! The water carried my voice across the crowd, and I taught them right there from the waves.

But here's where it gets WILD.

When I finished teaching, I turned to Simon and said, "Put out into deep water, and let down your nets for a catch."

Simon looked at me like I'd lost my mind. He said, "Master, we worked HARD all night and didn't catch a thing." Every bit of his experience was screaming, "This won't work." Fish don't bite in the middle of the day in deep water! But then he said something incredible — "But because YOU say so, I will let down the nets."

Because YOU say so. That's faith right there.

They dropped the nets — and the water EXPLODED with fish. So many fish the nets started ripping apart! They yelled for James and John in the other boat to come help, and when they did, BOTH boats were so full they started to sink!

Simon Peter fell at my knees, trembling. "Go away from me, Lord — I am a sinful man!" He wasn't just amazed. He was OVERWHELMED. He realized he wasn't standing in front of just another teacher. He was in the presence of God.

But I didn't walk away. I looked at him and said, "Don't be afraid. From now on, you will fish for people."

And you know what happened next? They pulled those boats to shore, left EVERYTHING — the fish, the nets, the boats, their whole livelihood — and followed me.

Here's the lesson: when I ask you to try again, trust me. Even when your experience says it's hopeless. Even when you're exhausted. Even when logic says no. Because when you obey in faith, the results will blow your mind. Trust me with your nets.
