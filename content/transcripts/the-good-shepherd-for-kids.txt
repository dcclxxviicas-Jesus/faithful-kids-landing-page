TITLE: The Good Shepherd
SERIES: The Ending & the Beginning
EPISODE: 5
SCRIPTURE: John 10:1-18

I said these words Myself. Standing in front of the crowds, looking into the eyes of people who were tired, lost, and led by leaders who didn't care about them — I said:

"I am the Good Shepherd."

The people who heard Me knew exactly what that meant. They knew shepherds. A shepherd doesn't clock in and clock out. A shepherd lives with the sheep. Sleeps beside them. Walks ahead of them into danger. Carries the weak ones on his shoulders. Calls each one by name.

And they know his voice.

"My sheep listen to My voice," I told them. "I know them, and they follow Me."

Not because they're forced. Because they recognize the voice of the one who loves them. A stranger's voice? They run. But the shepherd's voice — they follow it anywhere.

Then I drew a sharp line. The hired hand works for a paycheck. When the wolf comes — and wolves always come — the hired hand drops everything and runs. He doesn't own the sheep. He doesn't love them. He's not risking his life.

But the Good Shepherd? The Good Shepherd lays down His life for the sheep.

That's what I did. On the cross, I wasn't a victim. I chose it. "No one takes My life from Me," I said. "I lay it down of My own accord. I have authority to lay it down, and authority to take it up again."

I died for you on purpose — willingly, with My eyes wide open — because you are worth it.

And then I said something that stretched this even further: "I have other sheep that are not of this sheep pen. I must bring them also. They too will listen to My voice, and there shall be one flock and one shepherd."

I wasn't just talking about the Jewish people. I was talking about you. Every nation. Every language. Every kid watching this right now. You were always part of the plan.

Do you remember Psalm 23? "The Lord is my shepherd, I shall not want." David wrote that a thousand years before I walked the earth. He knew. Even then, he knew someone was coming who would be the ultimate shepherd.

I am that Shepherd. I know your name. I know your voice. I know what keeps you up at night and what makes you come alive. I am not a hired hand who will bail when things get hard. I laid down My life for you — and I took it back up again.

You are My sheep. And nothing — nothing — can snatch you from My hand.
