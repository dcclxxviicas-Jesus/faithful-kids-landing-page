There was a man named Paul. Before he followed me, he actually hunted down my followers and threw them in prison. But I changed his life — completely. And after that, Paul wrote letters to churches all over the world. His letter to the church in Rome? It's the most powerful explanation of the gospel ever written.

So let me walk you through it — because this message is for you too.

Paul starts with the truth that every person needs to hear: all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God. Every single person. Your parents. Your pastor. Your favorite Christ-following Christ-follower. You. Everyone has missed the mark. No one is perfect.

And sin has a cost. Paul says it plainly: "The wages of sin is death." That means sin earns something — and what it earns is separation from God. That's the bad news.

But here's where the story turns.

"But the gift of God is eternal life through Christ Jesus our Lord." Did you catch that word? Gift. You don't earn a gift. You don't work for a gift. You receive it. And this gift — eternal life, forgiveness, a relationship with the living God — I paid for it myself.

Paul wrote one of the most beautiful sentences in all of Scripture: "While we were still sinners, Christ died for us." Not after you cleaned up your act. Not after you became good enough. While you were still in your mess — that's when I died for you. That's how much I love you.

And when you put your faith in me, something incredible happens. You are justified — made right with God — not because of anything you did, but because of what I did on that cross. It's by faith, not by works.

And then Paul says this — and I want you to hold onto it forever: "There is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus."

No condemnation. That means no guilt hanging over your head. No shame that can stick. No voice that can tell you you're not enough — because in me, you are enough. You are forgiven. You are free.

That's the gospel. It's not complicated. You were lost. I came to find you. You were guilty. I took your punishment. You were far away. I brought you close.

Salvation is a gift. You can't earn it. Just receive it.

Will you?
