THE TEACHINGS OF JESUS — Episode 3
"The Lord's Prayer"
Based on Matthew 6:9-13

My disciples came to me one day and asked something so simple but so important: "Lord, teach us to pray." They'd watched me pray. Early mornings on mountainsides. Late nights alone. They saw something powerful happening and they wanted in.

So I gave them a prayer. But here's what I need you to understand — I didn't give you this prayer so you could memorize it and repeat it like a robot. I gave it so you could learn how to TALK to my Father. It's a framework. A blueprint. Let me walk you through it.

"Our Father in heaven." Stop right there. Did you catch that? FATHER. Not "Dear Distant Ruler of the Universe." Father. Dad. This is personal. When you pray, you're not sending a message into empty space. You're talking to a Father who knows your name, who loves you, who's leaning in to listen.

"Hallowed be your name." That means His name is HOLY. Set apart. Before you ask for a single thing, start by worshipping. Tell God how amazing He is. Praise Him. This shifts your whole mindset from "give me stuff" to "You are incredible."

"Your kingdom come, your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven." This is surrender. You're saying: God, I don't want MY plan — I want YOURS. Whatever You're doing in heaven, do it here. In my school. In my family. In my life. Your will, not mine.

"Give us today our daily bread." Now you can ask! But notice — I said DAILY bread. Not yearly bread. Not "God, set me up for the next ten years." Just today. Trust Him for today. He'll be there again tomorrow.

"Forgive us our debts, as we also have forgiven our debtors." This is huge. You're asking God to forgive you the same way you forgive others. So if you're holding a grudge against someone — that's blocking the pipeline. Forgiveness isn't optional. It's essential.

"And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one." You're asking for protection. You're saying: God, keep me away from traps. Guard my steps. Shield me from the enemy. You don't have to fight alone.

See the pattern? Relationship first. Then worship. Then surrender. Then trust. Then grace. Then protection. THAT is how you pray.

Prayer isn't a performance. It's not about fancy words or how long you go. It's a conversation with your Father — and He's always listening.

Start talking to Him. Right now.
