THE TEACHINGS OF JESUS — Episode 8
"The Lost Sheep"
Based on Luke 15:1-7

Alright, I need to set the scene for you because this one has some drama.

So there I was, eating dinner with tax collectors and sinners — people that most religious folks wanted NOTHING to do with. And the Pharisees and teachers of the law? They were standing off to the side, whispering and grumbling. "This man welcomes sinners and actually EATS with them!" They said it like it was the worst thing in the world.

And that is when I hit them with this story.

"Suppose one of you has a hundred sheep." Now a hundred sheep was a BIG deal — that was a shepherd's whole livelihood, everything he owned. "And one of them wanders off and gets lost. What do you do? Do you shrug and say, 'Well, I still have ninety-nine. That is good enough'?"

NO! You leave the ninety-nine in the open field and you GO after the one that is lost. You search the hills. You look behind every rock and through every valley. You call out in the dark. You do NOT stop until you find it.

And when you FINALLY find that one lost sheep — maybe it is stuck in a thorn bush, scared and bleating, completely helpless — you do not scold it. You do not yell at it. You lift it up, put it on your shoulders, and you CARRY it home.

And then — this is the best part — you call all your friends and neighbors together and you throw a PARTY. "Celebrate with me! I found my lost sheep!"

Then I looked right at those grumbling Pharisees and said: "I tell you, there is MORE joy in heaven over ONE sinner who turns back to God than over ninety-nine people who think they never wandered off."

Here is what I need you to understand. You are not just a number to God. You are not one face in a crowd that He would never miss. If you were the ONLY person on this entire planet, my Father would still come looking for you. I would still come looking for you.

Maybe you feel lost right now. Maybe you have wandered away from God — a little or a lot. Maybe you think you have gone too far for anyone to bother coming after you.

You haven't. I promise you — you haven't.

I am the Good Shepherd. And I will leave the ninety-nine to come find YOU. Not because you are perfect. Because you are MINE. And when I find you, all of heaven throws a party.

You matter THAT much.
