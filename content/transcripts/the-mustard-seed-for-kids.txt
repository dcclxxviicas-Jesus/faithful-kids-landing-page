THE TEACHINGS OF JESUS — Episode 7
"The Mustard Seed"
Based on Matthew 13:31-32, Mark 4:30-32, Matthew 17:20

Let me ask you something. What is the smallest seed you can think of? A sunflower seed? A sesame seed? Now imagine something even tinier. I am talking about a mustard seed — so small you could barely see it sitting on the tip of your finger. You might look at it and think, "That's nothing. What could THAT ever become?"

And that is exactly why I used it to teach one of my most powerful lessons.

I told the crowds: "The Kingdom of Heaven is like a mustard seed. A man takes this tiny little seed and plants it in his garden. It's the smallest of all the seeds he plants. But when it grows — oh, when it GROWS — it becomes the largest plant in the whole garden. So big that birds fly in and build their nests in its branches!"

Think about that. The smallest seed in the garden becomes the BIGGEST plant. Birds are literally making their homes in it. Something that started as almost nothing became a shelter for others.

That is how God's Kingdom works. It doesn't always start with a big, flashy explosion. Sometimes it starts with one person saying one kind word. One kid deciding to stand up for someone at school. One family choosing to help a neighbor. Tiny. Almost invisible. But give it time — and it grows into something MASSIVE.

And here is where it gets even better. I also told my disciples: "If you have faith as small as a mustard seed, you can say to this mountain, 'Move from here to there,' and it WILL move. Nothing will be impossible for you."

Did you catch that? I didn't say you need mountain-sized faith to move a mountain. I said you need mustard-seed-sized faith. TINY faith. Just a little bit of real, genuine trust in God — and mountains start moving.

So maybe you feel like your faith is small right now. Maybe you feel like you are too young, too unsure, too new at this. That is perfectly fine! You don't have to have it all figured out. A mustard seed doesn't look like much on day one either.

But plant it. Water it. Give it sunlight. And watch what God does with it. The smallest beginning can become the biggest blessing — not just for you, but for everyone around you.

Never underestimate what something small can become in the hands of God.
