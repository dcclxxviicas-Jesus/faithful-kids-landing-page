This is one of my favorite stories to tell, because I got to do something sneaky — in the best possible way.

It was that same Sunday — resurrection day. Two of my followers were walking from Jerusalem to a village called Emmaus, about seven miles away. Their names were Cleopas and a companion, and they were CRUSHED. Devastated. Everything they had hoped for — that I was the Messiah, that I would save Israel — seemed to be over. They had watched me die on a cross. They were walking home with broken hearts.

And then I showed up. I walked right up beside them and started walking along. But here is the amazing part — their eyes were kept from recognizing me. They had no idea who I was.

I asked them, "What are you two talking about? You look so sad."

Cleopas stopped and stared at me. He said, "Are you the only visitor to Jerusalem who does not know the things that have happened these last few days?"

I played along. "What things?"

And they told me EVERYTHING. About Jesus of Nazareth — a powerful prophet. About how the chief priests had Him crucified. About how they had hoped He was the one who was going to redeem Israel. And then the part that really got them — some women went to the tomb that morning and found it empty, and angels said He was alive. But they did not know what to make of it.

So I said — and I said this with love — "How foolish you are, and how slow to believe everything the prophets have spoken! Did not the Messiah HAVE to suffer these things and then enter His glory?"

And then I did something incredible. Starting with Moses and going through ALL the Prophets, I walked them through the entire Bible and showed them how EVERYTHING — every story, every prophecy, every promise — pointed to me. The lamb in Abraham's story? Me. The Passover lamb? Me. The suffering servant in Isaiah? Me. All of it. Every page.

When we reached Emmaus, they begged me to stay for dinner. I sat down, took the bread, blessed it, and broke it. And in THAT moment — their eyes were opened. They saw me. They KNEW.

And then — I vanished.

They looked at each other and said, "Did not our hearts BURN within us while He talked with us on the road and opened the Scriptures to us?"

They jumped up and ran all seven miles BACK to Jerusalem to tell the others.

I am hidden all through the Bible. Every story whispers my name. The whole book — from the very first page to the very last — it is all about me.

LESSON: Jesus is woven through every page of the Bible. The whole story — from Genesis to Revelation — points to Him. When you read Scripture, you are reading about Jesus.
