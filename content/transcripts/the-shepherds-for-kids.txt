Welcome back! Okay — I was just born in a manger. The King of the universe, lying in an animal's feeding trough. Now my Father had an announcement to make. The biggest announcement in history. And the question is — who does God tell first?

Not the emperor. Not the religious leaders. Not the wealthy or the famous. God chose shepherds.

Picture these guys. It's the middle of the night. They're out in the fields near Bethlehem, keeping watch over their flocks. Shepherds in those days were as low on the social ladder as you could get. They were dirty. They smelled like sheep. They couldn't even testify in court because people didn't consider them trustworthy. The overlooked. The nobodies.

And God handpicked THEM for the front-row seat.

Suddenly — out of nowhere — an angel of the Lord appeared. And the Bible says the glory of the Lord shone around them. Imagine the darkest night you've ever seen, and then a light brighter than a thousand suns explodes across the sky. These tough, weathered shepherds? They were absolutely terrified.

But the angel said — listen carefully — "Don't be afraid! I bring you good news of great joy that will be for ALL people. Today, in the town of David, a Savior has been born to you. He is the Messiah, the Lord!"

For ALL people. Not just the rich. Not just the religious. Not just one country. ALL people. That means you. Right now. Wherever you are.

And then — as if one angel wasn't enough — the sky ERUPTED. A massive army of angels filled the heavens, praising God and saying, "Glory to God in the highest heaven, and on earth peace to those on whom His favor rests!"

Can you even imagine? Thousands of angels lighting up the night sky, singing with a power that shook the ground. The shepherds must have been shaking, crying, laughing — all at the same time.

And what did they do when the angels left? They didn't sit around debating. They didn't say, "Let's think about this tomorrow." The Bible says they HURRIED to Bethlehem. They ran. And they found exactly what the angel described — a baby wrapped in cloths, lying in a manger.

These working-class nobodies were the first people to meet me. The first to spread the word, telling everyone what they had seen and heard.

Here's what I want burned into your heart today: the good news is for everyone, starting with the overlooked. God didn't send the announcement to a palace — He sent it to a field. If you've ever felt like you don't matter, like you're too ordinary for God to notice — this story is my answer. You're exactly who I came for.

I'll see you in the next one!
