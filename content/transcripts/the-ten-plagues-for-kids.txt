Alright, grab a seat because this episode is EPIC. We're talking about the Ten Plagues of Egypt -- ten times God showed Pharaoh exactly who's in charge. Ready? Let's count them down.

Plague number one: WATER TO BLOOD. Moses stretches his staff over the Nile -- Egypt's lifeline, the river they basically worshipped -- and it turns to blood. Every river, every pond, every bucket of water. The fish die. The whole country stinks. Pharaoh's response? "Meh." His magicians copy the trick, so he walks away. Stubborn.

Plague two: FROGS. And I don't mean a few cute frogs. I mean frogs EVERYWHERE -- in their beds, in their ovens, in their bread dough. Imagine opening your lunchbox and a frog jumps out. Pharaoh begs Moses to stop and promises to let them go. Moses prays, the frogs die, and Pharaoh says, "Just kidding. No."

Plague three: GNATS. Dust turns into swarms of tiny biting gnats covering every person and animal. This time, Pharaoh's magicians CAN'T copy it. They tell Pharaoh, "This is the finger of God!" Does he listen? Nope.

Plague four: FLIES. Massive swarms of flies covering Egypt -- BUT not where the Israelites live. God's drawing a line. Pharaoh negotiates, then breaks his promise again.

Plague five: LIVESTOCK DISEASE. Every Egyptian horse, donkey, camel, cow, and sheep gets sick and dies. The Israelites' animals? Perfectly fine. Pharaoh checks, confirms the Israelites are untouched, and STILL says no.

Plague six: BOILS. Painful, oozing sores break out on every Egyptian and their animals. Even the magicians can't stand before Moses because they're covered in boils. Pharaoh? Hard heart. No deal.

Plague seven: HAIL. The worst hailstorm in Egyptian history -- fire mixed with ice raining from the sky, destroying crops and killing anything left outside. Pharaoh admits he's wrong... then takes it back once the hail stops.

Plague eight: LOCUSTS. Billions of insects devour every green thing left in Egypt. Pharaoh's own officials beg him to give in. He won't.

Plague nine: DARKNESS. Three days of total, suffocating darkness. Not just "nighttime" dark -- a darkness you could FEEL. The Egyptians can't see their own hands. But the Israelites? They have light in their homes.

And then... plague ten. The worst one. The death of every firstborn son in Egypt -- from Pharaoh's palace to the prisoner's dungeon. This is the one that finally breaks Pharaoh.

Now here's what's really going on. Each plague wasn't random. Every single one targeted a specific Egyptian god. The Nile god, the frog goddess, the sun god Ra struck blind by darkness. God was saying, "Your gods are NOTHING. I am the only true God."

And notice the pattern: every time Pharaoh had a chance to do the right thing, he chose stubbornness instead. Each "no" made the next consequence worse.

There's a lesson in that for all of us. When God speaks to your heart, don't be a Pharaoh. Listen the FIRST time. Because His way is always better than what we're holding onto.
