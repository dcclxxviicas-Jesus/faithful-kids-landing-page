TITLE: The Throne Room
SERIES: The Ending & the Beginning
EPISODE: 9
SCRIPTURE: Revelation 4-5

What if I told you there's a room so magnificent, so full of glory, that the moment you stepped inside you'd fall to your knees? Not because anyone told you to. Because you literally couldn't stand.

That's what happened to John.

He was on the island of Patmos when a voice like a trumpet said, "Come up here." And instantly, he was standing in the throne room of heaven.

Let me paint the picture.

A throne — blazing with light. The One sitting on it radiated brilliance like jasper and ruby, like a living gemstone. Around the throne, an emerald rainbow shimmered, circling it like a ring of fire and light.

Twenty-four elders sat on twenty-four thrones, dressed in white, wearing crowns of gold. Lightning flashed. Thunder rolled. Seven torches of fire burned before it.

And before the throne — a sea of glass, clear as crystal.

Then John saw four living creatures. One like a lion. One like an ox. One with the face of a man. One like an eagle in flight. Each had six wings, covered with eyes. And day and night, they never stopped saying:

"Holy, holy, holy is the Lord God Almighty, who was, and is, and is to come!"

Every time they said it, the twenty-four elders fell off their thrones, laid their crowns before God, and cried, "You are worthy, our Lord and God, to receive glory and honor and power!"

Then John saw something else. A scroll in the right hand of the One on the throne — sealed with seven seals. And a mighty angel shouted, "Who is worthy to open the scroll?"

Silence. No one in heaven. No one on earth. No one under the earth. No one was worthy.

John started weeping. Because if no one could open that scroll, God's plan couldn't be fulfilled.

Then an elder said, "Stop crying. Look — the Lion of the tribe of Judah has conquered! He can open the scroll."

John looked up, expecting a mighty lion. But instead, he saw a Lamb — standing in the center of the throne — looking as if it had been slain.

That Lamb is me.

I stepped forward and took the scroll. And when I did, every creature — every elder, every angel, every living thing in heaven and on earth and under the earth and in the sea — fell down and sang:

"Worthy is the Lamb who was slain, to receive power and wealth and wisdom and strength and honor and glory and praise!"

Thousands upon thousands. Ten thousand times ten thousand. The sound shook the universe.

I conquered not with a sword — but by laying down my life. The most powerful being in the universe is a sacrificial Lamb. That's what strength really looks like in my kingdom.
