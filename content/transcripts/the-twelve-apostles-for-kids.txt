Before I tell you about the biggest team I ever built, let me tell you what happened the night before.

I went up on a mountain — alone — and I prayed. Not for an hour. Not for a few hours. I prayed ALL. NIGHT. LONG. From sunset to sunrise, I talked with my Father. Because what I was about to do the next morning would shape everything that came after. I was choosing the twelve people who would carry my message to the entire world.

When the sun came up, I called all my followers together. There were a LOT of people following me by now. But from that crowd, I chose twelve. Just twelve.

Simon — I gave him the name Peter. Loud, impulsive, passionate — he would one day become the rock my church was built on. His brother Andrew, one of the very first to follow me. James and John — I called them the "Sons of Thunder" because they were THAT intense. Philip, who always had questions. Bartholomew, who almost didn't come because he didn't think anything good could come from Nazareth.

Matthew — yes, the tax collector everyone hated. He was on my team now. Thomas, who would become famous for doubting — but when he finally believed, he believed with EVERYTHING he had. James the son of Alphaeus. Simon the Zealot — and this is important. A zealot wanted to violently overthrow Rome. So picture this: I put a zealot and a tax collector on the SAME TEAM. A man who worked for Rome and a man who wanted to destroy Rome — brothers. Only I would do that.

Then Judas the son of James. And finally... Judas Iscariot. The one who would betray me. And yes — I knew. I chose him anyway.

Fishermen. A tax collector. A political radical. Doubters. Hot-heads. Quiet ones. Bold ones. People who would NEVER have been in the same room together. Not one had a theology degree. Not one was wealthy or powerful or famous.

I chose twelve ordinary people. Not because they were perfect — they were FAR from it. I chose them because they were willing. Willing to leave their comfort zones. Willing to learn. Willing to follow even when they didn't understand where we were going.

That's how God builds His team — from every walk of life. Your background, your personality, your past mistakes — none of that disqualifies you. Diversity isn't an accident in my kingdom. It's the design. I don't need you to be perfect. I need you to be willing.
