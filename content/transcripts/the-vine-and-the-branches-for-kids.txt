TITLE: The Vine and the Branches
SERIES: The Ending & the Beginning
EPISODE: 6
SCRIPTURE: John 15:1-17

Picture a vineyard. Rows and rows of grapevines stretching across a hillside, soaking in the sun. The branches are heavy with clusters of ripe, purple grapes. It's beautiful. And this is exactly the picture I painted for my disciples on one of the most important nights of my life.

It was the night before the cross. I knew what was coming. And I needed them to understand something they would carry for the rest of their lives.

I told them, "I am the vine. My Father is the gardener. And you — you are the branches."

Now, here's what you need to know about branches. A branch that's connected to the vine is alive. It drinks water. It soaks up nutrients. It grows leaves and produces fruit — big, juicy, beautiful fruit. But a branch that gets cut off from the vine? It shrivels up. It dries out. It produces nothing. It's dead.

I looked at my disciples and said, "If you remain in me, and I remain in you, you will bear much fruit. But apart from me — you can do nothing."

Nothing. Not "a little less." Not "you'll struggle but manage." Nothing.

Think about it like this. Your phone can do amazing things — but if it's not plugged in, eventually the battery dies. It doesn't matter how fancy it is. Without the power source, it's just a dark screen. You are designed to be connected to me. I'm your power source. Your joy, your love, your patience, your courage — it all flows from me into you, like life flowing from a vine into a branch.

But here's the part that surprises people. My Father — the gardener — doesn't just leave the branches alone. The ones that don't bear fruit? He cuts them off. But the ones that DO bear fruit? He prunes them. He trims them back. Not to hurt them — but so they can grow even more.

Pruning doesn't feel good. It might be a hard season. A loss. A struggle. But it's not punishment. It's preparation. God is shaping you to produce more fruit than you ever thought possible.

And what's the fruit? Love. Joy. Peace. Patience. Kindness. The kind of life that makes people stop and ask, "What's different about you?"

Before I left, I told them one more thing. "You did not choose me. I chose you. And I appointed you to go and bear fruit — fruit that will last."

You didn't just stumble into a relationship with me. I chose you. On purpose. And I planted you so that your life would produce something that outlasts everything else on this earth.

Stay connected. Remain in me. That's the whole secret.
