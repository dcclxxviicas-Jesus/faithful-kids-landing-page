Alright, are you ready for one of the most EPIC moments in the entire Bible? Because this one is absolutely wild.

Israel has crossed the Jordan River — and yes, God parted the waters AGAIN, just like He did at the Red Sea. The people walked across on dry ground. They're finally IN the Promised Land. But there's a problem. A big, massive, thick-walled problem.

Jericho.

This city was a fortress. We're talking enormous stone walls — some experts think they were several stories high and thick enough to build houses on top of. Soldiers everywhere. Gates locked tight. The people inside had heard what God did to Egypt, and they were terrified... but they were also heavily armed and NOT coming out.

So what's the battle plan? Battering rams? Siege towers? A massive army charge?

Nope. God's plan was... march around the city. Yep. Just walk around it. Once a day. For six days. In complete silence.

I can only imagine what the people of Jericho were thinking. "Are they... are they just walking? What is HAPPENING right now?"

And Israel's soldiers might have been thinking the same thing! "This is the plan?! We're just going to WALK?!"

But they obeyed. Day one — they marched. Day two — they marched. Days three, four, five, six — march, march, march, march. Silently. No war cries. No attacks. Just footsteps and faith.

Then came day seven. This time, they marched around the city not once — but SEVEN times. The priests blew their trumpets. And then Joshua shouted, "SHOUT! For the Lord has given you the city!"

And the people let out the biggest shout you can imagine. And those massive, impossible, fortress walls? They came CRASHING down. Flat to the ground. Just like that.

Israel charged in and took the city. And guess who was saved? Rahab and her entire family — because that scarlet cord was hanging from her window, just like the spies had promised. Faith kept. Promise honored.

Here's what blows my mind about this story: God's plan made ZERO sense from a human perspective. Walking in circles doesn't knock down walls. But obedience does. When you trust God enough to follow His plan — even when it looks ridiculous to everyone else — that's when miracles happen.

My plans won't always make sense to you. But I promise they always work. Will you trust Me enough to take the next step?
