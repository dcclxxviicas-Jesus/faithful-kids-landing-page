THE WIDOW'S OIL
Series: Elijah & Elisha | Episode 2
Scripture: 1 Kings 17:8-24
Narrated by Jesus

Last time, Elijah was camped by a brook, eating room service delivered by ravens. But then the brook dried up. No water. No plan. Just a prophet and an empty stream. So what did God do next? He sent Elijah somewhere even MORE unlikely.

God said: "Go to a town called Zarephath. I've commanded a WIDOW there to feed you." Now hold on — a widow in those days was one of the poorest people in society. No husband meant no income. God was sending Elijah from one impossible situation to another. That's just how My Father rolls.

So Elijah arrives at Zarephath and finds this woman gathering sticks. He calls out, "Hey, could you bring me some water? And while you're at it — a piece of bread?"

And she stops. Looks at him. And with pain in her voice she says: "I swear to you, I have nothing baked. Just a HANDFUL of flour in a jar and a LITTLE bit of oil in a jug. I'm gathering these sticks to make one last meal for me and my son. After that — we're going to die."

ONE. LAST. MEAL. She's looking at the end of the road.

And what does Elijah say? "Don't be afraid. Go home and make the food — but make ME a small cake FIRST. Then make something for yourself and your son. Because the Lord says: the jar of flour will NOT run out and the jug of oil will NOT run dry until the day rain returns to the land."

Make ME something first? When you're down to your last handful? That sounds crazy! But here's the secret — God was asking her to give what she thought she couldn't afford to lose. And she did it. She TRUSTED and she OBEYED.

And guess what happened? That flour jar? Never emptied. That oil jug? Never ran dry. Day after day after day — there was always enough. God MULTIPLIED what she gave.

But the story doesn't end there. Later, her son got sick and DIED. She was devastated. Elijah took the boy, laid him on a bed, and cried out to God THREE times: "Lord, let this boy's life return!" And My Father heard that prayer. The boy came back to LIFE. The woman said, "Now I KNOW you are truly a man of God!"

Here's your lesson: sometimes God asks you to give the very thing you're most afraid to lose. Your time. Your comfort. Your last bit of courage. Give it anyway. Because what My Father does with your small offering will blow your mind.
