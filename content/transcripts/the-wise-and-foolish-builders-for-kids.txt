THE TEACHINGS OF JESUS — Episode 9
"The Wise and Foolish Builders"
Based on Matthew 7:24-27

Okay, I want you to imagine two people. Both of them decide to build a house. They both pick a spot, they both draw up plans, and they both get to work. From the outside, the two houses might even look the same. But there is one MASSIVE difference that nobody can see — and it changes everything.

The first builder is wise. Before he lays a single brick, he digs deep — all the way down to solid rock. It takes longer. It is harder work. His friends probably walked by and said, "Why are you digging so much? Just start building already!" But he kept digging until he hit the foundation that would NOT move.

The second builder is foolish. He looks around, finds a nice flat spot of sand, and thinks, "Perfect! This looks great. Why waste time digging?" So he starts building right away — faster, easier, and it looks just as good on the surface.

Then the storm comes.

Rain pours down like someone turned a firehose on from the sky. Rivers rise and flood the ground. Wind SLAMS into both houses with everything it has got.

The wise man's house? It takes every hit. The rain pounds it, the water pushes against it, the wind howls all around it — and it does not move. Not one inch. Because it is built on ROCK.

The foolish man's house? The same storm hits it and — CRASH — it comes down in a spectacular, total disaster. Sand washes out from underneath. The walls buckle. The whole thing collapses. Gone.

Now here is what I was really saying. The wise builder is anyone who hears my words AND actually does something with them. They put my teachings into practice. They build their life on a foundation that cannot be shaken.

The foolish builder hears the exact same words — but does nothing. They think "that's nice" and keep living however they want. No foundation. No roots. No anchor.

Here is the thing that most people miss: BOTH builders heard the same message. And BOTH faced the same storm. The difference was not what they heard or what they faced — it was what they DID.

Storms are coming for everyone. That is just life. You will face hard days, tough situations, and moments that shake you. The question is not IF the storm will come — it is what you have built your life on when it does.

Hear my words. AND do them. Build on the rock. And when the storm hits, you will still be standing.
