Hey, welcome back! Last time we talked about the shepherds who came running to see me on the night I was born. But they weren't the only ones looking for me. Far, far away — in the East — something incredible was happening.

A group of wise men — the Magi — were studying the night sky when a brand-new star appeared. Not just any star. This one was blazing, brilliant, and it meant something. These men had spent their whole lives studying the heavens, and they knew — this star was announcing the birth of a King.

So they packed up their camels, loaded their most precious treasures, and set out on a journey across deserts and mountains. They didn't have GPS. They didn't have a map. They had a star. And they followed it.

When they arrived in Jerusalem, they went straight to the palace — because where else would you look for a king, right? They asked, "Where is the one who has been born King of the Jews? We saw His star in the East and have come to worship Him."

Now here's where it gets tense. King Herod heard that someone had been born "King of the Jews" — and he was NOT happy. He was scared. Furious. He called in the religious experts, who told him the Messiah would be born in Bethlehem. Then Herod did something sneaky. He told the wise men, "Go find this child, and come back and tell me where He is — so I can worship Him too."

He was lying. He didn't want to worship me. He wanted to destroy me.

But the wise men followed the star, and it led them right to me. When they saw me — they fell to their knees. Powerful, wealthy, brilliant men dropped to the ground and worshiped a toddler. Because they recognized who I really was.

Then they opened their treasures. Gold — a gift for a King. Frankincense — an offering for God. And myrrh — a spice used for burial, pointing to the day I would give my life for the world. Every gift told part of my story.

And here's the twist — God warned them in a dream: "Don't go back to Herod." So they went home a completely different way.

Here's what I love about the wise men: they searched for me with everything they had. They crossed the world because they HAD to find me. And when they did? They worshiped.

Here's the truth — when you truly seek God with all your heart, you WILL find Him. Every single time. Your journey might not be across a desert, but if you search for me — I promise, I'm right here.
