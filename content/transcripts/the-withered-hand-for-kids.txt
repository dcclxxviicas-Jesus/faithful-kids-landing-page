Hey kids! Today's story is shorter than some of the others, but don't let that fool you — it's one of the most IMPORTANT miracles I ever performed. Because this one wasn't just about healing. It was about exposing what's wrong with religion that has no heart.

It was the Sabbath — the day of rest. I walked into the synagogue, and sitting right there was a man with a shriveled, withered hand. It was curled up, useless, probably had been that way for years. He couldn't work. He couldn't carry things. Simple tasks most people take for granted were impossible for him.

But here's the part that made my blood boil.

The Pharisees were there. And they weren't watching because they cared about this man. They were watching ME. They were studying me like hawks, waiting to see if I would heal on the Sabbath — so they could ACCUSE me of breaking their rules.

Let me say that again. A man was suffering RIGHT IN FRONT of them, and their only thought was, "Can we catch Jesus doing something wrong?"

So I called the man forward. Right to the front. In front of everyone. I wasn't going to do this quietly. I WANTED them to see.

Then I turned to the Pharisees and asked a question that should have been easy: "Which is lawful on the Sabbath — to do good, or to do evil? To SAVE life, or to KILL?"

Silence. Not one of them opened their mouth. They had no answer because they knew the truth would destroy their argument.

And in that moment, I looked at them with anger. Yes — I was ANGRY. But not the kind of anger that loses control. It was anger mixed with deep grief, because their hearts were so HARD. They cared more about their rules than about a man in pain standing three feet away from them.

I turned to the man and said, "Stretch out your hand."

He stretched it out — and it was COMPLETELY restored. Strong. Flexible. Every finger working. Just like the other one. Healed in an instant, right there in front of everyone.

And the Pharisees? Did they celebrate? Did they praise God? Did they fall on their knees?

No. They walked out and immediately started plotting how to DESTROY me.

They saw a miracle and chose murder. That's what happens when religion replaces relationship. When rules become more important than people. When looking righteous matters more than BEING compassionate.

Here's my message for you: I will NEVER choose a rule over a person. People matter more than traditions. Compassion matters more than being right. If your faith doesn't make you more loving, something is broken.

Love people. That's the whole point.
