You might think my first miracle was something dramatic — healing a blind man, calming a storm, raising someone from the dead. Nope. My first miracle was at a PARTY. And I did it to save someone from embarrassment. Let me explain.

There was a wedding in a little town called Cana. In my culture, weddings weren't a one-day thing — they were CELEBRATIONS that lasted for days. The whole community showed up. Food everywhere. Music. Dancing. And wine — lots of it. Running out of wine wasn't just inconvenient. It was HUMILIATING. It meant the family couldn't provide for their guests. It was a social disaster.

My mother Mary was there, and so was I with my disciples. Right in the middle of the celebration, my mom came to me and said, "They have no more wine." Now, she didn't exactly ASK me to do anything. She just... told me. Moms, right? I said to her, "Woman, why do you involve me? My hour has not yet come." But my mother — she knew me. She turned to the servants and said six powerful words: "Do whatever He tells you."

Do whatever He tells you. Remember that.

There were six large stone water jars nearby — the kind used for ceremonial washing. Each one could hold twenty to thirty gallons. I told the servants, "Fill the jars with water." They filled them to the BRIM. Then I said, "Now draw some out and take it to the master of the banquet." They did it. No questions. No hesitation.

When the master of the banquet tasted it, his eyes went wide. He pulled the bridegroom aside and said, "Everyone brings out the best wine first, and then the cheaper wine after people have had too much to drink. But YOU — you've saved the BEST for now!"

He had no idea what just happened. But the servants knew. My disciples knew. And they believed in me.

Here's what I want you to take from this: I care about your EVERYDAY life. Not just the "big spiritual moments." I care about your embarrassments, your awkward situations, your parties, your ordinary Tuesday afternoons. Nothing in your life is too small for me. And when you trust me — even when it doesn't make sense, even when it's just filling jars with water — I can turn the ordinary into something EXTRAORDINARY.

My mom had it right: do whatever He tells you. Even when you don't understand the plan.
